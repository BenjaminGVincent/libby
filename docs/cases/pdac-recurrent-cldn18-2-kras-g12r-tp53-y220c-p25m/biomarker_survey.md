_20 biomarkers surveyed: 16 with no usable result on file (0 essential), 2 measured but not to decision resolution, 2 already established. Panel coverage: 9 tumor-agnostic biomarkers plus 11 of 72 protein targets in scope for this tumor._

Twenty biomarkers were surveyed: sixteen never measured, and the two measured short of decision grade are the essential ones. Germline status is effectively unknown. The "none detected" on file is incidental tumor-NGS reporting, not the germline panel NCCN has recommended for every PDAC patient since 2019, and onset in the 50s sharpens it. BRCA1/2, PALB2, and ATM gate olaparib maintenance and platinum re-challenge. One blood draw, no tissue, an approved drug class behind a positive: the survey's highest-value order. The other essential gap, CLDN18.2, is a threshold call, not a yes-or-no. The tumor stained 2+/3+ in 60% of cells: below the 75% cutoff of zolbetuximab's registrational trials, clear of satri-cel's 2+ in 40%, ASP2138's 1+, and the radioligand protocol's 2+ in 20%, which 60% clears threefold. Which threshold applies decides which route is open; the clone went unnamed and the stain is archival.

Archival is the deeper problem: everything on file describes the December 2025 resection, not the July 2026 recurrence, with ten cycles of FOLFIRINOX in between. Two orders close the gaps: the blood draw (HLA class I typing rides along) and a recurrence biopsy, rank 1 for target validator and PI alike; one pass yields DNA and RNA sequencing and a six-stain block cut, CLDN18.2 re-stain included. Primary-to-recurrence concordance is 83.3%, but losses cluster in local recurrence and liver metastasis, this patient's setting, and biopsy sensitivity is 54.6%, so a negative stain on a small core is weak evidence of true loss.

The two finished results are answers, not gaps: microsatellite stability at TMB 0.83 mut/Mb closes single-agent PD-1 blockade, and the ranking says so. KRAS G12R is confirmed, but no G12C- or G12D-selective agent covers it. KMT2C S2816fs stays at the user's request despite no clinical route, and everything else on the panel is absent on purpose. Most of what remains will test negative. The case for testing is the size of the option a positive opens, not an implied likelihood of one.
