_12 standard options assessed: 6 to consider now, 2 behind an open gate, 1 already received._

Twelve standard options were assessed for this recurrence. Six are open to consider now, two sit behind workup gates, two belong to a later decision point, one has already been given, and one was set aside. The honest headline is that the standard of care here is thin. NCCN's own pathway marks a clinical trial as preferred for a recurrence inside 6 months of adjuvant completion, and this one came at about 4 months. The default chemotherapy is a class switch to a gemcitabine-based regimen, in US practice usually gemcitabine with nab-paclitaxel, and the right benchmark for it is the post-FOLFIRINOX AGEO cohort: response rate 17.5%, median survival 8.8 months (95% CI 6.2-9.7). ESMO's April 2025 Express Update grades gemcitabine plus conventional paclitaxel I, B in this position and gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel III, C; no grading changes how modest all three are. NCCN was verified at v2.2026; v3.2026 has posted and could not be read.

Already spent: 10 cycles of FOLFIRINOX across the neoadjuvant and adjuvant settings, which is why neither FOLFIRINOX re-challenge nor NALIRIFOX, an approved first-line regimen built from the same three drug classes, appears as a live option.

Gated: radiotherapy waits on the staging call that would make this an isolated local recurrence; olaparib waits on the germline panel that has never been drawn; and every taxane-containing row waits on a CTCAE grade for the residual oxaliplatin neuropathy.

This report adds the standard options and does not narrow the experimental options ranked elsewhere on this page. The workup at the top of both tables is the same step, and the one hard conflict between the tracks is timing: starting chemotherapy spends the six-week RASolute 303 consent window.

## Assessed and set aside

??? note "1 option(s) screened against this patient and set aside"
    Screened against this patient and found not to apply. Listed for the audit trail.

    - **NALIRIFOX (liposomal irinotecan + oxaliplatin + 5-FU/leucovorin)**: On paper the 2024 label covers first-line metastatic disease, and a patient entering her first metastatic line could be read into it. In practice this regimen re-runs fluorouracil, oxaliplatin, and irinotecan within months of the tumor progressing through 10 cycles of the same three classes, and the NCCN recurrence pathway routes a recurrence inside 6 months of adjuvant completion away from exactly that. Set aside for re-exposure and neuropathy reasons, not for lack of an approval.

## How these sit alongside the targeted options

_The ranked recommendations stay scoped to this case's targetable features, and nothing on this page changes that ranking. What this section adds is the sequencing: where a standard option and a targeted option compete for the same line, and where taking one would close the door on the other._

**Competes for the same line of therapy**

- **Chemoradiation or SBRT to an isolated operative-bed recurrence**: The staging call that makes this option live, locally recurrent disease without metastases, is the same call that closes the daraxonrasib expanded-access protocol, RASolute 303, and the ASP2138 monotherapy arms, while ASP5834 accepts locally advanced unresectable disease. Recent radiotherapy is itself an exclusion window in several protocols, so the timing question runs in both directions. (interacts with: daraxonrasib, asp2138, asp5834)
- **Olaparib maintenance (germline BRCA-gated)**: Same strategy as rank 5 of the experimental ranking; it sits in both tables because it is FDA-approved and biomarker-gated. The blood draw that closes its gate is part of the workup bundle at the top of both tables. (interacts with: olaparib-maintenance)
- **Gemcitabine + paclitaxel (GEMPAX regimen)**: Occupies the same second-line slot as the ranked experimental options, and like any chemotherapy start it spends the RASolute 303 six-week consent window. (interacts with: daraxonrasib)
- **Gemcitabine monotherapy**: Same second-line slot as the ranked options; starting it spends the RASolute 303 consent window like any other chemotherapy start. (interacts with: daraxonrasib)

**May foreclose a targeted option**

- **Gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel**: RASolute 303 requires consent within 6 weeks of the metastatic diagnosis, so starting this regimen first spends that window, and it changes the line count several CLDN18.2 protocols screen on. In the other direction, its AGEO benchmark (median OS 8.8 months) is the floor the experimental rows are measured against, and it remains available after a trial. (interacts with: daraxonrasib, ibi343, asp2138)

**May be foreclosed by a targeted option**

- **Liposomal irinotecan + 5-FU/leucovorin (NAPOLI-1 regimen)**: IBI343 carries an exatecan payload, another topoisomerase-1 poison. In either order, the second topoisomerase-1 exposure faces possible cross-resistance, and no published data settle that sequence. (interacts with: ibi343)

**Comes before the targeted options**

- **Biopsy confirmation of the recurrence, restaging, tumor NGS, and dedicated germline testing**: This is the same step the experimental ranking carries at rank 1. The staging call opens or closes the daraxonrasib expanded-access protocol, RASolute 303, and the ASP2138 monotherapy arms; the germline draw closes the olaparib gate on both tables; and the neuropathy grade decides taxane exposure on both tracks. (interacts with: recurrence-biopsy-workup, daraxonrasib, asp2138, olaparib-maintenance)

**Can run alongside the targeted options**

- **Early palliative care and a goals-of-care discussion**: Explicitly concurrent: choosing aggressive treatment, a trial, or neither does not change the recommendation. Documenting goals of care would also firm up the efficacy/toxicity weighting the rest of the case currently carries as an assumed default.
- **Pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy and nutritional support**: Runs alongside any systemic, radiotherapy, or trial route and conflicts with none of them. Nutritional state feeds the screening labs and performance-status criteria every protocol on the experimental table applies.
