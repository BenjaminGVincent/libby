## Research question

In newly diagnosed metastatic high-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma, systemic-therapy naive with bilateral pulmonary metastases and doxorubicin + trabectedin (LMS-04) planned and imminent, what interventions could target the histology, the entirely unmeasured molecular landscape (HRR/HRD, RB1, B7-H3, FAP, NTRK, TMB/MSI) and the pulmonary disease pattern, gated on the pre-induction workup and on HRR/HRD confirmation for the PARP branch?

## Patient profile (scrubbed)

- Female, age band 30-39.
- High-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma, spindle-cell morphology, confirmed on both the pulmonary biopsy and the re-reviewed uterine specimen by a reference pathology laboratory (August 2026).
- Stage IV: bilateral pulmonary metastases, read by the consulting sarcoma oncologist as unusually infiltrative and possibly inflammatory. Lifetime never-smoker.
- The 6.0 cm myometrial primary was resected September 2024 during hysterectomy for a presumed benign leiomyoma and signed out benign; the diagnosis was corrected in July 2026 and confirmed in August 2026, an interval of roughly 23 months. Whether the specimen was morcellated is unknown; the operative report has not been retrieved.
- Systemic-therapy naive. Planned first line: doxorubicin + trabectedin with trabectedin maintenance (the LMS-04 regimen), not yet started.
- ER <1% and PR <1% by IHC, recorded as a decision-relevant negative. The specimen provenance of that stain (2024 primary vs 2026 lung core) is unverified.
- Everything else is untested: no TP53, RB1, ATRX, MED12, HRR/BRCA2, NTRK, TMB, MSI/MMR or PD-L1 result exists. No laboratory values of any kind were supplied at intake. No LVEF, no PFTs, no CBC.
- ECOG 1 is an intake ASSUMPTION, never documented by a clinician. She is symptomatic with cough and dyspnea yet travelled for specialist consultation, which fits either ECOG 1 or 2.
- Resides at roughly 8,500 ft (~2,600 m). Chronic altitude adaptation shifts her baseline haemoglobin, oximetry and dyspnea thresholds, which bears on every anaemia, pneumonitis and pulmonary-reserve call to come.

## Target validation paths

Two test sets carry the weight of this case. An echocardiogram with LVEF and a clinician-documented ECOG decide whether the planned doxorubicin + trabectedin induction (the LMS-04 regimen, NCT02997358) can be given at all, and a tumor HRR/HRD panel decides whether olaparib + temozolomide (NCI protocol 10250, NCT03880019) exists as an option at progression. Neither result is on file. If the cardiac or pulmonary screen fails, the first-line fitness conversation changes and belongs to the treating team, not to Libby; if the HRR panel returns proficient, the olaparib + temozolomide branch closes for practical purposes. Nearly everything else below runs in parallel with induction and costs no treatment days.

### uLMS histology

Three items are essential before cycle 1. A transthoracic echocardiogram with LVEF and strain (same day to 1 week), because the anthracycline cannot be dosed without a reference value. A clinician-documented ECOG, because the 1 on file is an intake assumption and her symptoms fit either 1 or 2, a difference that decides full-dose induction and most trial screens. And a hepatic-renal panel with creatine kinase, which sets the trabectedin starting dose and its dose-modification rules. The CBC with reticulocytes and ferritin needs an altitude-adjusted reference interval stated in the chart: at roughly 8,500 ft her baseline haemoglobin runs high, and a sea-level-normed count will under-read chemotherapy anaemia. Expert sarcoma pathology review of both specimens, with mitotic index, coagulative necrosis and FNCLCC grade, is the essential item that gates no drug: a 6.0 cm mass handled as a benign fibroid was very unlikely to be sampled at the one-block-per-centimetre standard, major revisions occur in about a quarter of sarcoma second opinions, and the deeper levels should be cut before the molecular orders exhaust the block.

### Bilateral pulmonary pattern

PFTs with DLCO and oximetry recorded at residence altitude come before induction. Without them, an on-treatment desaturation cannot be split into progression, pneumonitis or altitude physiology, and the same numbers later price any lung-directed surgery or radiation. PD-L1 with CD3 and CD8 on the 2026 lung core answers whether the infiltrative, possibly inflammatory radiologic read reflects an immune infiltrate; it gates nothing on its own, and if the core runs scant, the MMR and B7-H3 stains take priority.

### Unmeasured molecular landscape

One RNA-inclusive tumor NGS panel on the 2024 resection block does most of the work here. The HRR gene set with an LOH score gates olaparib + temozolomide via NCT03880019 (2-3 weeks, archival FFPE). RNA fusion calling must be specified on the requisition: NTRK breakpoints sit in large introns, a DNA-only panel returns a false negative that reads as a closed question, and a positive gates larotrectinib, entrectinib or repotrectinib under the tumor-agnostic approval. TMB from a validated comprehensive panel and the four-stain MMR IHC close the pembrolizumab doors (TMB >=10 mut/Mb, or dMMR); both are rare in this histology and cheap to settle. B7-H3 IHC carries the best odds of a positive of any surface target (97% of soft-tissue sarcomas stained positive, 69% at high level) and gates screening for the B7-H3 ADC programs. The germline panel (BRCA1/2, PALB2, RAD51C/D, ATM, TP53 and the Lynch genes) goes out with the somatic order, after the consent conversation it actually is; ERN GENTURIS puts germline TP53 testing before genotoxic chemotherapy. HLA class I typing at allele-level resolution is one blood draw, with PRAME and MAGE-A4 IHC held as reflex stains until a restricting allele returns; that prices the TCR-T and ImmTAC class (IMA203, brenetafusp, the SURPASS programme) without spending tissue speculatively. TP53, RB1, ATRX and MED12 ride along free on the panel, and paired ATRX IHC on both blocks answers whether the 2024 primary represents the 2026 disease for the price of two stains. One assay cannot be ordered at all: the RAD51 foci test, the functional readout that separated 11.2 from 5.4 months of PFS in the olaparib + temozolomide trial, has no CLIA provider anywhere. Do not hold the somatic panel for it; ask any PARP-inhibitor trial site whether they run it at screening.

### ER/PR <1%

The <1% ER and PR result genuinely forecloses aromatase inhibition, since durable benefit with letrozole occurred only in tumours staining above 90%, but only if the stain was run on current disease with working controls. So the first step is a records one: which specimen was stained, which clone, and whether the internal control worked. A failed stain on the 2024 block reads identically to a true negative. If the result came off the 2024 primary, repeat ER and PR on the 2026 lung core with percentage and intensity reported; if it was already run on the lung core, skip the restain and leave the door closed.

### The 2024-2026 interval

Two records requests, one letter to the 2024 institution. The September 2024 operative report and gross description settle whether the specimen was morcellated; morcellation of an unrecognised leiomyosarcoma disperses tumour through the peritoneum, so a yes means pelvic and peritoneal disease must be actively excluded rather than assumed absent. And the pre-treatment CT with named RECIST target lesions has to happen before cycle 1, because the clean baseline window closes the day induction starts. Any interval imaging on disc bounds the growth rate of the lung disease, the closest available measure of tempo in a therapy-naive patient.

### Biomarkers measured, but not to decision resolution

This case has almost nothing in the category, and that is itself the finding. The biomarker survey handed eight questions across (HRR/HRD, NTRK fusion, MSI/MMR, TMB, HLA class I, B7-H3, PRAME and MAGE-A4), and every one arrived never measured or not assessable rather than measured at insufficient resolution; each has its closing assay in the sections above. The one result on file whose resolution is in question is the ER/PR <1% itself: the assay is decision-grade, the specimen provenance is not, and the records step above is what closes that gap.

## Rank 2. Doxorubicin + trabectedin, then trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04), first line

*The planned regimen and the best-evidenced option are the same regimen: the largest replicated effect in the dossier, bought with the heaviest first-line toxicity profile and an off-label authorization.*

### Evidence base

LMS-04 is the only RCT-grade, indication-matched result in this dossier and it survived the critic's scrutiny: randomized phase 3, n=150, first-line metastatic or unresectable leiomyosarcoma, median PFS 12.2 vs 6.2 months with doxorubicin alone (adjusted HR 0.41, 95% CI 0.29-0.58, p<0.0001) on a pre-specified primary read by blinded independent central review ([PMID 35835135](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35835135)). The 55-month final analysis held on survival: median OS 33 vs 24 months, adjusted HR 0.65 (0.44-0.95) ([PMID 39231341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39231341)). RoB2 Low. The limitations are equally plain: uterine primaries were 67 of 150, a stratified subgroup with no separately powered effect estimate; 107 deaths among 150 patients puts the OS upper bound at 0.95, so the survival claim is real but not robust to one contradicting trial; and no replication exists yet, with SaLuDo ([NCT06088290](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06088290)) the nearest confirmatory read and unreported.

### Likelihood of desired effect

High, for a patient who clears the gates. This is her exact population (first line, LMS, chemo-naive), the effect held at both endpoints, and the EORTC pooled prognostic model reads her age, line and long interval since initial diagnosis as favourable (p=0.0004 for the time-lapse covariate, [PMID 10458228](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10458228)). The critic's qualifier on that last point is adopted: the covariate was measured in patients whose sarcoma had been diagnosed and followed, whereas her 23-month interval encodes a pathology error, so it stands as reassurance about tempo and not as a number to quote her. How much the maintenance phase contributes to the 12.2 months is unmeasured, because continuation was never randomised against stopping.

### Toxicity profile

- Combination arm: grade 3-4 neutropenia 80% (vs 13% doxorubicin alone), thrombocytopenia 47% (vs 0%), febrile neutropenia 28% (vs 9%). Primary G-CSF prophylaxis belongs in the cycle 1 orders, not discovered at cycle 2.
- One treatment-related death in the trial, in the doxorubicin-alone arm.
- Maintenance-phase safety is the published gap: [NCT02997358](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02997358) has no posted results and the 2024 report gives no per-term rates, so monitoring defaults to the single-agent trabectedin dossier: G>=3 ALT elevation 25%, rhabdomyolysis 1.2%, treatment-related deaths 2.1% ([PMID 26371143](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26371143)); CK and LFTs stay on the recurring schedule through maintenance, with the uLMS subset mirroring the parent trial ([PMID 28651804](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28651804)).
- At 39, cumulative anthracycline exposure is a lifetime ledger; the dexrazoxane and dose-cap conversation belongs before cycle 1.
- Altitude effects cut across all of it: a polycythemic baseline masks chemotherapy anaemia against sea-level ranges ([PMID 31257609](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31257609)), and her presenting dyspnea cannot be split between disease, pneumonitis and altitude without the measured local baseline ordered at rank 1.
- No stated toxicity vetoes exist to check against. The advocate's consent gap stands: the 80%/28% trade has never been put to her.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

No dissent and no veto; this was the board's one unanimous therapeutic call across all five chairs, and the qualified round-2 rows were boundary-setting rather than objection. The conservative's withdrawn veto and its re-arm clause are documented at rank 1. The nearest thing to a mechanism concern is the maintenance design itself: the preclinical basis for the pairing was additive at best (combination indices just under 1, [PMID 12783202](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12783202)), no preclinical support for the maintenance phase exists at all, and an untested continuation can accrue hepatotoxicity without measurable contribution. That is an evidence gap inside an endorsed regimen, and it is priced into the monitoring plan rather than the rank.

### Practical considerations

First-line use is off-label: the trabectedin label requires a prior anthracycline and she is naive, so the prior authorization is filed as off-label from day one, citing [PMID 35835135](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35835135) and [PMID 39231341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39231341), because a payer denial is the one thing that could actually delay induction. The guideline picture is adoption lag and reimbursement, not opposition, and it should be stated precisely: BSG 2025 keeps single-agent doxorubicin at grade I,A and records the combination as "not yet supported by NICE" ([PMID 38734790](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38734790)); ESMO's operative STS guideline is the 2021 edition and predates the trial ([PMID 34303806](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34303806)); NCCN carriage is confirmed only through secondary sources behind a login wall ([PMID 38994959](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38994959)), so this page quotes no NCCN category. No society text argues against the regimen. If the echocardiogram or pulmonary reserve fails, the fallback conversation (gemcitabine + docetaxel) lives on the Standard-of-care table.

### Why this rank

Rank 2 only because the workup gates it; among therapies it is first without argument. Agreement 1.0, the largest effect size in the dossier, and the board's rare case where the aggressive option and the best-evidenced option coincide. The gap to rank 3 is categorical rather than incremental: rank 3 is conditional on a biomarker nobody has measured and on this regimen having already been used.

## Rank 3. Olaparib + temozolomide, queued for first progression

*Conditional on `hrd:positive`. Foreclosed for practical purposes if HRR/HRD testing returns proficient: expected benefit drops toward the 5.4-month arm and the off-label case collapses with it.*

### Evidence base

NCI protocol 10250 pre-specified a one-stage binomial bar of five or more responses in 22 patients and hit it: ORR 27% overall, best ORR within 6 months 23%, median PFS 6.9 months (95% CI 5.4-NE) in pretreated advanced uLMS, with responses lasting around a year ([PMID 37467452](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37467452), [NCT03880019](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03880019)). Everything past that is hypothesis-grade: n=22, single arm, no comparator, an erratum on the record, and the RAD51-foci split everyone quotes (mPFS 11.2 vs 5.4 months by functional HR status, p=0.05) comes from 9 of 18 evaluable patients in an exploratory analysis sitting exactly on the alpha boundary with no multiplicity control. The biology behind the branch is real: uLMS carries a recognised HR-deficient subset, and in the reference series confirmed HRD ran 5 of 58 tumours, every confirmed case a homozygous BRCA2 deletion ([PMID 37143137](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37143137)); BRCA2-deleted uLMS PDX models respond to PARP inhibition, and the RAD51 assay itself calls PARP response beyond BRCA mutation ([PMID 30377213](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30377213)).

### Likelihood of desired effect

Moderate, assuming the positive branch: if her tumor lands in the HRD subset, this is the strongest second-line bet in the disease, oral and potentially durable, with the exploratory split suggesting the deficient stratum carries most of the 27%. If the panel returns proficient, expected benefit drops toward the 5.4-month arm, and this rec is foreclosed as a preferred branch rather than merely discounted. The stratifier that produced the 11.2-month figure cannot be measured on her: the RAD51 functional assay has no CLIA provider anywhere, so the orderable substitute is HRR sequencing, and presenting the panel as the same test that generated the split would be false.

### Toxicity profile

- Grade 3-4 neutropenia is published two ways in the same trial: 75% in the abstract, 13/22 (59%) for the neutropenia term in full-text Table 2. A 16-point gap between two published figures for one event in a 22-patient trial is a reporting problem, and both numbers belong in the consent conversation.
- Thrombocytopenia 32%, anemia 32%; dose reductions were frequent, 3/22 discontinued for toxicity, no treatment-related deaths.
- The stacking risk is the practical brake: this marrow will have just absorbed an induction that ran 80% grade 3-4 neutropenia. A documented CBC recovery threshold precedes any prescription.
- No stated toxicity vetoes exist to check against.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

All five personas carried the branch (board ranks 2 through 5), so there is no dissent to override; the critic's round-2 row was a qualified agreement that sharpened the consent language rather than the rank. Its ruling is adopted whole: the rank was already set by the n=22 single-arm design, the toxicity discrepancy changes the sentence she hears and the CBC threshold, and both figures are quoted. The mechanism-level caveat is the biomarker asymmetry itself: in an HR-proficient tumor the synergy premise collapses toward temozolomide alone, in a disease where single-agent dacarbazine managed 9% in a randomized setting.

### Practical considerations

Both dedicated trials are active-not-recruiting ([NCT03880019](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03880019), [NCT05432791](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05432791)), so the interim route is an off-label combination authorization built on one phase 2, anchored to a positive HRR result; an unmeasured HRD status makes the payer case and the clinical case equally weak, which is exactly why the panel goes out now. The randomized successor's eligibility (two or more prior lines including an anthracycline, no prior temozolomide or dacarbazine) constrains sequencing between now and any reopening. Oral administration would matter if she cares about infusion-chair time; nobody knows whether she does.

### Why this rank

Below the induction regimen because it is a next-line branch conditional on a biomarker nobody has measured, and above every phase-1 door because it is the only experimental option with response data in her exact histology. The agreement gap is wide: 1.0 here versus -0.2 for the best of the phase-1 rows.

## Rank 4. B7-H3 ADC approach (MGC026), stain-gated referral at second line or later

*One stain on the 2024 block decides whether the most reachable experimental door is worth booking; the class carries a terminated predecessor and no LMS response data.*

### Evidence base

The duocarmycin bystander data say heterogeneous expression is survivable for this drug class ([PMID 37775116](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37775116)), and MGC026 is recruiting US sites with sarcoma on the named cohort list and no expression gate ([NCT06242470](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06242470)). Unknown, honestly. A strong positive stain raises the odds the target is drug-reachable; it does not supply a response estimate, because none exists in this disease for any agent in the class. ### Toxicity profile

## Rank 5. FAP-targeted alpha radioligand ([225Ac]RTX-2358), FAPi-PET-gated screen at progression

*A forward-running gate at zero tissue cost: the PET either finds the target or closes the branch. The open safety question is marrow-stroma dosimetry.*

### Evidence base

Sarcoma sits near the top of the FAP expression range, and unusually the antigen is on the malignant cells themselves rather than only stroma: tumour-cell FAP at intensity >=2 in 50.7% of sarcomas, the malignant cell being fibroblastic ([PMID 37333052](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37333052)). Radioligand crossfire does not depend on the antigen homogeneity that undermines ADC and cell-therapy cutoffs, and the beta-emitting sibling compound produced regressions in sarcoma PDX models ([PMID 35608703](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35608703)), with combined-isotope preclinical work extending the class ([PMID 39237746](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39237746)). [NCT07156565](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07156565) is recruiting relapsed/refractory soft-tissue sarcoma after one prior line, FAP-positivity established by FAPi PET. No human sarcoma efficacy exists for this agent.

### Likelihood of desired effect

Unknown for treatment effect; well-defined for the screen. The PET scan settles whether the target exists at no tissue cost, and a negative closes the branch cleanly. That gate direction is the reason this row outranks the CDK7 door: a positive scan means the target is there, where the RB1 stain mostly routes toward an arm with no LMS data.

### Toxicity profile

- No human grade profile for [225Ac]RTX-2358; radioligand-class cytopenias are expected.
- The on-target concern is real and specific: FAP sits on bone marrow stromal cells, FAP-directed CAR-T caused cachexia and lethal marrow toxicity in mice, and human marrow stromal cells were recognised by the same cells ([PMID 23712432](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23712432)). A renally cleared peptide is a different exposure question from a persisting engineered T cell, but that distinction is an argument for dosimetry the trial has not yet generated.
- Sequencing an alpha emitter after an induction that ran 80% grade 3-4 neutropenia requires documented marrow recovery. ECOG 0-1 is required against an ECOG that was assumed, never documented.
- No stated toxicity vetoes exist to check against.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

The conservative dissented, and its objection is the strongest single argument in the phase-1 block: an alpha emitter pointed at a target expressed on marrow stroma, in a patient whose CBC has never been drawn, straight after heavy myelosuppression. The risktaker's counters are also real: the exposure biology differs from CAR-T, the tumour-cell expression prior is decent, and the PET gate costs nothing. The critic's prevalence-is-not-dependency pattern critique applies here as at rank 4. The adjudication keeps the option as a screen-first branch: the scan and the marrow baseline are cheap and already ordered; any treatment decision waits on human dosimetry from the trial and a recovered CBC.

### Practical considerations

Screen the month LMS-04 progresses; the FAPi PET is the entire eligibility question and can be scheduled around the restaging scan. The beta-emitter LuMIERE route ([NCT04939610](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04939610)) runs on the same PET gate and may be the gentler first pass if uptake confirms; it is consolidated into this row rather than ranked separately. The advocate's referral hold applies unchanged.

### Why this rank

Below B7-H3 because the marrow-stroma signal is an on-target liability the ADC class does not carry, above REC-617 because the gate runs forward and the physics does not depend on a response estimate that does not exist. Agreement -0.2 with the dissent adjudicated rather than dismissed.

## Rank 6. REC-617 (CDK7 inhibitor) after RB1-null IHC, second line

*A one-slide stain opens a first-in-class door, but the gate runs backwards in this histology: RB1 loss is near-universal, and the arm it routes to holds no LMS data.*

### Evidence base

[NCT07633756](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07633756) is a phase 1b of a selective CDK7 inhibitor recruiting RB1-negative metastatic leiomyosarcoma after at least one prior systemic line, single site, safety and RP2D primary. No CDK7 data of any kind exist in leiomyosarcoma at any phase. The mechanistic case, replication-stress vulnerability in RB1-null tumors, is borrowed from YKL-5-124 in RB1/TP53-null small-cell lung cancer, a different compound in a different lineage ([PMID 31883968](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31883968)).

### Likelihood of desired effect

Unknown, with the structure of the bet working against her. Because RB1 loss is near-universal in this histology, the IHC most often routes a patient into the arm with zero efficacy data rather than away from it, which is a strange way to design a decision and is why the risktaker itself conceded the pick belongs below B7-H3. Dose escalation adds the standard risk of a subtherapeutic dose.

### Toxicity profile

- No published REC-617 grade profile; CDK7-class cytopenias and GI toxicity are the expectation, with a safety primary endpoint doing the characterizing.
- Requires a biopsy-amenable lesion and RECIST-measurable disease, both uncertain against her infiltrative pulmonary pattern.
- The missing-baseline problem applies as everywhere in this block.
- No stated toxicity vetoes exist to check against.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

Three chairs objected. The critic dissented on the borrowed mechanism and the backwards gate. The concensusite dissented on the slate: a run of unlisted phase-1 agents with no on-label anchor leaves nothing a payer recognizes when the stain, the biopsy requirement or the single-site logistics fail, which is part of why the approved second-line backbone lives on the Standard-of-care table. The conservative's missing-baseline objection extends here by its own words. The risktaker held the pick but conceded the ordering, and that concession is the rank.

### Practical considerations

The RB1 stain joins the bundled IHC pass at rank 1 and costs one slide. The trial is one site (Houston); her travel tolerance from 8,500 ft has never been asked, and the advocate's condition, doors ranked but no referral booked until she answers, applies with the most force here.

### Why this rank

Last among the ranked doors: same missing evidence as ranks 4 and 5 plus a gate that runs backwards and the heaviest logistics. Agreement -0.4, the lowest score of any ranked row.

## Caveats

- **Evidence-base caveats.** One trial carries this whole page: LMS-04 is a single phase 3 from one cooperative group with no replication yet, a stratified uterine subgroup of 67/150 with no separately powered estimate, and an OS upper bound of 0.95. The maintenance phase she will spend the longest on is its weakest-evidenced part: continuation was never randomised against stopping, the registry entry has no posted results, the 2024 report has no per-term adverse-event rates, and no preclinical support for the maintenance design exists. The PARP branch rests on n=22 single-arm with an internal toxicity-reporting discrepancy (75% vs 59% for the same event) and an exploratory stratifier with no orderable assay. The three phase-1 doors have no LMS response data at all. The morcellation and local-therapy literatures are retrospective with serious risk of bias, and the local-therapy series report local control and survival that should never be conflated (90% and 46% at 24 months in the same 44 patients).
- **Compartment and biomarker dependencies.** Rank 3 assumes HRR/HRD confirmation; a proficient result drops expected benefit toward the 5.4-month arm and forecloses the branch as a preferred option. Rank 4 assumes a strong B7-H3 stain, rank 5 a positive FAPi PET, rank 6 an RB1-null IHC, and none of the three markers has been measured. The ECOG that half these protocols require is an intake assumption. The endocrine foreclosure (ER/PR <1%) holds only as firmly as the unverified specimen provenance behind it. The lung-directed consolidation branch is gated by the unretrieved 2024 operative report and by anatomy she does not currently have.
- **What would change the ranking.** A cycle 1 date placed ahead of the echocardiogram re-arms the conservative's veto against the rank-2 regimen as scheduled. An HRD-deficient panel result hardens rank 3 into the presumptive second line and strengthens its payer case; a proficient result forecloses it. SaLuDo reporting in favour of its combination would replicate the rank-2 design logic in class and raise confidence in the effect; a null would complicate it. A negative B7-H3 stain, FAPi PET or RB1 stain closes the corresponding door at no cost; a documented Y220C-class germline TP53 result would reshape the anthracycline plan itself. A failed cardiopulmonary screen moves the first-line conversation to the Standard-of-care table entirely.
- **Re-scoping caveat.** Nothing in the preference file came from her. If the four unasked questions return answers (the neutropenia trade, trial appetite and travel, ovarian and hormonal aftermath at 39, tolerance for authorization fights), the weighting under every rank on this page should be re-run against her actual answers rather than the intake defaults.
