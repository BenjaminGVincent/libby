## Research question

## Target validation paths

Four essential gating workups load the front-line conversation in parallel: MGMT promoter methylation by pyrosequencing (gates the adjuvant temozolomide commitment in the Stupp regimen and assigns Arm A vs Arm B at the BGB-58067 trial via NCT07485049), central MTAP IHC by the SP347-class clone (gates BGB-58067 via NCT07485049 and navlimetostat / BMS-986504 via NCT07492680), EGFR amplification by FISH plus EGFRvIII-specific RT-PCR or RNA-based fusion NGS (gates EGFRvIII-directed CAR-T families including E-SYNC via NCT06186401 and CARv3-TEAM-E via NCT05660369), and a comprehensive tumor NGS panel covering TERT, +7/-10, BRAF V600E, NTRK fusions, MMR, TMB, and MSI (gates dabrafenib + trametinib for BRAF V600E via NCT02034110, larotrectinib for NTRK via NCT02122913, and tumor-agnostic pembrolizumab for MSI-H or TMB-H via NCT02628067). Two high-priority companions sit alongside: RB1 functional IHC and p16 IHC. The Heidelberg methylation classifier is the orthogonal subtype call. All assays run on archival FFPE from the subtotal-resection block plus a peripheral blood draw for the germline panel. Turnaround is five to seven business days for the IHC bundle, seven to ten days for MGMT pyrosequencing, and two to four weeks for the comprehensive NGS.

### MTAP

Essential: MTAP IHC (clone 2G4 or EPR6358) on archival FFPE, scored as complete absence of cytoplasmic staining in tumor cells with retained internal control in stromal lymphocytes and endothelium. This is the central confirmation that the MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor protocols now hard-require after MTAPESTRY-101 enrolled four of six patients on local CDKN2A-only NGS calls who turned out to have intact MTAP on central IHC and progressed on therapy. Tumor NGS copy-number at 9p21 is the wrong resolution because the signal-to-noise call rate is low and CDKN2A loss does not always extend through MTAP. Skipping the IHC risks enrolling on a non-target and forfeiting the highest-yield molecular axis in this case. The same standard carries to BGB-58067 via NCT07485049 and to navlimetostat via NCT07492680.

Medium priority: a baseline plasma SDMA draw by LC-MS/MS before starting any PRMT5 inhibitor, with a paired on-treatment draw at cycle 2 day 1 if a trial is initiated. SDMA is the most proximal pharmacodynamic readout of PRMT5 inhibition; the BMS-986504 phase 1 reported a 56% median plasma SDMA reduction from baseline to cycle 2 day 1 in MTAP-deleted responders. Most PRMT5 inhibitor trials collect this on protocol so the row often becomes a confirmation that the trial central lab is doing the draw rather than a separate send-out.

Low priority: MAT2A protein expression by IHC on archival FFPE. Research-grade antibody, useful as a translational baseline for interpreting MAT2A-inhibitor target context (IDE397, AG-270) but not required for enrollment.

### CDKN2A

High priority: RB1 status combining the NGS variant call with RB protein IHC (clone G3-245). CDK4/6 inhibitors only work when RB is intact, because the kinase substrate that CDK4/6 phosphorylates is gone if RB is lost. The abemaciclib recurrent-GBM phase 2 (NCT02981940) explicitly required CDKN2A/B loss with intact RB as the eligibility pair. Tumor NGS that reports RB1 wild-type at the DNA level still misses cases where RB protein is lost post-transcriptionally, so the IHC corroboration locks the pathway integrity before the abemaciclib conversation at recurrence has anywhere to land.

High priority: p16 IHC (clone E6H4 or JC8) with the homozygous-loss pattern defined as complete absence of nuclear and cytoplasmic staining in tumor cells with retained internal control. The orthogonal protein-level call is what most CDK4/6 inhibitor protocols and tumor boards expect when the rationale rests on a single NGS copy-number signal. The IHC also catches cases where CDKN2A is functionally silenced by promoter methylation without a DNA-level deletion, which a copy-number-only panel misses.

Medium priority: CDK4 and CCND1/2/3 amplification status off the same comprehensive NGS panel. CDK4 amplification or CCND1/2/3 amplification co-occurring with CDKN2A loss creates a stronger pharmacodynamic substrate for CDK4/6 inhibition than CDKN2A loss alone; several CDK4/6 inhibitor protocols stratify or prioritize the compound genotype. Marginal cost is zero since the panel is being run anyway.

Medium priority: germline panel covering CDKN2A (familial melanoma-astrocytoma syndrome), MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2 (Lynch and CMMRD), TP53 (Li-Fraumeni), NF1, and the DNA-repair genes triggered by any tumor-side MMR loss. The homozygous-loss tumor finding is the kind of result that warrants asking whether one of the hits is germline. A high-penetrance germline call reframes surveillance and triggers cascade testing of first-degree relatives.

### MGMT

Essential: MGMT promoter methylation by pyrosequencing on four to five CpG sites in exon 1, with the cutoff at approximately 8-10% average methylation and reflex to methylation-specific PCR if pyrosequencing fails. MGMT methylation is the strongest predictor of temozolomide benefit in IDH-wildtype GBM. Methylated tumors derive a 7-8 month median survival advantage on Stupp; unmethylated tumors derive limited TMZ benefit and become priority candidates for TMZ-sparing or TMZ-alternative front-line trials. Pyrosequencing was the most prognostic of the available methods in the Reifenberger and Quillien comparisons. The case has no MGMT call recorded; without it the adjuvant TMZ commitment is being made blind, the CeTeG / NOA-09 (lomustine + TMZ + RT via NCT01149109) conditional cannot open, and the patient cannot be triaged into Arm A versus Arm B at BGB-58067 via NCT07485049.

### EGFR / EGFRvIII

Essential: EGFR amplification by FISH or NGS copy-number plus EGFRvIII-specific testing by RT-PCR or RNA-based NGS fusion panel, reporting amplification ratio and vIII status separately. EGFR amplification is present in roughly 40-50% of IDH-wildtype GBM (INTELLANCE screening cohort), and EGFRvIII appears in approximately 20-30% of EGFR-amplified tumors. The vIII call is the binary gate for EGFRvIII-directed CAR-T trial enrollment (E-SYNC via NCT06186401, CARv3-TEAM-E via NCT05660369, the Penn dual-CAR family via NCT07209241). Pan-EGFR amplification without vIII opens a separate set of EGFR ADC and bispecific protocols. Without resolution the patient is invisible to the most active GBM cellular-therapy trial families.

### Comprehensive NGS

Essential: comprehensive tumor NGS covering TERT promoter, chromosome 7 and chromosome 10 copy-number, BRAF V600E, NTRK1/2/3 fusions, MMR genes (MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2), TMB, MSI, plus IDH1/2, ATRX, EGFR, PTEN, NF1, PDGFRA, and MET. Six high-priority biomarkers sit on one panel and each opens a different therapeutic branch: BRAF V600E gates dabrafenib + trametinib via NCT02034110 (ROAR HGG cohort, ORR 33%); NTRK fusions gate larotrectinib via NCT02122913 with confirmed CNS responses; MMR loss or MSI-H gates tumor-agnostic pembrolizumab via NCT02628067 (KEYNOTE-158, ORR 29% in TMB-H); TERT and +7/-10 confirm molecular GBM classification under cIMPACT-NOW Update 3. The same panel resolves the EGFR amplification context and the CDKN2A homozygous-loss call, and returns a TMB number for the ICI rationale. Order RNA-based fusion testing alongside the DNA panel; CNS NTRK breakpoints are often intron-deep and DNA-only panels miss them. If MMR returns abnormal on the tumor side, escalate to a germline Lynch or CMMRD panel and trigger cascade testing.

### Methylation classifier

High priority: whole-genome DNA methylation profiling with the Heidelberg / DKFZ CNS tumor classifier (Illumina EPIC array, v12.8 or later), reporting methylation class, subclass score, and MGMT promoter methylation from the same array. The classifier resolves IDH-wildtype GBM into RTK I (pediatric-type), RTK II (the most common adult subtype, enriched for 9p21 / CDKN2A homozygous loss and EGFR amplification), and mesenchymal subclasses. This case sits in RTK II genomic territory and the classifier confirms it. cIMPACT-NOW Update 9 recommends methylation profiling for high-grade glioma cases with diagnostic ambiguity; the classifier impacted the final diagnosis in 46.7% of high-confidence cases in the multicentric validation. The same array returns an orthogonal MGMT call from a different probe set than pyrosequencing, which is useful when the pyrosequencing result lands near the 8-10% cutoff.

## Cross-cutting caveat (read first)

**MGMT promoter methylation, MTAP IHC central confirmation, EGFR / EGFRvIII status, and the broader comprehensive NGS panel are all unknown right now, and every downstream decision in this case hangs from those four results.** The standard-of-care backbone (rank 2, Stupp + TTFields) does not flip on the MGMT result — the regimen recommendation is the same either way — but the candor of the patient conversation does, the CeTeG conditional (rank 4) only opens at all in MGMT-methylated tumors, the BGB-58067 trial slot (rank 3) requires central MTAP IHC by an assay the protocol accepts, and the E-SYNC Cohort 1 newly diagnosed cellular-therapy slot requires both EGFRvIII positivity AND MGMT unmethylated.

The load-bearing concern that shapes the entire ranking:

- **MGMT methylation is the dominant front-line effect modifier in IDH-WT GBM.** Hegi 2005 shows the Stupp HR_OS 0.63 concentrates in the methylated stratum (mOS 21.7 vs 15.3 mo, p<0.001); the unmethylated stratum gets mOS 12.7 vs 11.8 with no significant separation. The TMZ-sparing conversation only exists in unmethylated patients. The CeTeG / NOA-09 16.7-month median OS gain (Herrlinger 2019) only exists in methylated patients.
- **Central MTAP IHC by the SP347-class clone is the trial-enrollment gate that every MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor protocol now requires.** The MTAPESTRY-101 four-of-six false-positive episode (patients enrolled on local CDKN2A-only NGS calls turning out to have intact MTAP on central IHC) is why. Tumor NGS copy-number that calls 9p21 loss does not always extend through MTAP. Run the IHC before the screening call to Phoenix.
- **The intratumoral-hemorrhage history at presentation foreclosed front-line bevacizumab and is the load-bearing reason front-line CAR-T (rank 13) carries a sustained conservative veto.** Both the advocate's and risktaker's writeups respect this carry-forward; the only persona that ranked CAR-T at front-line was the risktaker, and the conservative seat's veto is documented and lifts to qualified-on-toxicity for recurrence-line consideration only if E-SYNC Cohort 1 publishes a safety analysis that includes pre-bled patients with documented re-bleed surveillance.

## Rank 2. Stupp regimen + TTFields — RT 60 Gy / 30 fx with concurrent and adjuvant TMZ, Optune Gio added at adjuvant cycle 1

*The OS-anchored standard the entire ranking is built around.*

### Evidence base

EORTC 26981 / NCIC CE.3 (Stupp 2005 NEJM, n=573, RoB2:Low; [PMID 15758010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15758010)) gave RT + concurrent TMZ followed by 6 cycles adjuvant TMZ HR_OS 0.63 (95% CI 0.52-0.75, p<0.001), mOS 14.6 vs 12.1 mo, 2-yr OS 26.5% vs 10.4%. ### Likelihood of desired effect

High. Stupp HR_OS 0.63 has been replicated across two phase 3 trials with overlapping confidence intervals, the EF-14 add-on stacks another HR 0.63 on top of adjuvant TMZ, and the patient (66, ECOG 1) sits inside the EORTC 26981 inclusion population. The 20.9-month median is the threshold every other rank in this list is measured against. The unmethylated-MGMT scenario lowers the TMZ-attributable component of that benefit, but the RT + TTFields components persist regardless.

### Toxicity profile

- **Adjuvant-phase G≥3 hematologic AE 14%** (Stupp 2005, n=223). At age 66, expect dose modification in roughly a quarter of patients. Weekly CBC during concurrent and the first two adjuvant cycles is on-label monitoring.

- **≥18 hr/day Optune wear-time** is the binding adherence variable; sub-18-hour wear roughly halves the EF-14 OS benefit per the per-protocol analyses. Not a toxicity per se but a counseling load-bearing point.

No toxicity vetoes from the preference file are triggered.

## Rank 3. BGB-58067 (brain-penetrant MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor) on NCT07485049 — Sanai phase 0/2 at Ivy Brain Tumor Center, Phoenix

*Layered on the Stupp backbone, not substituting for it. The synthesis ranks this 3 rather than 1 because three personas dissented on rank order — load-bearing dissent on the absence of published BGB-58067 phase 1 safety, contingent on Sanai's eventual readout.*

### Evidence base

NCT07485049 is the single best mechanistic match in the dossier for this patient's MTAP/CDKN2A 9p21 co-deletion — a phase 0/2 dual-arm trial designed specifically for newly diagnosed MTAP-deleted IDH-wildtype GBM, with MGMT stratification baked into the design (Arm A unmethylated + RT; Arm B methylated + RT + TMZ) and a phase 0 SDMA / H-score pharmacodynamic readout on re-resection tissue that forces a target-engagement decision before phase 2 commits. Direct human-GBM efficacy data on BGB-58067 itself is not yet published. The class proxy is anvumetostat (AMG 193) on MTAPESTRY-101 (Rodon AnnOnc 2024 / Cancer Discovery 2025 — [PMID 39282709](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39282709), n=80 heavily pre-treated all-comer MTAP-null) at ORR 11% with G≥3 TRAE 13.8% and treatment discontinuation 2.5% — the cleanest PRMT5 class safety on record, with 56% median plasma SDMA reduction at C2D1 confirming target engagement. **Anvumetostat was discontinued program-wide by Amgen on April 30, 2026 across four phase 1b/2 trials. TNG908 was terminated in November 2024 specifically because CSF sampling in n=3 patients confirmed inadequate brain exposure (0/23 evaluable GBM ORR — [PMID 38108025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38108025)).** BGB-58067 was engineered with explicit CNS-exposure modeling against the TNG908 failure mode, but the human CSF PK data closing that loop is not yet public.

### Likelihood of desired effect

Moderate uncertainty. The mechanism case (synthetic-lethal MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibition in MTAP-null disease) is well-replicated mechanistically (Kryukov / Mavrakis / Marjon 2016 Cell / Nature companion). The clinical translation in CNS has lost twice — TNG908 and anvumetostat — and BGB-58067's brain-penetrant chemistry is the engineered answer to the proximate failure mode, but the published evidence on the molecule itself is the gap. Honest framing for the patient: open phase 0/2 slot at a high-volume center, mechanism-matched at biomarker, layered on Stupp, no published efficacy data on the drug itself.

### Toxicity profile

- **Class proxy G≥3 TRAE 13.8%, discontinuation 2.5%, no treatment-related deaths** in anvumetostat MTAPESTRY-101 (the cleanest PRMT5 class safety on record).

## Rank 8. Navlimetostat (BMS-986504) — dedicated recurrent-GBM CNS-penetration trial NCT06883747 (recruiting) plus MountainTAP-5 NCT07492680 (July 2026), with human GBM target engagement and cross-tumor NSCLC activity

*Post-board evidence-ranked addition, not yet board-voted. The board saw only the MountainTAP-5 July-2026 re-screen note; the updated-workflow re-screen added the dedicated recurrent-GBM CNS-penetration trial and the NSCLC efficacy anchor.*

### Evidence base

Two things changed since the board deliberated. First, a dedicated recurrent-GBM CNS-penetration trial — NCT06883747, run by Nader Sanai at the Ivy Brain Tumor Center, **recruiting now** — reported its first PK/PD in GBM tumor tissue at SNO 2025 ([doi:10.1093/neuonc/noaf201.1669](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/noaf201.1669)): a 77% SDMA H-score reduction confirming on-target PRMT5 engagement, and unbound drug at 45.1 nM in Gd-non-enhancing tumor that cleared the prespecified >18 nM (5x IC50) CNS-penetration threshold (plasma 111.3 nM, CSF 54.7 nM, enhancing tumor 269.0 nM). Second, the cross-tumor NSCLC cohort (Jänne, WCLC 2025, n=35 pretreated MTAP-deleted NSCLC — [doi:10.1016/j.jtho.2025.09.054](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtho.2025.09.054)) reported ORR 29% (all partial responses), DCR 80%, and mDoR 10.5 mo. Together these answer the two questions the class most needed answered after TNG908 failed on CNS exposure and anvumetostat was discontinued — does the molecule reach brain tumor, and does it drive durable single-agent activity. The MountainTAP-5 basket (NCT07492680, opens July 2026) carries a newly-diagnosed-GBM + TMZ + RT cohort. Class-context efficacy remains the anvumetostat MTAPESTRY-101 record ([PMID 39282709](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39282709)); GBM response on navlimetostat itself is unpublished, and the NSCLC numbers do not transfer to glioma.

### Likelihood of desired effect

Moderate. The molecule now has human GBM proof-of-mechanism — CNS penetration above threshold and target engagement in resected tumor — plus durable activity in another MTAP-null tumor (NSCLC ORR 29%, mDoR 10.5 mo). What it does not have is a GBM efficacy readout: NCT06883747 is a phase 0/1 surgical study measuring drug delivery, not response, and it is recurrence-gated, so the newly-diagnosed patient is not eligible today. The GBM PK/PD signal is n=1 at the 200 mg cohort. Read it as early proof-of-mechanism, not proof-of-benefit.

### Toxicity profile

- Cross-tumor safety (all-solid-tumor population, Jänne 2025): G≥3 TRAE 14%, SAE 4%, discontinuation for toxicity 2%, no treatment-related deaths.
- Hematologic events (anemia 8%, neutropenia 7%, thrombocytopenia 7%) were grade 1-3 — lighter myelosuppression than first-generation PRMT5 inhibitors.
- The GBM-specific AE table does not exist yet; the phase 0/1 trial had 2 enrolled and 1 post-surgery at the 06/06/25 cutoff, so no toxicity aggregation is possible on the GBM side.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

No board dissent, because the board did not vote on this evidence — the endorse pills carry only the advocate and risktaker, the two seats that had already flagged navlimetostat before the re-screen. The counter-productive-mechanism severity drops to Low relative to BGB-58067: the class CNS-exposure failure mode that killed TNG908 is now directly addressed for this molecule, since GBM tumor unbound drug exceeded the 5x-IC50 penetration threshold on the SNO 2025 readout. The honest caution is procedural, not mechanistic — this rec has not been through toxicity or evidence-quality scrutiny from the conservative and critic seats.

### Practical considerations

For the actionable-now slot, the gate is recurrence with re-resection candidacy: NCT06883747 requires recurrent disease and a planned operation for the paired-tissue PK, so it enters the recurrence conversation, not the front-line one — contact the Ivy Phase 0 Navigator (**602-406-8605**) when the patient progresses. For the newly-diagnosed cohort, pin July 2026 and call the BMS Clinical Trials Contact Center (**855-907-3286**) or email `Clinical.Trials@bms.com` the week MountainTAP-5 opens. The rank-1 MTAP IHC central confirmation will be in hand for either; check whether the trial's MTAP-confirmation assay matches the SP347-class IHC or needs a central-lab re-stain.

### Why this rank

It sits alongside the board-voted BGB-58067 (rank 3) on the same MTAP axis rather than above it, and above monotherapy abemaciclib (rank 10), for two reasons. Against BGB-58067: the navlimetostat GBM data is target-engagement / PD only with no response readout, the actionable-now GBM trial is recurrence-gated, and the newly-diagnosed cohort is a calendar trigger — so despite the newer CNS-penetration proof it does not displace the board-endorsed front-line-compatible option. Against abemaciclib: the MTAP axis is the patient's primary handle and navlimetostat now has human CNS proof-of-mechanism plus cross-tumor efficacy, which the CDK4/6 monotherapy rec does not. The rank is a post-board judgment, not a board vote.

## Rank 10. Abemaciclib (CDK4/6 inhibitor, CNS-penetrant) — recurrence-time pre-position keyed to the 9p21 co-deletion, gated on RB1 functional confirmation

*Recurrence pre-position; status considered_with_caveats. The critic and consensusite explicitly did NOT rank it; the advocate and risktaker flagged it for the recurrence-time combination conversation.*

Low for monotherapy — PFS6 ~9.7% in recurrent CDKN2A-loss GBM defines the activity ceiling. The combination case (CDK4/6i + PRMT5i at progression) is preclinical and untested in clinic. Assuming a positive RB1 IHC at rank 1, the rationale stands; an RB1-loss result forecloses it entirely.

### Toxicity profile

- **G≥3 diarrhea** characteristic of class; dose-limiting. At age 66, start 150 mg BID rather than the on-label 200 mg BID with close monitoring.
- **Neutropenia, fatigue** also characteristic.
- Manageable with appropriate dose modification; no class veto from the preference file.

### Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent

The critic and consensusite seats explicitly did NOT rank abemaciclib. The critic dissent is on evidence quality: PFS without OS in INSIGhT, modest monotherapy ceiling in NCT02981940, Taal 2018 palbociclib failure as cautionary precedent. The consensusite dissent is on guideline fit: NCCN Compendium does not currently list GBM as a compendia-supported off-label use, so the medical-exception letter is the route. The advocate and risktaker picked it as a recurrence pre-position; the conservative did not pick. Counter-productive-mechanism severity is Moderate because Pgp efflux at the BBB blunts the CDK4/6 class in CNS — the Taal palbociclib failure is the mechanism-level cautionary precedent, and abemaciclib was selected for partial CNS penetration but intratumoral concentrations are modest (Wen 2024 paired-surgical PK).

## Caveats

- **Evidence-base caveats.** Stupp 2005 / 2009 (RoB2:Low) and Perry 2017 (RoB2:Low) are the only RoB2:Low phase 3 evidence at front-line; EF-14 is RoB2:Some (open-label by modality), CeTeG is RoB2:Some leaning Serious (n=141, per-protocol primary, CI crosses 1.0). BGB-58067 has no peer-reviewed phase 1 published; the class proxy is anvumetostat (program discontinued April 30, 2026) and TNG908 (terminated November 2024). The rank-8 navlimetostat data is conference-abstract only (SNO 2025 GBM PK/PD is n=1; WCLC 2025 NSCLC ORR is a 35-patient cross-tumor cohort), and the rank-9 TNG456 evidence is preclinical plus a trial-in-progress with no clinical readout. All recurrence pre-positions (navlimetostat GBM slot, TNG456, CAN-3110, FUS-BBB, abemaciclib) rest on single-arm, n<50, or pre-clinical data.
- **Post-board additions (ranks 8-9).** Navlimetostat (BMS-986504) and TNG456 + abemaciclib entered the ranking on a lighter re-run of the trial screen after the board had closed deliberation. Their endorse / dissent / veto pills reflect only what the board said before the re-screen — no board endorsement or veto was fabricated for them — and both carry `considered_with_caveats` pending a full board re-vote. The toxicity and evidence-quality seats (conservative, critic) have not scrutinized the new CNS-penetration, NSCLC, or dual-axis evidence.
- **Compartment / biomarker dependencies.** Rank 3 (BGB-58067) is hard-gated on central MTAP IHC by an assay the protocol accepts. Rank 4 (CeTeG) only opens if MGMT pyrosequencing returns methylated. Rank 7 conditional axes only activate on a positive biomarker result. Ranks 8-9 (navlimetostat NCT06883747, TNG456 NCT06810544) are recurrence-gated — both require documented progression, so neither is actionable at the current newly-diagnosed timepoint; the navlimetostat MountainTAP-5 newly-diagnosed cohort is a July-2026 calendar trigger. Rank 9's abemaciclib arm and rank 10 (abemaciclib monotherapy) require RB1 intact on IHC. Rank 13 cellular-therapy menu requires EGFRvIII positivity AND (for E-SYNC Cohort 1 newly diagnosed front-line) MGMT unmethylated.
- **Hemorrhage carry-forward.** Front-line bevacizumab is foreclosed by 4/5 personas. Front-line CAR-T is vetoed by the conservative seat on hemorrhage-compounded CRS / ICANS edema risk; the veto lift trigger is an E-SYNC Cohort 1 safety analysis that includes pre-bled patients with documented re-bleed surveillance. CAN-3110 carries a hemorrhage-relative caution (annotate, do not exclude). All other ranked rows are hemorrhage-neutral.
- **What would change the ranking.**
    - **Sanai's phase 0 BGB-58067 PD / safety readout publishes.** Lifts the conservative and critic dissents on rank 3; could move BGB-58067 above CeTeG in the methylated branch or even alongside Stupp + TTFields in the unmethylated branch.
    - **MGMT returns unmethylated.** Drops rank 4 (CeTeG) entirely; strengthens the case for rank 3 (BGB-58067 Arm A) and opens the TMZ-sparing conversation as a legitimate substitute for the adjuvant TMZ component of rank 2.
    - **MTAP IHC returns intact** despite the NGS-level loss call (the MTAPESTRY-101 false-positive scenario). Drops rank 3 entirely; rank 5 (GBM AGILE) inherits the trial-enrollment slot.
    - **EGFRvIII returns positive AND MGMT unmethylated.** Re-opens the rank 13 conversation conditional on the conservative veto lift trigger; if E-SYNC Cohort 1 has published a hemorrhage-aware safety analysis by then, the row moves up the recurrence-pre-position list.
    - **BRAF V600E or NTRK or MSI-H returns positive** on the rank 1 NGS. Activates the corresponding rank 7 axis as a recurrence-time first move.
    - **The board re-votes ranks 8-9 on the new evidence.** A full board pass over the navlimetostat CNS-penetration + NSCLC data and the TNG456 dual-axis protocol could move either up (if the CNS proof-of-mechanism and cross-tumor activity persuade the conservative and critic seats) or clarify a toxicity caveat that the current post-board placement has not stress-tested.
    - **Navlimetostat publishes a GBM efficacy readout, or TNG456 reports its first GBM cohort.** Either would convert a proof-of-mechanism / preclinical rec into an efficacy-anchored one and could reorder ranks 3, 8, and 9 on the MTAP axis.
- **Re-scoping caveat.** If performance status declines between resection and RT planning, swap rank 2 to Perry CCTG CE.6 hypofractionated (40 Gy / 15 fx + concurrent and adjuvant TMZ). If the patient declines the ≥18 hr/day Optune wear-time commitment, drop the TTFields component of rank 2 (the regimen stays on TMZ alone). If the patient prefers a randomized-platform slot over a single-arm phase 0/2 (e.g. randomization-anxiety preference), swap rank 3 to rank 5 (GBM AGILE).
