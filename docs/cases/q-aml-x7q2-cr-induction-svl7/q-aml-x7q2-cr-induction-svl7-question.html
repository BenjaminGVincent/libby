Question report

Which remission-directed therapy, deliverable now as her next line, has the best evidence for inducing a complete remission (ELN 2022 CR; CRi, CRh, MLFS and composite rates reportable only when labelled as what they are) in relapsed/refractory AML-MR matched to her situation: relapse six years after a matched-sibling allograft, refractory to FLAG-IDA plus venetoclax, roughly 85% marrow blasts, complex adverse karyotype, and a right-thigh myeloid sarcoma whose response counts toward the remission?

Read the verdict precisely. It is not "we don't know": the matched populations are studied, in a 594-patient benchmark, a randomised control arm and a matched prospective cohort, and they answer negatively. Nor "nothing can be done": every deliverable candidate keeps a real, undifferentiated chance of remission, and three of five board members ranked CLAG-GO on NCT04050280 first as the one open protocol written to admit her, an eligibility pick, not a CR rate it has never published. Two findings travel with the choice.
