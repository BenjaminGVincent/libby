    DLL3-targeting interventions (1)¶

    1. tarlatamab (Imdelltra) tarlatamab, AMG 757, Imdelltra¶

    Access status: Off-label use Clinical trial   Modality: BiTE   Verified: 2026-05-06

    Two real access paths: (1) off-label prescription — Imdelltra is FDA-approved for SCLC and can be prescribed off-label for DLL3+ osteosarcoma by a clinician willing to navigate payer review; (2) clinical trial — NCT06788938 (UCLA basket) accepts DLL3-IHC-positive non-SCLC tumors and is the on-protocol pathway. The trial path is preferred when accessible because it carries trial-billing infrastructure and protocolized monitoring; off-label is the fallback if the trial is foreclosed.

    Regulatory
    FDA accelerated approval (2024) for ES-SCLC after platinum-based chemotherapy. EMA conditional approval (2025). NOT approved for osteosarcoma — but the SCLC approval makes off-label prescription a real access path in addition to trial enrollment.
    Guidelines
    NCCN category 1 for relapsed ES-SCLC; off-guideline for osteosarcoma — basket-trial enrollment is the on-protocol route.
    Geographic scope
    US — UCLA-led trial with potential satellite arrangements. Off-label prescription is available wherever Imdelltra is commercially distributed (US, EU).

    Next steps

    1. Confirm DLL3 IHC SP347 result first — required for both paths (the trial mandates ≥1% / preferably ≥25%; off-label use should not proceed without protein confirmation).
    2. Trial path: Email NCT06788938 central contact (istteam@mednet.ucla.edu) or call 888-798-0719 to request a screening slot. If outside a UCLA-affiliated geography, ask about satellite or referral arrangements.
    3. Off-label path: If the trial is foreclosed (slot unavailable, geography mismatch, screening exclusion), discuss off-label Imdelltra with the treating oncologist and institutional pharmacy. Pre-authorization with the payer is the load-bearing step; a biomarker-justified appeal letter is typically required.
    4. Contact Amgen medical information (1-800-772-6436) for technical product information and compassionate-use guidance if both trial and off-label paths are blocked.

    2. IMA203 (PRAME-TCR-T) IMA203, ACTengine PRAME¶

    Access status: Clinical trial   Modality: CAR-T   Verified: 2026-05-06

    Best-evidenced PRAME pathway with explicit pan-solid sarcoma-inclusive basket eligibility. Conditional on PRAME IHC AND HLA-A*02:01 positivity. Trial enrollment via ACTengine NCT03686124 is the realistic path; direct sponsor outreach to Immatics is the first action.

    Regulatory
    Investigational. No FDA/EMA approval. Phase 3 in cutaneous melanoma (NCT06743126); pan-solid phase 1/2 basket (NCT03686124) is the path for non-melanoma tumors.
    Guidelines
    Off-guideline. Pan-solid basket eligibility is published and protocolized.
    Geographic scope
    US, EU. Confirm specific site availability with Immatics medical affairs.

    Next steps

    1. Confirm PRAME IHC result AND HLA-A*02:01 typing first — both required for eligibility.
    2. Email Immatics ctgovinquiries@immatics.com or call +1 346 204-5400 to confirm sarcoma-cohort slot availability and screening procedure.
    3. Confirm the treating institution has CAR-T / TCR-T infrastructure (Cy/Flu lymphodepletion + CRS monitoring + ICU step-up).
    4. Plan for 4-6-week manufacturing turnaround between leukapheresis and infusion.
