Four gaps are essential: MSI/dMMR, tumor mutational burden, NTRK fusion, and homologous recombination with BRCA status. The other 63 panel entries were assessed against this tumor and set aside, with reasons at the foot of the page.

Plasma against tissue is the distinction doing the work. The Guardant tumor fraction was never supplied, so its non-detections exclude nothing. NTRK and HR/BRCA are logged as measured but not hardened: a plasma negative exists for each. MSI and TMB stayed unmeasured: the plasma assay returned no call, so there is nothing to harden.

HLA class I typing is the one settled row, and typing is not eligibility. A confirmed A*02:01 turns MAGE-A4, PRAME and NY-ESO-1 into live expression questions, all three off one archival block. Leiomyosarcoma runs well below synovial sarcoma for MAGE-A4 and NY-ESO-1; PRAME is the likeliest of the three.

Three bundles and one blood draw carry nearly every actionable row: comprehensive tissue DNA plus RNA profiling, an IHC block led by INI1, the cancer/testis stain set, and a germline HR panel. Nothing here needs a fresh biopsy, and no biopsy or cost preference was recorded at intake. Every tissue order waits on release of the September 2024 outside-institution block, the rate-limiting step; with LMS-04 pending, ordering in series costs weeks. INI1 is the stain that would move the plasma SMARCB1 finding from hypothesis to actionable, against a hard prior: 170 consecutive uterine smooth-muscle tumors all retained SMARCB1. Eight rows hand off to the target-validation track.

Most of this testing will come back negative. What justifies running it is the size of the option a positive would open, not any expectation that one is coming.
