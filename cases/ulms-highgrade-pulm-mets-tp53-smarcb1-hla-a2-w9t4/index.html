    Research question¶

    In systemic-therapy-naive metastatic high-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma with bilateral pulmonary metastases and LMS-04 planned but not started, what non-standard-of-care interventions could target the hypothesised SMARCB1/INI1 deficiency, the confirmed HLA-A*02:01 restriction allele, or the unmeasured homologous-recombination and cell-cycle landscape, gated on a tissue workup that has not yet been ordered?

    Patient profile (scrubbed)¶

    • Woman, age band 30-39. ECOG 1 is an intake assumption, never scored by a clinician, and the schema forced an integer where the honest value is unknown.
    • High-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma, spindle-cell morphology, confirmed by a reference pathology laboratory on both the pulmonary biopsy and the re-reviewed uterine specimen.
    • Stage IV: bilateral pulmonary metastases. The consulting sarcoma oncologist read the lesions as unusually infiltrative and possibly inflammatory.
    • Primary resected in September 2024 for a presumed benign 6.0 cm myometrial fibroid and signed out benign; the case was amended after re-review in July 2026, roughly a 23-month interval.
    • No systemic therapy has ever been given. Doxorubicin + trabectedin with trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04) is planned and not started.
    • ER <1% and PR <1% by IHC — confirmed, and a decision-relevant negative that closes the endocrine door.
    • SMARCB1 G344Afs*13 — plasma ctDNA only, IHC pending. Truncating, heterozygous, tumour fraction not supplied. No tissue confirmation.
    • TP53 Q52* — plasma ctDNA only, NGS pending, with an explicit clonal-haematopoiesis caveat and no paired white-cell sequencing.
    • HLA-A*02:01 — confirmed present (allele-level typing; also A*03:01, B*39:01, B*44:02, C*05:01, C*12:03).
    • MAGE-A4 / PRAME / NY-ESO-1 expression — not tested. Type first, stain second; the stain has not happened.
    • RB1, ATRX, MED12, HRD/BRCA, NTRK fusion, TMB — all NGS-pending on tissue. Plasma non-detection at unstated tumour fraction settles none of them.
    • MSI and mismatch repair — untested. PD-L1 — untested.
    • Four ctDNA variants of uncertain significance at 0.09-1.1% VAF recorded and explicitly not pursued; ERBB3 G914E at 0.9% is not a HER-family rationale.
    • Organ function: no laboratory values were supplied at intake or at this re-run. No LVEF, no pulmonary function tests, no oximetry, no baseline haemoglobin.
    • Resides at roughly 8,500 ft (~2,600 m). Lifetime never-smoker.
    • Geography band: US.

    Preferences¶

    • Efficacy/toxicity weight: 0.7 — an intake default, not her number.
    • Toxicity vetoes: none listed — because nobody asked, not because nothing is off the table.
    • Modality constraints: none listed — same caveat.
    • Trial preference: prefers trials — yes, another intake default.
    • Free text: the file says outright that no preferences were stated by the user and that every value in it is an assumption. Goals of care were never discussed at intake. A woman in her thirties with an aggressive induction planned plausibly implies curative or maximal-disease-control intent, but that is inference. prefers_trials is newly load-bearing at this re-run because both hypothesis branches the new molecular data opened are trial or off-label pathways.

    Target validation paths¶

    One block-release request decides most of what this case can become. Nearly every open question runs on two archival specimens, the September 2024 uterine resection and the 2026 pulmonary core, and the orders should go out together on a single request naming both. The INI1/SMARCB1 stain and the tissue SMARCB1 sequencing decide whether tazemetostat exists as an off-label route or the whole SWI/SNF branch closes; the HRR/HRD panel decides whether olaparib plus temozolomide (the NCI protocol 10250 regimen, NCT03880019) is winnable as an off-label authorisation at progression; the mismatch-repair and TMB reads decide whether tumor-agnostic pembrolizumab or dostarlimab is reachable; the RNA fusion panel decides whether a TRK inhibitor is even conceivable; and the antigen stains behind her confirmed HLA-A*02:01 decide whether IMA203 (NCT03686124) and its neighbours are worth a screening call. The clinic half, an echocardiogram, baseline labs and a scored ECOG, gates the planned doxorubicin plus trabectedin induction itself. Stains return in 3 to 5 days once a block is in the laboratory and tissue sequencing in 2 to 3 weeks; the slow step is administrative, because the 2024 block sits at an outside institution and nobody clinical currently owns the release request. A fully negative panel would still be a productive result: closed branches, a fitness baseline the planned chemotherapy needs anyway, and a clean record of what was looked for. The conversation about standard care from there belongs to the treating team, not Libby.

    SMARCB1¶

    The plasma G344Afs*13 frameshift is a hypothesis, not a finding, and the prior against it is steep: 170 of 170 uterine smooth-muscle tumours retained SMARCB1 in the one consecutive IHC series in this histology (PMID 34271067). The decision-grade read is INI1/SMARCB1 IHC (BAF47/SNF5 antibody, clone 25 or MRQ-27) showing complete loss of nuclear staining in tumour cells against an intact internal control; that is the result the tazemetostat basket accepted, with biallelic molecular loss as the alternative where the stain is equivocal (NCT02601950). Order the stain and the tissue SMARCB1 sequencing with copy-number and deletion calling at the same time rather than in sequence, because an equivocal stain with no molecular backup is a two-to-three-week delay this first-line window cannot absorb. Stain both specimens separately: the shedding compartment is the lung, and a metastasis-acquired event would be invisible on the 2024 block. Expectations should stay modest either way, since even a confirmed loss buys a 9 percent response rate in the INI1-negative non-epithelioid basket cohort (PMID 41882006). Germline SMARCB1 sequencing is a reflex, ordered only if tumour loss is confirmed; a reported schwannomatosis-associated uterine leiomyosarcoma (PMID 26001331) makes the question real but not urgent enough to front-load the counselling burden.

    HLA-A*02:01 and the cancer/testis antigens¶

    The typing is done and it is the expensive half of the gate: allele-level HLA class I typing confirms A*02:01. What is missing is every antigen result that would pair with it. PRAME is the most plausible positive of the three and both programs behind it, IMA203 (NCT03686124) and brenetafusp (NCT04262466), enrol on the same two-part gate she is halfway through; the operative caution is that IMA203 screens on the sponsor's IMADetect RT-qPCR, not IHC, so a local stain (clone EPR20330) is a one-slide screen whose negative closes the axis cheaply but whose positive still has to be reproduced on the assay that decides entry. MAGE-A4 is scored to a protocol threshold, at least 1+ staining in at least 10 percent of cells in SPEARHEAD-1 (NCT04044768), and the approved product is licensed in synovial sarcoma, not this histology; smooth-muscle sarcomas expressed MAGE-A4 in only a minority in the one multi-subtype series carried here (PMID 40152485). NY-ESO-1 earns one slide on the same block cut and no more, since expression runs low across sarcoma subtypes and the enrolling master protocol (NCT03967223) is currently histology-restricted away from her disease. The resistance check rides along free: ask the sequencing laboratory to report allele-specific HLA copy number, because somatic loss of A*02:01 would undercut the entire class at once, however strong the antigen stain.

    Unmeasured molecular landscape¶

    Plasma non-detection at unstated tumour fraction settles nothing, and that sentence covers most of this case. One validated comprehensive tissue panel is the single order that answers several questions at once: an MSI call, a TMB in mutations per megabase, the HRR gene set with a genomic-instability score, RNA fusion calling, and whether TP53 Q52* is in the tumour at all. Four mismatch-repair stains (MLH1, PMS2, MSH2, MSH6) on the block already being cut are the fast orthogonal read, and either route closes or opens the tumor-agnostic PD-1 indications (NCT02628067, NCT02715284). The NTRK question needs RNA rather than DNA, because plasma capture misses breakpoints in the large NTRK introns, and a spindle-cell tumour once signed out as benign is exactly where an NTRK-rearranged mesenchymal neoplasm hides. The HRR/HRD result is the one that opens a branch rather than closing one: uterine leiomyosarcoma has a recognised HR-deficient subset including BRCA2 events (PMID 37143137), and olaparib plus temozolomide produced a 27 percent response rate with median PFS 6.9 months in pretreated disease (PMID 37467452), so this is the panel result an off-label authorisation would lean on. TP53 attribution deserves its own order: paired buffy-coat sequencing or tissue interrogation of the Q52* position separates tumour DNA from clonal haematopoiesis, which contributed features to 53.2 percent of cfDNA mutations in matched sequencing (PMID 31768066), and the answer calibrates how much weight the rest of the plasma report, the SMARCB1 call included, can carry. The RB1/ATRX/MED12 backbone with its IHC surrogate panel (sequencing concordance kappa 0.941, PMID 36788080) corroborates a diagnosis whose primary was once called benign and supplies the co-alteration context trial screens ask about; the Rb read in that panel is also what the abemaciclib study (NCT06498648, Rb-intact required) and the REC-617 study (NCT07633756, Rb-loss required) will each want to see before screening. A germline multigene hereditary panel runs on the same draw as any other blood work; the slow parts are counselling and insurance, not the assay.

    uLMS histology and fitness¶

    Three orders are essential before cycle 1 and none involves tumour tissue. A transthoracic echocardiogram with LVEF and strain, because the LMS-04 schedule carries doxorubicin into the cardiotoxicity surveillance band and no cardiac reference value exists (PMID 35835135; PMID 27918725; PMID 36017568). A metabolic panel with bilirubin, transaminases and creatine kinase plus a CBC, because trabectedin's dose-limiting hepatotoxicity needs a baseline to be read against, and her count needs an altitude-adjusted haemoglobin reference: at roughly 8,500 feet a sea-level-normed CBC will make real chemotherapy-induced anaemia look normal. And a clinician-scored ECOG with Karnofsky equivalent, because the 1 on file is an intake assumption, and nearly every protocol in scope, from the PRAME TCR-T program (NCT03686124) to olaparib plus temozolomide (NCT03880019), sets a 0-to-1 ceiling at screening. The FNCLCC grading parameters behind the phrase high-grade are a report request rather than a new assay; the reference-laboratory diagnosis itself is settled and needs no second opinion.

    Bilateral pulmonary pattern¶

    Nobody has measured the pulmonary reserve of a lifetime never-smoker living at 8,500 feet with infiltrative bilateral metastases. Baseline pulmonary function tests with DLCO, plus resting and ambulatory oximetry recorded at her own altitude and labelled as such, are the only reference against which on-treatment dyspnea can be attributed to progression, adaptation or treatment; several cell-therapy protocols in scope set saturation floors of 90 percent room air, one at 92, written for sea level. A pre-treatment CT of chest, abdomen and pelvis does double duty: it establishes whether the infiltrative pattern yields RECIST v1.1 measurable target lesions at all, better discovered before a screening slot than during one, and read against anything shot in the 2024-to-2026 interval it bounds the growth kinetics of disease that went untreated for roughly 23 months. PD-L1 with matched clone and scoring, plus CD3 and CD8 infiltrate density on the lung core, informs rather than decides: it is the one way to learn whether the inflammatory radiologic impression corresponds to anything in the tissue.

    ER/PR <1%¶

    The result is confirmed and it forecloses the endocrine branch, but the report on file does not say which specimen produced it. This is a records question first: if the under-1-percent call came from the 2026 lung core, nothing more is needed; if it came from the 2024 primary, a repeat stain on the metastasis costs two slides and makes sure the door is genuinely shut, since hormone-receptor expression can differ between a uterine primary and a distant metastasis.

    The 2024-2026 interval¶

    A 6.0 cm myometrial mass removed as a presumed benign fibroid may have been morcellated, and morcellation of an unrecognised leiomyosarcoma disperses tumour through the peritoneum and worsens outcomes (PMID 28209496; PMID 26646120). The staging picture is currently pulmonary only, and whether peritoneal disease must be actively excluded rather than assumed absent turns on an operative note nobody has yet had reason to re-read. Retrieving the September 2024 operative report and gross description is free, and it decides how wide the staging imaging field needs to be drawn.

    Biomarkers measured, but not to decision resolution¶

    Two results on file are real measurements that cannot carry a decision as they stand, and both are plasma non-detections. The NTRK call is a DNA assay's silence at unstated tumour fraction, which the survey behind this report declined to read as a negative; the assay that closes it is an RNA-based fusion panel on tissue, agnostic to where in the intron a breakpoint sits. The homologous-recombination call is the same problem one step worse: the plasma panel reported no BRCA1/2 alteration but cannot produce a genomic-instability score at all, so HR status is open rather than proficient, and closing it takes tissue NGS across the HRR genes with an HRD score plus a germline panel on blood. The distinction matters: nobody doubts these targets were looked for, the looking was just done with an instrument that cannot rule them out. Six further handoffs, mismatch repair, TMB, MAGE-A4, PRAME, NY-ESO-1 and B7-H3, were forwarded because they were never measured on any assay, not because an existing number fell short; each has a matching workup order above, and no handoff was dropped. Reading either group as negative would be the mistake; the record supports pending, nothing stronger.

    Rank 4. IMA203 / IMA203CD8 PRAME-directed TCR-T cells (NCT03686124), sponsor screening started in the first-line window¶

    The only cellular axis her confirmed HLA-A*02:01 opens, double-gated on a sponsor assay and a standard-of-care-exhaustion clause.

    Evidence base¶

    The published dataset is a 41-patient first-in-human phase 1 dose escalation and extension in PRAME-positive HLA-A*02-positive solid tumours, melanoma-dominant with sarcoma patients included: confirmed objective response rate 28.9%, maximum tolerated dose not reached, median duration of response 4.4 months (range 2.4-23.0) (PMID 40205198).     Rank 5. In vivo lung perfusion with doxorubicin plus metastasectomy (NCT02811523, Toronto), as post-induction consolidation¶

    The one option in this dossier aimed at cure rather than control, and the one whose anatomic premise her current imaging may defeat.

    Evidence base¶

    Two separate evidence bases sit under this row and they should not be blended. The perfusion technique itself is a phase 1 with acute lung injury as its registered primary endpoint, resting on preclinical work (NCT02811523, PMID 26952295); there is no published human efficacy series. The survival numbers everyone quotes belong to metastasectomy: 55.4% five-year overall survival in the uterine-sarcoma subset of a 319-case Japanese multicentre registry selected on a disease-free interval over 12 months (PMID 37063124), 36% five-year survival after complete resection in the 5,206-case international registry (PMID 9011700), and median overall survival 70 months in a leiomyosarcoma subset undergoing repeated resection (PMID 21867989).     Likelihood of desired effect¶

    Genuinely unknown, and the uncertainty is about her rather than about the numbers. Her roughly 23-month interval from primary resection to metastatic diagnosis clears the registry's disease-free-interval selector comfortably. Her current disease distribution does not: bilateral, multifocal, and read as infiltrative.     • Thoracotomy morbidity, plus acute lung injury — which is the trial's registered primary endpoint, meaning the safety question is what the study is asking rather than what it has answered.

    Rank 6. Ivonescimab (PD-1 x VEGF bispecific), MSK single-arm phase 2 in leiomyosarcoma (NCT07516925)¶

    The only seat reachable before cycle 1 by explicit protocol language — and the one four of five personas refused.

    Evidence base¶

    Zero published sarcoma efficacy for the molecule. The credibility rests entirely on HARMONi-2, where ivonescimab beat pembrolizumab head-to-head on PFS in PD-L1-positive NSCLC: HR 0.51 (95% CI 0.38-0.69), median PFS 11.1 versus 5.8 months, at an interim analysis of a China-only trial with immature overall survival (PMID 40057343). The MSK study is a 20-patient single-arm phase 2 with an objective-response endpoint (NCT07516925). Against that sit three trials in or adjacent to her disease: GOG-0250, a randomised double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3 showing bevacizumab added nothing to first-line gemcitabine/docetaxel in chemotherapy-naive metastatic uterine leiomyosarcoma, PFS HR 1.12, p=0.58 (PMID 25713428); SARC028, where the leiomyosarcoma cohort went 0 for 10 while other histologies carried the signal (PMID 28988646); and a dedicated uterine leiomyosarcoma nivolumab study stopped at stage 1 for futility, 0 of 12, median PFS 1.8 months (PMID 28440953).

    Likelihood of desired effect¶

    Low. Both mechanisms in this bispecific have already been tested in this disease and lost, one of them in a randomised trial designed to answer exactly this question. The bispecific-cooperativity argument — that co-targeting produces something neither axis achieves alone — is real preclinical biology and has no clinical support in any sarcoma. HARMONi-2 says the molecule is active in lung cancer. It says nothing about hers.

    Toxicity profile¶

    • Grade 3+ treatment-related adverse events 29% versus 16% for pembrolizumab in NSCLC, the increment mostly VEGF-class effects; grade 3+ immune-related events 7% versus 8%.
    • VEGF-class hypertension and bleeding risk, in a patient with bilateral pulmonary lesions.
    • Repeated cross-country travel from 8,500 ft during first-line treatment, which is a toxicity of a different kind and is not on any adverse-event table.
    • No stated toxicity veto is engaged. The empty list is the problem, not the permission.

    Counter-productive mechanisms / dissent¶

    Three personas dissented and the advocate alone ranked it, which is where hard rule 3 bites: an option the advocate flagged as preference-aligned against three objections stays visible with caveats rather than being dropped. The critic's convergence verdict belongs on this row and cuts both ways. Four of five refused the seat off the same three papers, so that is one evidence base read four times and nobody should bank the unanimity as extra weight — but the refusal survives the correction, because GOG-0250, SARC028 and the uterine futility stop are three mutually independent trials and one of them is a randomised null in this exact disease. The risktaker's dissent is the most pointed, because the risktaker is the persona who normally takes thin-evidence bets: thin evidence with a live mechanism is a risk worth taking, and a first-line window spent on two mechanisms this disease has already beaten is not.     The eligibility text is the whole reason this row is here: patients who decline standard first-line systemic therapy are eligible, and the ECOG ceiling is 2 rather than 0-1.     How to read this table

    Toxicity burden is patient-level adverse-event severity (Low / Moderate / High by grade-3+ rates and any treatment-related death). Counter-productive MoA is a different axis: the mechanism-level risk that an intervention works against the therapeutic goal — antigen-loss escape, cell-cycle antagonism of a chemotherapy partner, an anthracycline budget spent before the door that needs it. Those are not patient side effects. The persona pills under each intervention are the at-a-glance board signal; the full per-persona rationale lives on the board page.

    Caveats¶

    • Evidence-base caveats. Rank 2 is a 22-patient single-arm phase 2 with open-label response assessment and an unreported randomised confirmation. Rank 3 has no efficacy data at all for its investigational component; its control arm's 35.8% uterine response rate comes from a 42-patient single-arm study, and the randomised comparison that keeps it honest ran the other way. Rank 4 is a 41-patient phase 1 with no patient of her histology in it. Rank 5's survival numbers come from retrospective registries whose selection criteria her current disease does not meet. Rank 6 has zero sarcoma data. The exploratory RAD51 subgroup on rank 2 (11.2 versus 5.4 months, p=0.05, n=18) is not a finding and should not be quoted as one, and the 4-patient BRCA2 series behind rank 39 is tier 4.
    • Biomarker dependencies. Ranks 7, 24, 25, 26 and 40 through 42 assume INI1/SMARCB1 loss confirmed by IHC on tumour tissue; a retained stain forecloses all seven, and the prior for a retained stain is high. Rank 3 assumes intact Rb by IHC; rank 8 assumes the opposite. Rank 4 assumes PRAME positivity on the sponsor's own RT-qPCR, not on a local stain. Rank 2's authorisation case assumes a tissue HRD or HRR result. None of these has been measured, and plasma non-detection at unstated tumour fraction is not a negative for any of them.
    • The performance-status caveat cuts across everything. ECOG 1 was assumed at intake and never scored. Every effect estimate on this page comes from a trial that enrolled ECOG 0-1, so an unmeasured score is an applicability defect inside the numbers rather than a screening formality. If the measured score is 2, a substantial fraction of what this board argued over disappears.
    • What would change the ranking. A confirmed INI1 loss on tissue lifts rank 7 out of the thin-evidence flag and makes the SWI/SNF branch real, though still modest. A tissue homologous-recombination-deficiency result moves rank 2's confidence up materially and strengthens its off-label authorisation. A thoracic surgeon calling the bilateral infiltrative pattern resectable moves rank 5 up sharply, because nothing else here has its ceiling. Publication of the accrued randomised olaparib + temozolomide phase 2/3 would either confirm rank 2 or deflate it. Her answering the trial-and-travel question either way would settle the tension the advocate has flagged on ranks 3, 4 and 6.
    • Re-scoping caveat. Every value in preferences.json is an intake default and the file says so. If she states a toxicity veto, declines travel, or turns out to want gentler therapy than an efficacy weight of 0.7 implies, this ranking changes shape rather than order — and if goals of care turn out to be something other than the maximal-disease-control intent the planned induction implies, several of these rows stop being appropriate to offer at all.
