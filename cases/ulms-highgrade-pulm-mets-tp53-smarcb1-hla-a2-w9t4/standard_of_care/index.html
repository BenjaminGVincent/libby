    16 standard options assessed: 4 to consider now, 3 behind an open gate, 0 already received.

    Sixteen standard options were screened for this systemic-therapy-naive metastatic uterine leiomyosarcoma, against NCCN Uterine Neoplasms v2.2026, NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma v1.2025, the ESMO-EURACAN-GENTURIS sarcoma guideline, and current FDA labels. Four are open now, and the first is the regimen already planned: doxorubicin + trabectedin with trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04), preferred by NCCN for uterine LMS and the board's first choice on four of five slates. Its trial doubled progression-free survival and moved median survival from 24 to 33 months, though no uterine-only effect size has been published and the regimen's cost, including 28% febrile neutropenia, has not yet been discussed with her. Gemcitabine + docetaxel, doxorubicin + ifosfamide, and single-agent doxorubicin are the endorsed alternatives.

    Nothing systemic is spent. Her only prior treatment is the 2024 hysterectomy, so the whole first-line landscape is open, and that is why sequencing dominates this table: off-protocol gemcitabine permanently closes the abemaciclib trial seat (NCT06498648), which LMS-04 preserves, and every doxorubicin cycle draws down two later trial ceilings (360 mg/m2 for peposertib, exactly six LMS-04 cycles; 450 mg for in-vivo lung perfusion). The induction cycle count is a before-cycle-1 decision.

    Three label routes sit behind open gates: pembrolizumab and dostarlimab on TMB and MSI/MMR that were never measured, larotrectinib on an NTRK fusion never tested on tissue. One door is already closed by results: ER/PR under 1% forecloses endocrine therapy. Metastasectomy and SBRT wait at restaging; their favourable series describe countable nodules, not her current infiltrative bilateral pattern.

    This report adds the standard options alongside the experimental ranking. It does not narrow, reorder, or argue against anything ranked there.

    Assessed and set aside¶

    2 option(s) screened against this patient and set aside

    Screened against this patient and found not to apply. Listed for the audit trail.

    • Gemcitabine + docetaxel + bevacizumab: Assessed because the routing worklist carried it, and set aside on its own randomised evidence: GOG-0250 tested exactly this regimen in exactly this population, chemotherapy-naive metastatic uterine LMS, and stopped early for futility with the hazard ratios pointing the wrong way. Bevacizumab holds no FDA sarcoma indication, and the current uterine-sarcoma guideline lists do not carry it, so the plain gem/doc row above is the live version of this backbone.
    • Aromatase inhibition / anti-estrogen therapy: Included so the absence of endocrine therapy reads as a decision, not an oversight: anti-estrogen therapy is the guideline's carve-out for hormone receptor-positive uterine sarcoma, and her confirmed ER/PR <1% takes her out of that population. This is the one standard-of-care door her existing biomarker results have already closed.

    How these sit alongside the experimental options¶

    These options and the experimental ranking are two co-equal tables, and nothing here changes that ranking. What this section adds is the sequencing: where a standard option and an experimental one compete for the same line, and where taking one would close the door on the other.

    Competes for the same line of therapy

    • Pulmonary metastasectomy after systemic control: The in-vivo lung perfusion trial (NCT02811523, Experimental rank 5) and pulmonary suffusion are metastasectomy-plus-perfusion packages occupying this same post-induction local-therapy slot; IVLP excludes cumulative doxorubicin above 450 mg, so the induction plan chosen now decides whether the surgical route can still be the trial version or only the standard one. (interacts with: ivlp-doxorubicin, pulmonary-suffusion)
    • Stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT) to lung metastases: Occupies the same post-induction local-consolidation slot as metastasectomy and the surgical-perfusion trial packages (IVLP, suffusion); ablating index lesions may also change eligibility for lung-directed trial procedures, so the local-therapy modality is best chosen once, at restaging, with all of these on the table. (interacts with: ivlp-doxorubicin, pulmonary-suffusion)

    May foreclose a targeted option

    • Doxorubicin + trabectedin, then trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04 regimen): Contains no gemcitabine, so it preserves the seat on the abemaciclib + gem/doc trial (NCT06498648, Experimental rank 3), which off-protocol gemcitabine would permanently close. Each cycle also draws down two anthracycline ceilings: peposertib (NCT05711615) requires cumulative doxorubicin of 360 mg/m2 or less, exactly six LMS-04 cycles at 60 mg/m2, and in-vivo lung perfusion (NCT02811523) excludes more than 450 mg cumulative. The induction cycle count is a before-cycle-1 decision; a five-cycle plan is a choice made up front, not at cycle six. (interacts with: abemaciclib-gemcitabine-docetaxel, peposertib-liposomal-doxorubicin, ivlp-doxorubicin)
    • Gemcitabine + docetaxel: Off-protocol gemcitabine permanently closes NCT06498648 (abemaciclib + gem/doc, Experimental rank 3), which admits treatment-naive patients; the trial is the protocolised way to receive this same backbone. LMS-04 contains no gemcitabine and leaves that seat open, an asymmetry that belongs in the first-line conversation. Choosing gem/doc off protocol spends none of the anthracycline ceilings the doxorubicin-based options draw down. (interacts with: abemaciclib-gemcitabine-docetaxel)
    • Doxorubicin + ifosfamide (with mesna): Draws down the same two anthracycline ceilings as LMS-04, and faster: at 75 mg/m2 per cycle, the 360 mg/m2 cap for peposertib (NCT05711615) is crossed inside five cycles and the 450 mg cumulative exclusion for in-vivo lung perfusion (NCT02811523) follows shortly after. Contains no gemcitabine, so the abemaciclib + gem/doc trial seat stays open. (interacts with: peposertib-liposomal-doxorubicin, ivlp-doxorubicin)
    • Doxorubicin, single agent: Same anthracycline-ceiling arithmetic as the combinations: cumulative doxorubicin above 360 mg/m2 excludes peposertib (NCT05711615) and above 450 mg excludes in-vivo lung perfusion (NCT02811523). No gemcitabine, so the abemaciclib + gem/doc seat stays open. (interacts with: peposertib-liposomal-doxorubicin, ivlp-doxorubicin)
    • Gemcitabine + dacarbazine: Contains gemcitabine, so the same foreclosure as gem/doc: any off-protocol gemcitabine closes NCT06498648 (abemaciclib + gem/doc) if that seat is still open when this option comes up. (interacts with: abemaciclib-gemcitabine-docetaxel)
    • Temozolomide: Single-agent temozolomide off protocol may affect eligibility for the ranked temozolomide combinations, olaparib + temozolomide (Experimental rank 2) and tuvusertib + temozolomide; protocol prior-exposure rules should be checked before it is used alone. (interacts with: olaparib-temozolomide, tuvusertib-temozolomide)
    • Pembrolizumab (tumor-agnostic, TMB-high or MSI-high/dMMR): Prior PD-1 exposure can exclude patients from PD-1-based trial seats, including the ivonescimab study the board weighed (Experimental rank 6); if the gate ever opens, the trial-versus-label sequencing is worth checking before dosing. (interacts with: ivonescimab)
    • Dostarlimab (tumor-agnostic, dMMR): As with pembrolizumab, prior PD-1 exposure can close PD-1-based trial seats such as the ivonescimab study; the two label routes share one gate and one sequencing caveat. (interacts with: ivonescimab)

    An option earns a row here only if a regulator approved it for a population that includes this patient, or a major society's guideline carries it. Fit to this patient is judged against the case profile alone, so an option the patient has already had and progressed on is marked rather than re-offered.
