    Persona stances

    PersonaRankConfidenceRationaleConcerns

    conservative4lowI hold pembrolizumab in reserve for second line because its safety profile is the best characterised of anything non-cytotoxic in this dossier: over a decade of post-marketing use since the 2014 approval, and irAE algorithms every oncology unit already runs. The chondrosarcoma evidence is genuinely thin (1 responder of 5 in SARC028, 1 PR of 5 in the Bui cohort), so this pick firms up only if PD-L1 on the dedifferentiated component, TMB or MSI comes back supportive. Monotherapy is the version I can defend; the sunitinib-nivolumab bone cohort logged a treatment-related grade 5 pneumonitis and 17% toxicity discontinuation, which is a different risk conversation for an assumed-ECOG-2 patient. The tumour-agnostic TMB-high label is a real if unlikely on-label route after first-line chemotherapy.PD-L1 prevalence of 41-52% in dedifferentiated tumours is prevalence, not response prediction.; Chondrosarcoma is typically TMB-low, so the on-label pembrolizumab route is a long shot that costs nothing to test but should not be planned around.; All efficacy data are single-arm or retrospective; there is no PFS estimate to schedule a second line against.
    critic4lowAdd up the whole prospective checkpoint experience in this disease and it comes to 1 responder of 5 in SARC028, inside a cohort whose primary endpoint was not met (2 of 40) and whose responder's subtype the primary report never states, plus 1 PR of 5 pembrolizumab-treated in a retrospective five-centre series, plus a single case report of a sustained CR. The attribution of that SARC028 responder to dedifferentiated histology is secondhand in later sources, and I will not plan around a citation that traces back to nobody. The microenvironment work argues against the axis rather than for it: macrophages are the dominant population, a high CD68+/CD8+ ratio was independently prognostic (p<0.01, n=76), and in the immunocompetent rat model depleting T cells accelerated growth while depleting CD163+ macrophages slowed it. It keeps a place on my list only because a TMB >=10 or MSI-high call would make it on-label, and the panel that answers that is already going out.PD-L1 positivity of 41-52% in dedifferentiated tumours is prevalence, and the one documented responder in the five-centre series was TPS 7% / CPS 8 and microsatellite-stable, so the marker is doing no predictive work in either direction.; The on-label TMB route is a low-prior bet: TMB >=10 mut/Mb appears in 3.9% of sarcomas and chondrosarcoma was not among the ten KEYNOTE-158 cohorts.; Open-label single-arm response ascertainment throughout, with no PFS estimate in this histology against which to schedule a second line.; The 1-of-5 and 1-of-4 numbers everyone cites are subgroups of four and five patients. Their confidence intervals run from roughly nothing to roughly everything.
