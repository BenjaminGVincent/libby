    Selected general biomarker report — chondrosarcoma-mets-acetabular-fx-idh-unknown-y34r¶

    Which biomarkers on the selected panel this patient has been tested for, and which have never been measured. The panel covers targets established in this tumor type, targets relevant across a subset of tumor types, and biomarkers that predict benefit regardless of where the tumor started, such as MSI-H/dMMR and high tumor mutational burden gating PD-1 blockade.

    14 biomarkers surveyed: 14 with no usable result on file (2 essential), 0 measured but not to decision resolution, 0 already established. Panel coverage: 9 tumor-agnostic biomarkers plus 5 of 72 protein targets in scope for this tumor.

    Fourteen biomarkers were surveyed against this record, and not one has a usable result. Eleven were never measured. The other three, MAGE-A4, NY-ESO-1 and PRAME, cannot be assessed, because each reads against an HLA class I genotype that was never obtained. Two gaps are essential: MSI/dMMR and tumor mutational burden, the pair gating tumor-agnostic PD-1 blockade. Every row here is a hole in the record, not a statement about the tumor.

    Closing all fourteen takes three orders. A comprehensive tumor DNA plus RNA panel, run on the dedifferentiated component, returns TMB, NTRK and RET fusions, BRAF V600E and the homologous recombination genes, and settles the IDH question the case has carried since intake. One block cut from the diagnostic biopsy covers the immunohistochemistry set: four mismatch-repair stains, PD-L1, B7-H3, HER2. A blood draw types HLA class I, and it should go first, since the allele decides whether the three cancer-testis antigen stains are worth the tissue; a non-A*02 type closes that class in one step. Those four rows sit with the target-validation track under their survey IDs. FAP is the outlier, imaging rather than tissue, worth ordering only if a FAP-directed trial is in play.

    No gap needs a fresh biopsy: blood, the existing block (or the acetabular specimen if stabilisation surgery goes ahead), and one optional scan.

    Expect negatives. MSI-H turned up in under 1 percent of sequenced sarcomas, TMB of 10 or more mutations per megabase in about 4 percent, and a TRK or RET fusion in a cartilaginous tumor would be a case report. The argument for testing is not that a positive is likely; it is the size of what a positive opens, approved tumor-agnostic drugs for a patient who otherwise has no approved targeted option, which fits her stated intent to pursue every possible one.

    What this collapses to¶

    The gaps above resolve onto this many distinct orders. Biomarkers that ride along on the same assay are grouped together.

    • Comprehensive tumor DNA plus RNA NGS panel run on the dedifferentiated component — BRAF V600E mutation, Homologous recombination deficiency / BRCA1 / BRCA2 pathogenic variant, NTRK1 / NTRK2 / NTRK3 gene fusion, RET gene fusion, Tumor mutational burden, high (TMB-H, 10 or more mutations per megabase)
    • Blood draw for HLA class I genotyping — HLA class I genotype (4-digit), MAGE-A4 (HLA-A02:01-restricted), NY-ESO-1 (CTAG1B, HLA-A02:01-restricted), PRAME (HLA-A*02:01-restricted)
    • One tissue block cut for the immunohistochemistry set — B7-H3 (CD276) surface protein expression, HER2 protein overexpression (IHC 3+), Microsatellite instability / mismatch-repair deficiency (MSI-H / dMMR), PD-L1 protein expression
    • 68Ga-FAPI PET/CT, or FAP immunohistochemistry if a block is already being cut — Fibroblast activation protein alpha (FAP)

    Reading this report

    This page answers one question per biomarker: has this patient been tested for it, and was the test at a resolution that can carry a treatment decision. Not measured means nothing is on file, which is not the same as a negative result. Measured, not decision-grade means a result exists but cannot be acted on as it stands; those gaps are worked into the Target validation paths report. Priority weighs what is going unassessed against how routine the assay is. Most of these biomarkers will be negative if tested; the case for testing rests on the size of the option that opens if positive, not on how likely a positive is.
