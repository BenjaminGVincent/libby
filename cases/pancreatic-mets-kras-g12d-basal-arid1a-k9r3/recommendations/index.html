    Reading the columns

    Toxicity burden is patient-level G3+ AE severity (Low / Moderate / High) summarized from trial publications. Counter-productive MoA is the mechanism-level risk that the intervention's own pathway could blunt the therapeutic goal — distinct from patient AEs. The board's endorse / dissent / veto state appears as pills under each intervention; full per-persona rationale lives on the board page.
