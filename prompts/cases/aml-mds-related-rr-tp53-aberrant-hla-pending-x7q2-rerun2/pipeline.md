## Explicitly excluded from the roster

- **Revumenib, ziftomenib, bleximenib (menin inhibitors).** Approved or in
  late-stage AML trials, and mechanistically irrelevant here: they act on
  KMT2A-*rearranged* or NPM1-mutant disease. This patient has KMT2A
  **amplification** and no reported NPM1 mutation. Route to
  `standard_of_care_screener` flagged "target absent", not into `trials.jsonl`.
- **CPX-351, oral azacitidine, glasdegib, FLT3 and IDH inhibitors.** Standard or
  targeted AML care that touches none of the nominated features.
