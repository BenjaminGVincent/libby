## Coverage gaps carried into the run log

- Nectin-4 has ≥ 10 named agents; all with a registry record were searched by
  alias with and without hyphens. Three (9MW2821, AK146D1, LNF2105) have no
  US-reachable trial and are rostered without a row.
- The MUC1 bench is thin and the two US-reachable agents both carry structural
  problems for this patient (topoisomerase-payload washout, ILD history).
- FAP is imaging-lead-only in this case; the therapeutic FAP programs either do
  not enroll urothelial carcinoma (FiREBOLT) or require FAP-2286 PET positivity
  the 2025-09 scan argues against.
