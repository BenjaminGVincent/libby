# Executive summary

**Case:** `chondrosarcoma-mets-acetabular-fx-idh-unknown-y34r`
**Question:** In treatment-naive metastatic high-grade chondrosarcoma with dedifferentiated features, presenting with a pathologic left acetabular fracture and dispersed bilateral pulmonary metastases, which investigational options could target the dedifferentiated histology, the untested IDH1/IDH2 status and the unmeasured molecular landscape?
**Evidence base:** 27 trial rows, 30 clinical-evidence rows, 30 preclinical rows and 20 target-validation rows; a 5-persona board with 39 critiques; agreement scores from 0.8 (the diagnostic panel) to -1.0 (particle therapy).

## What this report covers

The Experimental table of a two-table case: 23 feature-targeting investigational rows, six ranked live and seventeen surfaced with a flag instead of a rank. The co-equal Standard-of-care page carries the approved and guideline-endorsed options.

## Routed to standard care, not rejected

Four interventions the board ranked are absent from the table below because they are standard care for a population that includes her, not because anyone argued against them. Doxorubicin-based chemotherapy was the unanimous round-1 first choice of all five personas and remains the expected first-line treatment. Orthopaedic fixation of the fractured acetabulum, palliative radiotherapy with early specialist palliative care, and referral to a high-volume sarcoma centre travel with it. All four live on the Standard-of-care page. A reviewer who concludes from this table that the board declined chemotherapy has been misled by the filing system, which is why this paragraph comes first.

## Top-line findings

- The only `recommended` row is a diagnostic: a comprehensive tumour DNA plus RNA panel (IDH1/2, TMB, MSI, fusions) on macrodissected dedifferentiated tissue. It runs on archival tissue alongside first-line treatment and must not delay it.
- Nothing molecular is measured yet, and the recorded ECOG of 2 is an intake assumption from the broken hip, never scored in clinic. Most protocols in this screen require ECOG 0-1, so a measured score is the single highest-leverage number in the case. Whether fixing the hip would raise her performance status (the advocate's argument) or spend it on weeks of recovery (the conservative's) is unresolved; nothing in the dossier measures it.
- Every therapeutic rank carries low confidence from every persona who ranked it. The top therapeutic row, IACS-6274 on NCT05039801, holds its place on availability rather than evidence: what is ranked is a screening call to the one open protocol that names treatment-naive chondrosarcoma, over the critic's dissent that no glutaminase inhibitor has a published response in this disease.
- Ranks 13-16 are conditional on a confirmed IDH1/IDH2 mutation and foreclose if sequencing is negative. The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped: a negative panel exhausts the within-scope options on that axis, and standard care for the indication is the treating team's conversation, outside this report. Even a positive call buys less than the word "mutation" suggests: both chondrosarcoma IDH trials exclude her histology by name, and the only published figure in her subtype is a median PFS of 1.5 months.
- One unanimous rejection: particle therapy to the acetabulum (rank 23, agreement -1.0, all five personas in dissent). No formal veto was filed anywhere in this case, so this dissent block is how the board's only outright rejection reaches the reader.
- The board would not settle her first-line response expectation and this summary does not either. The bracket runs 9% (modern five-centre cohort) to 20.5% (a 44-patient subset treated 1988-2011 with local response assessment, whose p=0.04 compares dedifferentiated against conventional histology rather than testing whether chemotherapy helped anybody). She should hear the range and the reason the ends disagree.
- Also unresolved: whether "pursue all possible therapeutic options," her one stated preference, licenses agents with no disease-specific evidence. The risktaker and concensusite built opposite slates from the same sentence, and asking her costs a conversation.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** comprehensive tumour DNA plus RNA panel on macrodissected dedifferentiated tissue (rank 1, agreement 0.8, four of five personas at their highest confidence). It opens or closes the IDH axis, the abemaciclib seat, TAPUR matching and the checkpoint route.

- **Rank 2. IACS-6274 (glutaminase-1 inhibitor), phase 1 NCT05039801** - the screening call, not the drug; entry needs a measured ECOG of 0-1. The critic dissents.
- **Rank 3. Abemaciclib on NCT04040205** - the cleanest trial design in the screen, sitting behind three untested gates and a completed anthracycline line.
- **Rank 4. Pembrolizumab, second line, conditional on TMB-high or MSI-high** - the entire prospective checkpoint record in chondrosarcoma is five patients; a qualifying call would move it on-label and off this table.
- **Rank 5. TAPUR (NCT02693535)** - access without any efficacy estimate; the one fitness bar (ECOG 0-2) she may clear as written.
- **Rank 6. Sunitinib plus nivolumab, off-label at first progression** - the only checkpoint dataset in which a dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma responded, bought with a treatment-related grade 5 pneumonitis; agreement -0.2, the sharpest split on the board.

Ranks 7 through 23 are surfaced with flags rather than ranked: the B7-H3 CAR-T and ADC programmes behind an unordered H-score, the IDH-gated agents, the HLA-gated cell-therapy platforms, and particle therapy.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across treatment lines, payer and access mechanics, and everything on the Standard-of-care page. This is decision support for a conversation with her treating team, not a treatment plan.

## How to use this report

Each ranked row carries the board's agreement state (endorsements, dissents, score) and one to three anchor citations; the full per-trial extraction and per-persona rationale live on the case page. Sixty seconds here should leave you holding the two gates: an unmeasured molecular landscape, and an ECOG nobody has measured.
