# Executive summary

**Case:** `gist-sdh-multifocal-resected-m1-k4n8`
**Question:** For multifocal SDH-deficient gastric GIST (KIT/PDGFRA wild-type) status post R0 distal gastrectomy with Roux-en-Y plus complete resection of a single M1 deposit, currently NED, what is the right adjuvant or surveillance plan and what belongs on file for first measurable recurrence?
**Evidence base:** 15 trials surfaced, 22 clinical-evidence rows (13 included, 9 excluded under molecular-subset transport rules), 22 preclinical rows (14 included). Twelve ranked recommendations span agreement scores from +1.0 (workup, registry) to -0.8 (adjuvant imatinib, vetoed by every persona). Four of five personas converged on surveillance; two formal dissents on pemigatinib; one conservative veto on olaparib + temozolomide bounded to trial-route only.

## What this report covers

The board's synthesis of the adjuvant-versus-surveillance decision today, the diagnostic gate that must clear before any trial-route option is reachable, and the contingency menu held in reserve for first measurable recurrence.

## Top-line findings

- The dSDH-GIST label is not fully locked in. Retained SDHB IHC alongside biallelic SDHA inactivation at ~85% combined VAF is paradoxical, and every dSDH-GIST trial in this dossier uses SDHB-IHC loss as the entry phenotype. Rank 1 is a one-tissue-pull workup bundle that resolves this before any contingency is acted on.
- Adjuvant systemic therapy is the unanimous reject. Boikos 2016 logged 1/49 (2%) PR in dSDH-GIST on imatinib; NCCN GIST v1.2025 and ESMO-EURACAN-GENTURIS 2022 both explicitly recommend against it. Active surveillance plus the NCI Rare Tumor Natural History Study, paraganglioma baseline screening, and Signatera ctDNA (informative, not therapy-triggering) carry the management today.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped to dSDH-GIST. If the SDHA workup collapses (SDHB intact plus SDHA intact on clean re-stain, or no germline finding and L306P proves tumor-only artifact), the trial-route picks are foreclosed. In that branch this report has no within-scope recommendations and the conversation about KIT/PDGFRA-WT systemic care belongs to the treating team.
- Preferences are defaulted and geography is unspecified. Both axes become load-bearing at recurrence (NCT05661643 is Asan in Seoul, PEMIGIST is DFCI single-site, LITESPARK-015 has 14 US sites) and should be elicited before any contingency hardens.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** SDHA germline panel + SDHA/SDHB IHC re-cut with internal control + tumor-tissue NGS confirmation of the ctDNA-only variants (one tissue pull, three answers).

1. **Active surveillance** with imaging every 3 to 6 months for 2 to 3 years, no adjuvant systemic therapy. Cross-society guideline call.
2. **NCI Rare Tumor Natural History Study (NCT03739827) plus cancer-genetics counseling.** Zero toxicity, highest yield per cost on the page. The only trial the patient can join today.
3. **PGL / PCC baseline surveillance** with plasma free metanephrines and rapid-sequence whole-body MRI. Patient-safety floor for an SDHx carrier.
4. **Signatera tumor-informed ctDNA MRD** as imaging adjunct. A positive draw prompts shorter-interval imaging, not a TKI.
5. **Sunitinib** at first measurable recurrence. Best-characterized 2L for KIT/PDGFRA-WT GIST; Boikos 2016 18% dSDH ORR versus 2% on imatinib.
6. **Belzutifan on LITESPARK-015 wt-GIST cohort (NCT04924075).** Cleanest mechanism case; 14 US sites recruiting. *Conditional on `dSDH-GIST phenotype` positive (SDHB-IHC loss). Foreclosed if test is negative.* Roux-en-Y anatomy compounds the anemia-management problem.
7. **Temozolomide on NCT05661643 (Asan) or NCT03556384 (UCSD, watch for readout); off-label generic with MGMT enrichment.** Yebra 2022 2/5 PR with mechanism support; n=5 uninformative as a point estimate. *Conditional on `dSDH-GIST phenotype` positive. Foreclosed if test is negative.*
8. **Pemigatinib on PEMIGIST (NCT07434843, DFCI single-site).** Freshest mechanism story; two formal dissents on evidence pyramid and preference fit. *Conditional on `dSDH-GIST phenotype` positive. Foreclosed if test is negative.*
9. **Regorafenib** at 3L progression. Registration-grade GRID anchor; dSDH subset by extrapolation only.
10. **Olaparib + temozolomide** late-line, trial-route or hematology-co-managed only. *Conditional on `dSDH-GIST phenotype` positive. Foreclosed if test is negative.* Conservative veto overridden to bounded use with a pre-specified 9 to 12 month cumulative-exposure stopping rule.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose modification schedules, payer authorization pathways, the standard KIT/PDGFRA-WT systemic backbone if the dSDH phenotype does not confirm, and any conversation about the patient's specific symptom burden, scan-anxiety tolerance, or travel constraints. Those are care-team conversations.
