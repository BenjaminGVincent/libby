# Executive summary

**Case:** `pancreatic-mets-kras-g12d-basal-arid1a-k9r3`
**Question:** In newly diagnosed metastatic basal-like pancreatic adenocarcinoma (treatment-naive, large primary plus multiple liver metastases), what can target the KRAS G12D driver or the ARID1A loss-of-function synthetic-lethal axis?
**Evidence base:** 16 trials, 13 clinical-evidence rows (4 ranked anchors), 8 preclinical rows, and 6 target-validation rows; agreement scores 0.6 to 1.0. All five personas endorsed all four options. The disagreement is about order, not membership, and nobody issued a veto.

## What this report covers

The PI scoped the ranking to two molecular targets, KRAS G12D and ARID1A loss, and ranked four therapeutic options under a single unbranched list since every biomarker reads as confirmed. This PDF carries that synthesis plus the diagnostic workup that hardens the target calls.

## Top-line findings

- The immunotherapy axis here is a mirage. A lone somatic MLH1 variant sits in an MSS, TMB-8.6, germline-negative tumor that most likely reflects MMR-proficient biology, so checkpoint monotherapy is low-yield. MMR protein IHC is the gate before any dMMR/MSI-H claim.
- Rank 1, daraxonrasib, is the only feature-targeting option carrying a positive randomized phase 3 (RASolute 302, OS HR 0.40, n=500). That result was measured at second line; the patient is treatment-naive, so the first-line benefit rests on the unread RASolute 303, and his G12D estimate is borrowed from the RAS G12 subgroup.
- No veto was lodged, but the ranking is contested. The risktaker and advocate would lead with the gentler allele-matched zoldonrasib for a 79-year-old; the critic and concensusite refused to rank that n=40 phase 1 abstract above the randomized survival data.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. It covers only KRAS-G12D-directed and ARID1A-directed agents. Standard first-line care for this indication is a separate conversation with the treating team that this report does not cover.
- The patient is HLA-C*05:01/C*06:02, not C*08:02, so the published anti-KRAS-G12D TCR-T cannot present his epitope. The ASXL1 plasma frameshift may be clonal hematopoiesis and needs paired buffy-coat sequencing before it counts as a tumor target.

## Recommendation summary

1. **daraxonrasib (RMC-6236), pan-RAS(ON)** via RASolute 303. The only ranked option with randomized phase 3 survival data; first-line benefit inferred from an unread trial.
2. **zoldonrasib (RMC-9805), G12D(ON)-selective** via RASolute 305. Allele-matched and the gentlest agent in the set, but the evidence is a single-arm n=40 abstract with no durability readout.
3. **INCB161734, G12D-selective + chemo** via DAWN-303. Strongest single-agent response number, but the enrollable front-line construct bundles chemotherapy with about 40% grade 3+ neutropenia.
4. **ceralasertib (AZD6738), ATR inhibitor** for the ARID1A axis via NCT03682289. A banked second axis. No PDAC responder exists, entry is BAF250a-IHC-gated, and the lead basket is not recruiting.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer or access logistics all sit outside this synthesis. It is decision support for the targetable-feature question, not a treatment plan.

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state (endorsed or dissented by persona) and 1 to 3 anchor citations you can verify in two clicks. The full per-trial extraction lives on the live case page; this PDF is the synthesis.
