# Executive summary

**Case:** `osteosarcoma-mets-dll3-h7r2`
**Question:** In metastatic osteosarcoma after first-line MAP, what interventions target the patient's DLL3 and PRAME features, both currently detected only at the RNA level?
**Evidence base:** 41 trials on the DLL3 and PRAME axes, 6 included clinical-evidence rows, 6 preclinical rows; agreement scores span 1.0 to −0.2, with all five personas endorsing the rank-1 workup and none issuing a veto.

## What this report covers

Libby's synthesis of the antigen-directed options the patient nominated: a shared biomarker workup that gates everything, then six therapeutics ranked across the DLL3 and PRAME pathways. This PDF is the packaged synthesis; the per-trial extraction lives on the live case page.

## Top-line findings

- Both features are RNA-only, and RNA is not a target. A rank-1 workup (DLL3 IHC SP347, PRAME IHC, HLA-A*02:01 typing) is the load-bearing first move: it opens or forecloses each axis, and every therapeutic rec below it is conditional.
- The rank-1 therapeutic, tarlatamab, carries the only RCT-grade evidence on the board (DeLLphi-304 OS HR 0.60), but every efficacy row is SCLC. No osteosarcoma activity has been shown; the IHC gate carries the whole extrapolation.
- The board split on which drug leads. The critic and conservative rank tarlatamab first on RCT tier and managed safety; the risktaker and advocate lead IMA203 on its 54% ORR. IMA203 also carries one treatment-related death in n=28, which drove three dissents.
- The ranking is scoped to the nominated DLL3/PRAME axis. A negative test exhausts the within-scope options on that axis; standard care for relapsed osteosarcoma is a separate treating-team conversation this report does not cover.
- Whether osteosarcoma contributes any response to these largely cross-tumor (SCLC, melanoma) datasets is unresolved.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** DLL3 IHC SP347 + PRAME IHC + HLA-A*02:01 typing. The diagnostic gate; batch all three day one.

1. **Tarlatamab** (DLL3 × CD3 BiTE): best-validated mechanism, RCT-tier but SCLC-only; lighter-toxicity DLL3 route. Conditional on `dll3_ihc` positive; foreclosed if negative.
2. **IMA203** (PRAME TCR-T): highest ORR in the dossier (54%, n=28) with a named sarcoma cohort, and one treatment-related death; board split. Conditional on `prame_hla` positive; foreclosed if either test is negative.
3. **Brenetafusp / IMC-P115C** (PRAME × CD3 ImmTAC): best-tolerated PRAME route, off-the-shelf, but modest single-agent efficacy and a currently-closed sarcoma slot. Conditional on `prame_hla` positive.
4. **IMC-P115C** (next-gen PRAME ImmTAC): recruiting-today platform bet, no efficacy readout of its own yet. Conditional on `prame_hla` positive.
5. **Tarlatamab plus radiation**: parallel DLL3 slot with an abscopal rationale, zero combination data. Conditional on `dll3_ihc` positive.
6. **NW-101C** (PRAME TCR-T): redundant fallback slot, no data under this name. Conditional on `prame_hla` positive.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer/access are out of scope. Guideline TKIs for relapsed osteosarcoma (regorafenib, cabozantinib) sit off the nominated antigen axis and are the treating team's standard path, not Libby recommendations here. This is decision support, not a treatment plan.
