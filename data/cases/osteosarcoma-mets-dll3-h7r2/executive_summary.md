# Executive summary

**Case:** `osteosarcoma-mets-dll3-h7r2`
**Question:** Metastatic osteosarcoma with user-reported DLL3 RNA expression — are DLL3-directed therapies a reasonable option, and what should the patient pursue if they aren't?
**Evidence base:** 5 trials, 5 clinical-evidence rows, 4 preclinical rows; board agreement ranged from full consensus on regorafenib and the DLL3 IHC workup down to one persistent dissent on the lead trial option.

## What this report covers

The PI's synthesis across two parallel ranked tracks: one assuming DLL3 protein confirmation by IHC, one assuming the IHC comes back negative. The shared first action — IHC SP347 on tumor — is recommendation rank 1 under both branches.

## Top-line findings

- DLL3 IHC is the load-bearing test. RNA expression alone does not establish membrane DLL3, and every DLL3-directed therapy in current clinical use gates on IHC. All five personas converged on this without dissent.
- In the IHC-positive branch, the lead option is tarlatamab via NCT06788938 — a basket trial that includes osteosarcoma. The conservative's earlier toxicity veto lifts on biomarker confirmation.
- The critic's dissent on the trial persists in BOTH scenarios: there are no published osteosarcoma data with tarlatamab; cross-tumor translation from SCLC is unproven and is the load-bearing scientific question this enrollment would answer.
- Regorafenib (two RCTs) is the universal-consensus backbone in both scenarios. In the IHC-negative branch it ranks first because the trial pathway is foreclosed.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** DLL3 IHC (SP347) on tumor — gates branch selection. All 5 personas endorse.

**Path A — if DLL3 IHC ≥1% (preferably ≥25%):**

1. **Tarlatamab via NCT06788938** — strong preference fit (high-risk-high-reward + prefers trials); critic dissent on cross-tumor translatability persists.
2. **Regorafenib** — off-trial backbone; SARC024 + REGOBONE evidence base; universal endorsement.
3. **Cabozantinib** — NCCN alternative; CABONE phase-2 ORR 11.9%.

**Path B — if DLL3 IHC negative or below threshold:**

1. **Regorafenib** — universal endorsement, two RCTs; the highest-utility option remaining.
2. **Cabozantinib** — sequencing alternative.
3. **Non-DLL3 trial search (mechanism-agnostic)** — considered with caveats; honors the prefers_trials preference even with the DLL3 pathway closed.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, payer/access logistics, or institution-specific trial slot availability. Those belong to the treating team.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state (endorsed / dissent / veto by persona) and 1–3 anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction lives on the live case page; this PDF is the synthesis. The two scenario tracks are parallel — the patient and clinician can plan against both branches in advance of the IHC result rather than serially.
