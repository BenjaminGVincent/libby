# Executive summary

**Case:** `mcrc-kras-a59t-pik3ca-peritoneal-pre-hipec-q8k4`
**Question:** In a patient in their 30s with MSS metastatic colon cancer carrying an atypical KRAS A59T switch-II mutation and a PIK3CA M1043I kinase-domain allele, on 1L FOLFIRI + bevacizumab with resected liver metastases and active peritoneal disease heading into cytoreductive surgery plus HIPEC, which interventions act on those two targetable features?
**Evidence base:** 40 trials, 72 clinical-evidence rows, and a preclinical set. Board agreement across the four ranked options runs from +0.60 (aspirin) down to -0.20 (pan-RAS), with no veto issued anywhere.

## What this report covers

The four feature-targeting interventions the board weighed on the PIK3CA and KRAS A59T / MSS-peritoneal axes. This PDF is the synthesis for a reviewer who has not seen the live case. The rank-by-rank narrative, the board proceedings, and the full evidence tables live on the site.

## Top-line findings

- The one place where a low-bias randomized endpoint and the patient's biomarker line up is the PIK3CA driver, not the KRAS one. Low-dose aspirin leads on that basis.
- Everything reaching KRAS A59T does so by structural read-across. No approved or investigational drug has been shown to work on this allele, and no trial has ever dosed an A59T patient.
- The sharpest board split is at rank 4: the risktaker and advocate ranked codon-agnostic pan-RAS first, while the conservative, critic, and concensusite all dissented on evidence quality and guideline fit.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. It does not resolve standard chemotherapy, the operation itself, or the population-level drugs. Those belong to the treating team and sit outside this report.
- The patient is pre-CRS-HIPEC, so aspirin's bleeding risk and the VEGF-axis agents in the pan-RAS combination arms both have to be timed around the operative window rather than started now.

## Recommendation summary

1. **Low-dose aspirin 160 mg/day (PIK3CA adjunct).** The one RoB-low randomized biomarker match: ALASCCA time-to-recurrence HR 0.49 (95% CI 0.24-0.98) in the PIK3CA subset M1043I falls into. A do-no-harm add-on, held peri-operatively. The trial studied resected disease, so the biology travels but the effect size does not transfer to active peritoneal spread. Pending somatic and clonal confirmation of M1043I.
2. **Botensilimab + balstilimab (2L+).** The highest board agreement, and the one MSS-CRC immunotherapy with a signal at the peritoneal compartment (no-liver-mets ORR ~22%, mOS 20.9 months). Held back by single-arm subgroup evidence, a ~35% grade 3+ immune-toxicity envelope, and a 2L+ timing gate after FOLFIRI-bev progression.
3. **Mutant-selective PI3K-alpha inhibition (CGT6297).** The one trial naming a CRC-and-PIK3CA cohort. Efficacy rests entirely on cross-tumor, abstract-tier data, and M1043I is a non-canonical off-hotspot allele, so it is a weaker, less reliable predictor of benefit than a canonical PIK3CA change would be. Correspondingly less certain than rank 1.
4. **Codon-agnostic pan-RAS / RAS(ON) inhibition.** The sole option touching the KRAS A59T driver, and a mechanism read-across only, not proven A59T efficacy. Trial- and referral-gated, contingent on a sponsor accepting A59T onto a codon-agnostic basket.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across later lines, and payer or access logistics. Standard chemotherapy backbones, anti-EGFR for the population, the CRS-HIPEC operation, and HER2-directed therapy act outside the two named features and are the treating team's to weigh.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state, one to three anchor citations, and the timing or foreclosure caveat that would change it. The highest-leverage single action for the KRAS axis is a sponsor eligibility call on A59T once the confirmatory NGS locks the allele. This PDF is the synthesis for a hand-off conversation; the live page is the worked-out audit trail.
