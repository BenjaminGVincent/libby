# Executive summary

**Case:** `hgsoc-tuboovarian-stage3c-r0-hrd-pending-idyq`
**Question:** Which maintenance interventions fit an R0-resected stage IIIc high-grade serous tubo-ovarian carcinoma now that tumor NGS confirms a BRCA-wild-type but genomically-unstable, ER-positive/PR-negative, HER2-low (IHC 1+) tumor, across the homologous-recombination, angiogenesis, endocrine, on-study IL-12, DDR, and HER2-low axes?
**Evidence base:** 26 screened trials, 21 clinical-evidence rows, 17 preclinical rows; twelve ranked interventions, agreement scores running 0.8 down to -0.4 for the board-voted rows (disitamab vedotin at -2), with the four post-board additions carried at a neutral 0 and no single lead the board converged on.

## What this report covers

The PI ranked eleven therapeutic options plus a board-vetoed twelfth, each tied to its registration evidence. Ranks 1-7 carry the board's agreement state; ranks 8-11 were surfaced by a post-board re-screen and were not voted on. This PDF is that synthesis, for a reader who has not seen the live case page.

## Top-line findings

- Every biomarker has resulted. The tumor is BRCA-wild-type on both germline and somatic testing but genomically unstable on a qualitative Altera call, which is not a validated companion-diagnostic HRD-positive result.
- Rucaparib (ATHENA-MONO intent-to-treat mPFS 20.2 vs 9.2 months, HR 0.52) takes rank 1: a replicated all-comer phase 3 that qualifies a BRCA-wild-type tumor with no GIS gate. Its grade 3+ anemia (28.7%) presses the patient's myelosuppression veto, so it is an evidence near-tie with niraparib, not a toxicity escape.
- Four options at ranks 8-11 (T-DXd + olaparib, azenosertib, ART0380, abemaciclib + AI) entered by a post-board re-screen. They carry no persona endorsement, dissent, or veto and are ranked on evidence alone; their data are conference-level or have no readout, so read them as relapse-context leads for the treating team, not board-vetted picks.
- The HER2 axis is investigational and its ADCs press the marrow. T-DXd (rank 7) and the post-board T-DXd + olaparib combination (rank 8) both rest on HER2-low breast extrapolation; IHC 1+ is a weaker, less reliable predictor of ADC benefit than IHC 3+. T-DXd carries a 10-12% ILD rate that has been fatal, and the olaparib combination stacks PARP marrow toxicity on top. Disitamab vedotin (rank 12) is rejected by all five personas because its MMAE payload triggers both named vetoes head-on.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. Two workups remain: a MyChoice CDx GIS at or above 42 promotes olaparib + bevacizumab (below 42 it is foreclosed), and a HER2 IHC re-stain hardens the HER2 options (a HER2 0 result forecloses them). PARP deliverability at this patient's marrow reserve (baseline WBC 3.5) is unproven until a hematology dose plan is documented.

## Recommendation summary

1. **Rucaparib (1L maintenance).** Replicated all-comer PARP; no veto, no dissent, but grade 3+ anemia sits on the named veto.
2. **Niraparib (1L maintenance).** Cleanest guideline fit with the only tumor-specific pharmacology; conditional conservative veto and advocate dissent on myelosuppression.
3. **Bevacizumab (continued maintenance).** The only option clearing both vetoes on a backbone she already tolerates, but PFS-only with a live VTE/bleeding caution.
4. **GEN-1 / IMNN-001 (IL-12 immunogene).** On-study continuation with a real OS signal (HR 0.69), but sponsor-topline-immature and intraperitoneal.
5. **Letrozole (endocrine maintenance, MATAO-enrollable).** Lowest-toxicity oral option clearing every veto, now enrollable on the randomized MATAO phase 3 rather than resting on a retrospective series; PR-negativity and PARAGON's non-predictive ER histoscore temper the expected magnitude.
6. **Olaparib + bevacizumab (PAOLA-1).** Highest magnitude if HRD-positive, but contingent on a GIS she lacks and held by a toxicity veto plus a subgroup-only evidence dissent.
7. **Trastuzumab deruxtecan (HER2-low IHC 1+, investigational).** Clears both vetoes but rests on breast-only extrapolation with a fatal-tail ILD risk; a relapse option after a confirmatory re-stain, not approved standard care at IHC 1+ ovarian.
8. **Trastuzumab deruxtecan + olaparib (HER2-low, post-board).** Enrollable HER2-low + PARP relapse combination on a non-peer-reviewed phase 1 signal; stacked marrow toxicity presses the veto, IHC 1+ is a weak predictor.
9. **Azenosertib (WEE1, post-board).** Later-line DDR option on-axis with the genomic instability, but gated on an untested cyclin E1 marker, backed by conference-only data, and heavily myelosuppressive.
10. **ART0380 (ATR, post-board).** Second DDR option, thinner still: no ovarian efficacy estimand and conference-only data.
11. **Abemaciclib + aromatase inhibitor (CDK4/6 + AI, post-board).** Speculative oral ER/CDK combination adjacent to letrozole; the ovarian CDK4/6 + AI signal is weak (ORR 5%) and driven by low-grade, not high-grade, serous.

Not recommended: disitamab vedotin, vetoed by all five personas on the peripheral-neuropathy and myelosuppression vetoes its MMAE payload triggers.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, cytopenia-monitoring schedules, sequencing across later lines, and payer or access logistics are out of scope. This is decision support for the maintenance question, not a treatment plan.
