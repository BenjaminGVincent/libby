# Executive summary

**Case:** `hgsoc-tuboovarian-stage3c-r0-hrd-pending-idyq`
**Question:** Which maintenance interventions fit an R0-resected stage IIIc high-grade serous tubo-ovarian carcinoma now that tumor NGS confirms a BRCA-wild-type but genomically-unstable, ER-positive, HER2-low (IHC 1+) tumor, across the homologous-recombination, angiogenesis, endocrine, on-study IL-12, and HER2-low axes?
**Evidence base:** 21 screened trials, 14 clinical-evidence rows, 17 preclinical rows; seven ranked interventions plus a board-vetoed eighth, agreement scores running 0.8 down to -0.4 (disitamab vedotin at -2), with no single lead the board converged on.

## What this report covers

The PI ranked seven maintenance options and kept an eighth as a not-recommended row, each tied to its registration evidence and the board's agreement state. This PDF is that synthesis, for a reader who has not seen the live case page.

## Top-line findings

- Every biomarker has resulted. The tumor is BRCA-wild-type on both germline and somatic testing but genomically unstable on a qualitative Altera call, which is not a validated companion-diagnostic HRD-positive result.
- Rucaparib (ATHENA-MONO intent-to-treat mPFS 20.2 vs 9.2 months, HR 0.52) takes rank 1: a replicated all-comer phase 3 that qualifies a BRCA-wild-type tumor with no GIS gate. Its grade 3+ anemia (28.7%) presses the patient's myelosuppression veto, so it is an evidence near-tie with niraparib, not a toxicity escape.
- The objections sit at the bottom. Olaparib + bevacizumab (rank 6) carries a conservative toxicity veto plus critic and advocate dissents; disitamab vedotin (rank 8) is rejected by all five personas because its MMAE payload triggers both named vetoes, peripheral neuropathy and myelosuppression, head-on.
- The HER2 axis is investigational. Trastuzumab deruxtecan (rank 7) has no ovarian IHC 1+ data, and the tumor-agnostic approval needs IHC 3+, so it rests on HER2-low breast extrapolation. It is a relapse consideration after a confirmatory HER2 re-stain, not a maintenance pick.
- Two workups remain, neither moving the lead. A MyChoice CDx GIS at or above 42 promotes olaparib + bevacizumab (below 42 it is foreclosed); a HER2 IHC re-stain hardens the T-DXd option (a HER2 0 result forecloses it). PARP deliverability at this patient's marrow reserve (baseline WBC 3.5) is unproven until a hematology dose plan is documented.

## Recommendation summary

1. **Rucaparib (1L maintenance).** Replicated all-comer PARP; no veto, no dissent, but grade 3+ anemia sits on the named veto.
2. **Niraparib (1L maintenance).** Cleanest guideline fit with the only tumor-specific pharmacology; conditional conservative veto and advocate dissent on myelosuppression.
3. **Bevacizumab (continued maintenance).** The only option clearing both vetoes on a backbone she already tolerates, but PFS-only with a live VTE/bleeding caution.
4. **GEN-1 / IMNN-001 (IL-12 immunogene).** On-study continuation with a real OS signal (HR 0.69), but sponsor-topline-immature and intraperitoneal.
5. **Letrozole (endocrine maintenance).** Lowest-toxicity oral option clearing every veto; evidence is a single ROBINS-I-serious retrospective series.
6. **Olaparib + bevacizumab (PAOLA-1).** Highest magnitude if HRD-positive, but contingent on a GIS she lacks and held by a toxicity veto plus a subgroup-only evidence dissent.
7. **Trastuzumab deruxtecan (HER2-low IHC 1+, investigational).** Clears both vetoes but rests on breast-only extrapolation; a relapse option after a confirmatory re-stain, not approved standard care at IHC 1+ ovarian.

Not recommended: disitamab vedotin, vetoed by all five personas on the peripheral-neuropathy and myelosuppression vetoes its MMAE payload triggers.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, cytopenia-monitoring schedules, sequencing across later lines, and payer or access logistics are out of scope. This is decision support for the maintenance question, not a treatment plan.
