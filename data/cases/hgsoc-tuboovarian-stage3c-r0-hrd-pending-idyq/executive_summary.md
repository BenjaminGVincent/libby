# Executive summary

**Case:** `hgsoc-tuboovarian-stage3c-r0-hrd-pending-idyq`
**Question:** In newly-diagnosed, platinum-responsive, R0-resected Stage IIIc high-grade serous tubo-ovarian carcinoma with wild-type germline BRCA, which maintenance options target homologous-recombination status, the VEGF axis, and ER expression, gated on a pending tumor HRD / somatic-BRCA assay?
**Evidence base:** Twelve trial / evidence rows (four anchoring 1L-maintenance phase-3 RCTs plus class comparators), nine clinical-evidence rows, and nine preclinical rows; agreement scores run from 1.0 on the workup to -0.2 on GEN-1, with all five personas converging on the workup and the board splitting on the lead therapeutic.

## What this report covers

The PI ranked seven options across three of the patient's stated targetable features (homologous-recombination status, the VEGF axis, and ER expression) and a pending biomarker that gates the highest-magnitude picks. This report packages that synthesis for an external reviewer.

## Top-line findings

- The pending tumor HRD genomic-instability score and somatic BRCA1/2 call is the load-bearing input. It runs on archival FFPE with a two to four week turnaround, and it decides whether the largest-magnitude PARP options are reachable.
- niraparib leads the therapeutic options because it is the one PARP that does not wait on the HRD score (PRIMA all-comers PFS HR 0.62). Its cytopenia profile sits on her severe-myelosuppression veto, so the weight-and-platelet individualized starting dose travels with the recommendation.
- The conservative vetoed olaparib + bevacizumab on compounded bleeding / VTE risk in a patient with a prior IVC filter. That veto lifts only in the HRD-positive branch with a documented monitoring plan, and the critic's veto on GEN-1 stands on evidence quality.
- The ranking is scoped to the patient's targetable features. If the HRD score is negative and somatic BRCA is wild-type, the two conditional PARP rows drop out and the within-scope options narrow to niraparib, bevacizumab, and letrozole. Standard care for the indication beyond those features is a separate care-team conversation this report does not cover.
- The HRD-conditional survival figures rest on pre-specified subgroups, not confirmed ITT results: the PAOLA-1 5-year OS gain comes from the HRD-positive subgroup, and the ITT OS was not significant.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Order the tumor HRD genomic-instability score plus somatic BRCA1/2 on archival FFPE before committing the maintenance strategy.

1. **niraparib**: the PARP that does not wait on the score; cytopenia profile requires the individualized starting dose.
2. **bevacizumab**: lowest-regret interim that clears both vetoes; modest PFS-only magnitude, no ITT OS gain.
3. **olaparib + bevacizumab**: the largest effect on the board. Conditional on `hrd_status` positive, foreclosed if the test is negative; needs a documented bleeding/VTE and CBC plan.
4. **olaparib monotherapy**: highest-quality evidence and the only mature OS readout. Conditional on a somatic BRCA1/2 mutation, foreclosed if the somatic call is wild-type.
5. **letrozole**: targets her ER-positive biology with the lowest toxicity, on tier-4 cohort evidence in a PR-negative tumor.

## What this report does *not* cover

It does not set doses, monitoring schedules, or sequencing across lines, and it does not address payer or access logistics. The frame is decision support, not a treatment order.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state by persona and one to three anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction lives on the live case page; this summary is the synthesis.
