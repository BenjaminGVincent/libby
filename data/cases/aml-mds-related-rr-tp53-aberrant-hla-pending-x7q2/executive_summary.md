# Executive summary

**Case:** `aml-mds-related-rr-tp53-aberrant-hla-pending-x7q2`
**Question:** In relapsed/refractory MDS-related AML at ~85% marrow blasts, six years after a matched-sibling allo-HCT and now refractory to FLAG-IDA plus venetoclax, which interventions could reach the HLA-restricted minor-antigen axis (HA-1/HA-2, WT1), the CD33/CD123 antigens, a possible TP53 Y220C handle, and a second-transplant platform?
**Evidence base:** 26 screened trials (CBX-250 added on this re-run), 17 clinical-evidence rows (14 included), and 16 preclinical rows across six feature axes; board agreement ran from unanimous (1.0) on the workup and the second transplant down to -0.2 on rezatapopt, with one threshold-conditional CRS veto on CBX-250 that the ranking overrides.

## What this report covers

The PI synthesized twelve ranked recommendations into one curative-intent arc. This PDF packages that ranking, the biomarker gates it hangs on, and the board's dissents and standing veto for a reader who has not seen the live site.

## Top-line findings

- The rank-1 action is a diagnostic gate, not a drug: HLA-A*02:01/A*24:02 typing, HA-1/HA-2 genotyping of both the patient and the sibling donor, resolution of the TP53/Y220C discrepancy, and STR chimerism on sorted CD34+ blasts. Everything the case was submitted for hangs on these, and the board placed its entire ranking behind them unanimously.
- The curative backbone is a second allogeneic transplant (rank 2), deliverable only after the 85%-blast marrow is cytoreduced and a fitness workup that has not been done. Iomab-B conditioning (rank 3) is the single RCT-grade way to carry a high-blast marrow into that transplant, but the FDA judged the SIERRA package insufficient for a BLA in August 2024 and asked for a fresh non-crossover study, so it stays unapproved with an unsettled regulatory path.
- The newly added CBX-250 (rank 11) carries a standing conditional CRS veto from the board. It is an off-the-shelf CG1/HLA-A*02:01 T-cell engager with no manufacturing wait and prior transplant permitted, but it is first-in-human with no efficacy readout, and the veto lifts only on a documented CRS-mitigation plan: cytoreduce the 85%-blast burden first, step-up dosing, tocilizumab on hand.
- The ranking is scoped to the patient's targetable features. A negative HLA-A*02:01 forecloses all three A*02:01-restricted levers (HA-1/HA-2 TCR-T, WT1 TCR-T, CBX-250), after which standard salvage care for the leukemia is a separate care-team conversation this report does not cover.
- TP53 is held aberrant pending confirmation and not collapsed; fitness and organ-function data are absent, and the efficacy-leaning 0.75 weight is flagged for revisiting once they exist. Guidelines referenced are NCCN AML v3.2026 and ELN 2022.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Rank 1, the confirmatory diagnostic gate that opens or forecloses every downstream option.

1. **Second allo-HCT**. Curative backbone with a real cure fraction, deliverable only after cytoreduction.
2. **Iomab-B conditioning**. The RCT-grade enabler for a high-blast marrow; unapproved after the FDA declined the SIERRA package, prior-transplant eligibility unverified.
3. **Gemtuzumab ozogamicin**. The one on-label CD33 debulk a team can simply write; time its VOD risk around the transplant.
4. **Sibling DLI**. A readily available adjunct, modest in florid disease; belongs after cytoreduction.
5. **Pivekimab sunirine**. A CD123 ADC debulk whose boxed VOD warning works against the transplant it bridges to.
6. **HA-1/HA-2 TCR-T**. Highest ceiling, thinnest evidence (n=9 feasibility). Conditional on `hla_a0201_ha` positive; foreclosed if the type or the mismatch is negative.
7. **FLAMSA-RIC**. The chemo-based conditioning alternative to Iomab-B for the same transplant.
8. **Dual CD33/CD123 CAR-T**. Antigen-escape hedge held back by xenograft-only evidence and China-only access.
9. **WT1 TCR-T**. Keeps the axis open if HA-1/HA-2 does not mismatch. Conditional on `hla_a0201` positive; prophylaxis evidence, not salvage.
10. **CBX-250**. Off-the-shelf and fastest to move once typing confirms, but first-in-human with no efficacy and gated behind a cytoreduction-first CRS-mitigation plan. Conditional on `hla_a0201` positive; foreclosed if the type is negative.
11. **Rezatapopt**. A Y220C longshot off the stated axis. Conditional on `tp53_y220c` positive; cross-tumor data only.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer or access mechanics are all out of scope, as is standard salvage chemotherapy that does not act on one of these handles.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state (endorsed, dissent, considered-with-caveats, or the CBX-250 conditional veto) and one to three anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction and per-persona rationale live on the case page; this PDF is the synthesis.
