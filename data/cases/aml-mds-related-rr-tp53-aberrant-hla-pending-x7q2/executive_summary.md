# Executive summary

**Case:** `aml-mds-related-rr-tp53-aberrant-hla-pending-x7q2`
**Question:** In relapsed/refractory MDS-related AML at ~85% marrow blasts, six years after a matched-sibling allo-HCT and now refractory to FLAG-IDA plus venetoclax, which interventions could reach the HLA-restricted minor-antigen axis (HA-1/HA-2, WT1), the CD33/CD123 antigens, a possible TP53 Y220C handle, and a second-transplant platform?
**Evidence base:** 25 screened trials, 16 clinical-evidence rows (13 included), and 15 preclinical rows across six feature axes; board agreement ran from unanimous (1.0) on the workup, the second transplant, and Iomab-B conditioning down to -0.2 on the WT1 and rezatapopt longshots, with no formal vetoes.

## What this report covers

The PI synthesized eleven ranked recommendations into one curative-intent arc. This PDF packages that ranking, the biomarker gates it hangs on, and the board's dissents for a reader who has not seen the live site.

## Top-line findings

- The rank-1 action is a diagnostic gate, not a drug. HLA-A*02:01/A*24:02 typing, HA-1/HA-2 genotyping of both the patient and the sibling donor, resolution of the TP53/Y220C discrepancy, and STR chimerism on sorted CD34+ blasts. Everything the case was submitted for hangs on these, and the board placed its entire ranking behind them unanimously.
- The curative backbone is a second allogeneic transplant (rank 2), deliverable only after the 85%-blast marrow is cytoreduced and a fitness workup that has not been done. Iomab-B conditioning (rank 3) is the single RCT-grade way to carry a high-blast marrow into that transplant.
- No veto was issued. The load-bearing tension is HA-1/HA-2 TCR-T (rank 6), the axis the run was built around: the critic and conservative dissent on thin evidence (n=9 feasibility) and undocumented fitness, so it is carried with caveats even in the positive branch.
- The ranking is scoped to the patient's targetable features. A negative HLA-A*02:01 forecloses both TCR-T levers, after which standard salvage care for the leukemia is a separate care-team conversation this report does not cover.
- Fitness and organ-function data are absent; the efficacy-leaning 0.75 weight is flagged for revisiting once they exist.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Rank 1, the confirmatory diagnostic gate that opens or forecloses every downstream option.

1. **Second allo-HCT**. Curative backbone with a real cure fraction, deliverable only after cytoreduction.
2. **Iomab-B conditioning**. The RCT-grade enabler for a high-blast marrow; prior-transplant eligibility unverified.
3. **Gemtuzumab ozogamicin**. The one on-label CD33 debulk a team can simply write; time its VOD risk around the transplant.
4. **Sibling DLI**. A readily available adjunct, modest in florid disease; belongs after cytoreduction.
5. **HA-1/HA-2 TCR-T**. Highest ceiling, thinnest evidence. Conditional on `hla_a0201_ha` positive; foreclosed if the type or the mismatch is negative.
6. **FLAMSA-RIC**. The chemo-based conditioning alternative to Iomab-B for the same transplant.
7. **Dual CD33/CD123 CAR-T**. Antigen-escape hedge held back by xenograft-only evidence and China-only access.
8. **Pivekimab sunirine**. A CD123 ADC debulk whose boxed VOD warning works against the transplant it bridges to.
9. **WT1 TCR-T**. Keeps the axis open if HA-1/HA-2 does not mismatch. Conditional on `hla_a0201` positive; prophylaxis evidence, not salvage.
10. **Rezatapopt**. A Y220C longshot off the stated axis. Conditional on `tp53_y220c` positive; cross-tumor data only.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer or access mechanics are all out of scope, as is standard salvage chemotherapy that does not act on one of these handles.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state (endorsed, dissent, or considered-with-caveats by persona) and one to three anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction and per-persona rationale live on the case page; this PDF is the synthesis.
