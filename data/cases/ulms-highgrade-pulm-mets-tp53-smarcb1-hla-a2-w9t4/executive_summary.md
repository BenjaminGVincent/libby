# Executive summary

**Case:** `ulms-highgrade-pulm-mets-tp53-smarcb1-hla-a2-w9t4`
**Question:** In a woman in her thirties with systemic-therapy-naive metastatic high-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma, bilateral infiltrative pulmonary metastases, and doxorubicin + trabectedin (the LMS-04 regimen) planned but not started, what could treat her: approved and guideline-carried care on one table, and trial-only, off-label or not-yet-accessible options aimed at the hypothesised SMARCB1/INI1 deficiency, the confirmed HLA-A\*02:01 allele and the largely unmeasured molecular landscape on the other?
**Evidence base:** 55 trial rows, 41 clinical-evidence rows, 53 preclinical rows, 25 target-validation rows, 15 biomarker-survey rows, 64 accessibility rows and a 16-option standard-of-care screen; a 5-persona board over two rounds with 20 cross-critiques; agreement from 1.0 (the workup) down to -0.4 (ivonescimab); no formal veto in either round.

## What this report covers

Two co-equal tables, split by regulatory maturity, each ranked from 1 within itself. The Standard-of-care table holds 16 approved or guideline-carried options and is where the first-line decision lives. The Experimental table holds 45 trial-only, off-label and not-yet-available rows and opens with a set of tests rather than a drug. The two number 1s are not rivals: the plan asks for the top of both in the same week, one a treatment and one a requisition that runs beside it. Every rank quoted below names its table, because reading the two lists as one produces a contradiction that does not exist.

## Top-line findings

- **The case's most important therapy sits on the Standard-of-care table, not the Experimental one.** Doxorubicin + trabectedin with trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04) leads that table; it was the board's first choice on four of five slates and appeared on all five, and it landed there by regulatory maturity, not demotion. Its randomised phase 3 gives the only survival result anywhere in this case: PFS HR 0.41 (median 12.2 vs 6.2 months) and final OS HR 0.65 (median 33 vs 24 months) against doxorubicin alone. Two qualifiers travel with those numbers. Uterine disease was a 45% stratification stratum and no uterine-only effect size has been published. And the cost, 80% grade 3-4 neutropenia and 28% febrile neutropenia, has never been put to her.
- **Experimental rank 1 is a diagnostic gate, not a therapy**: one block-release request naming the 2024 uterine specimen and the 2026 lung core, plus a scored ECOG, an echocardiogram, labs and altitude-referenced oximetry, all running alongside first-line treatment. It leads because 12 of the 15 surveyed biomarkers were never measured. The placement also settles the board's sharpest structural dispute: the advocate's measurement-first instinct leads the table, and the critic's objection that a measurement is non-comparable with therapies is answered because the workup slot never competes with a drug for a seat.
- **The new molecular findings are mostly negative results, and that is the honest headline.** SMARCB1 G344Afs\*13 is a plasma-only, heterozygous call in a histology where the one prevalence series carried here found 170 of 170 uterine smooth-muscle tumours retaining SMARCB1. Even a confirmed loss opens little: the EZH2 route's cross-histology basket bought a 9% response with no uLMS patient, the live valemetostat protocol demands biallelic homozygous deletion in France only, both tazemetostat expanded-access programs are listed as no longer available, and both BRD9 degrader programs were discontinued without successors. TP53 Q52\* is likewise plasma-only, with an explicit clonal-haematopoiesis caveat and no paired white-cell sequencing; if it proves blood-derived, the SMARCB1 call's credibility falls with it.
- **HLA-A\*02:01 is confirmed, and it is half a gate.** MAGE-A4, PRAME and NY-ESO-1 expression are all unmeasured, IMA203 screens on the sponsor's own assay rather than a local stain, and afamitresgene autoleucel is labelled for synovial sarcoma only. The allele opens a conversation, not a therapy.
- **The four-persona ivonescimab refusal carries the critic's convergence verdict.** Four of five refused the seat off the same three papers, so the unanimity is one evidence base read four times and adds no extra weight. The refusal stands anyway, because GOG-0250, SARC028 and the uLMS futility stop are mutually independent, and one of them is a randomised null in this exact disease.
- **Three decisions belong before cycle 1, not before cycle 6.** An off-protocol gemcitabine prescription permanently closes the abemaciclib seat on NCT06498648, which LMS-04 (no gemcitabine) preserves. In-vivo lung perfusion excludes above 450 mg cumulative doxorubicin. The peposertib protocol caps at 360 mg/m², exactly six LMS-04 cycles at 60 mg/m². A five-cycle plan is a choice made up front; discovered at cycle six, it costs two doors.
- **One Rb stain routes her between two MD Anderson trials** (intact Rb to NCT06498648 now; Rb loss to the REC-617 study after a first line), and one block-release request to the 2024 outside institution, the one that signed the primary out benign, unlocks nearly every pending test. Nobody clinical currently owns that request.
- **ECOG 1 was assumed at intake and has never been scored.** Every effect estimate quoted anywhere in this case comes from a trial that enrolled ECOG 0-1, so the unmeasured score is an applicability defect inside the numbers, not paperwork. The roughly 23-month interval from a resection signed out benign to the correct diagnosis makes the disease's two-year tempo visible, and her residence at about 8,500 ft belongs on every oximetry report a trial screener will read.

Three disagreements stayed live, preserved rather than resolved: whether a measurement belongs in a therapeutic ranking at all, which the workup slot accommodates without settling; whether preferences can carry weight when every value in the file is an intake default, with the advocate reading the empty veto list as unasked rather than permissive; and whether the board converged correctly or too cautiously on locoregional therapy, where the risktaker holds that the resectability call belongs to a thoracic surgeon reading her films, not to a board table.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** the Experimental table's rank-1 diagnostic gate (agreement 1.0, all five personas): one block release carrying INI1, Rb, mismatch-repair, B7-H3 and antigen stains, tissue NGS with HRD/HRR and TMB, plus the clinic half, a scored ECOG, echocardiogram, labs and altitude-referenced oximetry. Ordered alongside, never instead of, first-line treatment.

**Standard-of-care table, ranked 1 to 16:** LMS-04 (1); gemcitabine + docetaxel (2); doxorubicin + ifosfamide (3); single-agent doxorubicin (4); trabectedin after anthracycline (5); pulmonary metastasectomy after systemic control (6); lung SBRT (7); pazopanib (8); gemcitabine + dacarbazine (9); eribulin (10); temozolomide (11); then the gated tumour-agnostic routes, pembrolizumab on a TMB or MSI result nobody has produced (12), dostarlimab on dMMR (13) and larotrectinib on an NTRK fusion never tested on tissue (14); gemcitabine/docetaxel + bevacizumab, set aside on the GOG-0250 null (15); and aromatase inhibition, set aside on ER/PR under 1% (16).

**Experimental table, ranked 1 to 45:** behind the workup gate come olaparib + temozolomide (2, agreement 0.8): ORR 27% and median PFS 6.9 months in her exact histology, reachable post-induction as an off-label prescription whose authorisation case the tissue HRD result would carry, at the cost of a second consecutive severely myelosuppressive regimen; abemaciclib + gemcitabine/docetaxel on NCT06498648 (3, agreement 0.8): Rb-intact required, no efficacy data for the add-on, and the only trial seat whose floor inside the trial is guideline chemotherapy; IMA203 PRAME TCR-T (4, agreement 0.0): a free sponsor screening conversation now against a cross-tumour phase 1 with no uLMS patient, the critic in dissent; in-vivo lung perfusion + metastasectomy (5, agreement 0.0): the one cure-aimed option, whose anatomic premise her infiltrative bilateral pattern may defeat, the conservative in dissent; and ivonescimab (6, agreement -0.4): the only seat reachable before cycle 1 by explicit protocol language, refused by four of five. Ranks 7 through 45 carry flags rather than live seats: sixteen not-enrollable, twelve thin-evidence, four unavailable, three consolidated, and four assessed and not recommended on the evidence, including the checkpoint-blockade record in this histology and an ATR/ALT hypothesis already tested in her disease and lost. A retained INI1 stain forecloses the seven SWI/SNF-conditional rows (Experimental ranks 7, 24 through 26 and 40 through 42) and touches nothing else on either table.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across later lines beyond the named foreclosures, and payer or access mechanics beyond the access labels the Experimental rows carry. If both tables' options exhaust, the standard-care conversation from there belongs to her treating team. This is decision support for that team, not a treatment plan.

## How to use this report

Read the Standard-of-care table first; her next decision sits there, LMS-04 at its head with numbers she has not yet been shown. The Experimental table ranks what a trial seat, an off-label route or a future programme could add, and its access column keeps those routes distinct. Sixty seconds here should leave you holding four things: the whole board backs the planned chemotherapy and the induction's toxicity conversation is owed before consent; a requisition leads the experimental list because almost nothing has been measured; the striking-looking plasma findings are probably red herrings until tissue says otherwise; and three cheap decisions made before cycle 1 keep doors open that cycle 6 would quietly close.
