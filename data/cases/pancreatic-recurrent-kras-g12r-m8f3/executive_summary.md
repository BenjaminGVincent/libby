# Executive summary

**Case:** `pancreatic-recurrent-kras-g12r-m8f3`
**Question:** In recurrent pancreatic adenocarcinoma after adjuvant FOLFIRI, what interventions can target KRAS G12R, CDKN2A loss, and CCND3 alteration in an MSS / sub-threshold-TMB background?
**Evidence base:** 26 trials, 16 clinical-evidence rows (11 included; 5 SoC chemo rows logged `considered_excluded` per the targetable-feature scope rule), 22 preclinical rows, 12 target-validation rows. Eleven ranked recommendations spanning agreement scores 1.0 (rank 1 workup; rank 2 daraxonrasib monotherapy) to -0.2 (rank 7 RMC-7977 + ivonescimab). All five personas converged on daraxonrasib monotherapy as the lead therapeutic option.

## What this report covers

The PI synthesized eleven ranked options for a confirmed-biomarker case: pan-RAS / RAS(ON) class on KRAS G12R, PRMT5 synthetic lethality on CDKN2A / MTAP, gated PARP maintenance, and CDK4/6-axis context. Standard-of-care chemotherapy for recurrent PDAC is out of scope.

## Top-line findings

- Daraxonrasib monotherapy on NCT05379985 is the unanimous lead. Wolpin NEJM 2026 (PMID 42090791) reports ORR 35%, mPFS 8.5 mo, mOS 13.1 mo in the 2L RAS G12 PDAC subgroup at 300 mg (n=26); G12R sits inside the pooled G12X estimand. The critic's evidence caveat persists: no allele-resolved G12R subgroup, RASolute-302 phase 3 OS readout pending.
- Even with biomarkers confirmed, sponsor screening re-tests KRAS G12R on plasma. Rank 1 is the orthogonal NGS + ctDNA workup, framed as the screening-fail hedge plus a baseline VAF anchor, not a binary gate.
- Two lower-rank rows carry load-bearing dissents. Daraxonrasib + chemo on NCT06445062 (rank 6) is the advocate / risktaker combination lane against conservative and concensusite dissent; RMC-7977 + ivonescimab (rank 7) draws three dissents on evidence quality, toxicity, and guideline fit.
- Open question: germline BRCA1 / BRCA2 / PALB2 status is not on file. A positive result moves olaparib (rank 5) and rucaparib (rank 10) from off-table to decision-relevant after a successful platinum induction.

## Recommendation summary

1. **KRAS G12R orthogonal NGS + ctDNA confirmation.** Sponsor re-test at screening and the on-treatment monitoring anchor. Runs in parallel with germline panel, MMR IHC, p16 IHC, and MTAP reflex.
2. **daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) 300 mg PO daily on NCT05379985.** Only published on-axis effect size in this allele; phase 3 confirmation pending.
3. **PF-07934040 pan-KRAS on NCT06447662.** Backup pan-KRAS class with G12R explicit in eligibility; no efficacy readout yet.
4. **anvumetostat + daraxonrasib on NCT06360354.** Two-feature regimen on KRAS plus CDKN2A / MTAP, gated on MTAP co-deletion reflex.
5. **olaparib maintenance after platinum induction.** Only RCT-grade option in the dossier; two locked gates (germline BRCA / PALB2; platinum induction). Status `considered_with_caveats`.
6. **daraxonrasib + chemo on NCT06445062.** Higher-ceiling combination lane on an oxaliplatin-naive backbone; PDAC combination-safety data not yet published. Status `considered_with_caveats`.
7. **zoldonrasib (RMC-7977) + ivonescimab on NCT07397338.** Highest preclinical ceiling on the immune axis; zero published PDAC clinical data. Status `considered_with_caveats`.

Ranks 8 to 11 (ELI-002 7P vaccine, RAMP-205, rucaparib, palbociclib combinations) sit on the page as informational context with explicit caveats.
