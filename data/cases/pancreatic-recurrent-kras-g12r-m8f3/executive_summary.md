# Executive summary

**Case:** `pancreatic-recurrent-kras-g12r-m8f3`
**Question:** In recurrent KRAS G12R pancreatic adenocarcinoma after adjuvant FOLFIRI (ECOG 1, MSS, TMB 4.1, with CDKN2A loss and CCND3 co-alteration), what can target KRAS G12R or the CDKN2A/CCND3 cell-cycle axis?
**Evidence base:** 32 trial rows, 19 included clinical-evidence rows, 22 preclinical rows. Eleven ranked interventions, board-agreement scores 1.0 down to 0.2. All five personas placed daraxonrasib at rank 1, the only unanimous pick; below it the board splits on maturity, deliverability, and guideline order.

## What this report covers

The PI ranked eleven molecular-feature options: the pan-RAS / RAS(ON) class on KRAS G12R, pan-KRAS ON/OFF trials, HRD-gated PARP maintenance, an ERK+CDK4/6 combination for the CDKN2A/CCND3 axis, MTAP-gated PRMT5 inhibition, and a G12R vaccine. Standard chemotherapy for recurrent PDAC is out of scope.

## Top-line findings

- This genotype is MSS with sub-threshold TMB, so immunotherapy is off the table and nearly every actionable option is investigational. Order the germline BRCA1/2/PALB2 panel now; NCCN recommends it for every PDAC patient.
- Daraxonrasib monotherapy is the rank-1 lead and the only peer-reviewed, indication-matched efficacy on the KRAS axis: ORR 35%, mPFS 8.5 mo, mOS 13.1 mo in the 2L RAS G12 PDAC subgroup at 300 mg (n=26; Wolpin NEJM 2026). G12R is folded into a pooled G12 estimand, never read on its own.
- The RASolute 302 phase-3 survival figures (mOS 13.2 vs 6.7 mo, HR 0.40) and the FDA Expanded Access Program are company/FDA-disclosed context, not peer-reviewed or in the dossier. The rank-1 weight does not rest on them, and no PMID was minted for them.
- Sharpest dissent: the conservative left the rank-2 ERAS-0015 glue off entirely because its grade-resolved safety table is undisclosed (a single April 2026 press release), while the advocate and risktaker ranked it second on a 40-42% ORR.
- If the germline panel returns negative, the PARP axis closes; a negative or retained MTAP closes the PRMT5 axis. A negative workup does not name a within-scope option, and standard 2L care becomes the treating team's conversation.

## Recommendation summary

1. **daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) monotherapy.** Unanimous rank 1; the only peer-reviewed on-axis efficacy, with G12R inside a pooled G12 estimand.
2. **JYP0015 / ERAS-0015 (pan-RAS glue).** A second pan-RAS shot with a striking 40-42% ORR, but press-release-only with no grade-3+ safety table; the conservative dissents.
3. **olaparib maintenance (POLO).** The only guideline-listed drug (category 2A), double-gated on a germline BRCA1/2 hit not on file and a platinum induction the patient has never had, so it cannot start today.
4. **daraxonrasib + chemo (RMC-GI-102).** Deeper-response ceiling for this oxaliplatin-naive patient, at the cost of uncharacterized combination toxicity.
5. **ulixertinib + palbociclib.** The only regimen covering KRAS, CDKN2A, and CCND3 together; best published PDAC signal is stable disease, not response.
6. **KST-6051 / FALCON (pan-KRAS ON/OFF).** Pre-data axis optionality; first patient dosed April 2026.
7. **PF-07934040 (pan-KRAS).** Names G12R in written eligibility; no efficacy readout.
8. **JAB-23E73 (pan-KRAS ON/OFF).** Axis redundancy; all-comer basket, qualitative activity only.
9. **rucaparib maintenance.** Widens the PARP net to somatic BRCA and PALB2; same germline-plus-platinum gate, phase 2 evidence.
10. **anvumetostat (PRMT5i).** Synthetic-lethal for CDKN2A loss, entirely gated on an unrun MTAP reflex test.
11. **ELI-002 (G12R vaccine).** Cleanest allele-matched mechanism and safety, foreclosed by an MRD/adjuvant enrollment window this overt-recurrent patient sits outside.

## What this report does *not* cover

Standard chemotherapy for recurrent PDAC, dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer or access logistics are outside scope. Libby ranks targetable-feature options; the treating team owns the rest.
