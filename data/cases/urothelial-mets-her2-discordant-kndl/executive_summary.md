# Executive summary

**Case:** `urothelial-mets-her2-discordant-kndl`
**Question:** In metastatic urothelial carcinoma progressing on pembrolizumab maintenance after a 25-month first-line complete response, what investigational options target HER2, Nectin-4, TROP2, TP53 R175H with HLA-A\*02:01, FGFR3, or the mesothelin, MUC1, PRAME, FAP and ARID1A research leads, once the discordant HER2 result is reconciled?
**Evidence base:** 40 trials, 48 clinical and 41 preclinical evidence rows, 24 target-validation rows, 23 surveyed biomarkers, 12 standard-of-care rows, 5 board positions and 20 cross-critiques. Agreement runs from 1.0 down to -0.6. Three vetoes were recorded against two interventions; none was dropped.

## What this report covers

The board's 54-row ranking of investigational options scoped to the stated targetable features, plus the workup that gates it. Standard care for this disease sits on its own table of equal standing, which this report does not summarize.

## Top-line findings

- The rank-1 recommendation is not a drug. It is a records retrieval. The validated HER2 IHC was already drawn in 2026-08 and only its tier is unretrieved; sequencing reports generated between 2023 and 2025 have never been read for FGFR3, for ERBB2 copy number, or for the single OncoKB-actionable alteration the 2023 report counts and never names. All five personas endorsed it at agreement 1.0, and four made continuation of trastuzumab deruxtecan contingent on the tier.
- Everything below rank 1 is investigational and held loosely. The best therapeutic agreement score in the case is 0.4, no therapeutic row drew more than three of five endorsements, and ranks 3, 4, 8 and 9 carry no clinical efficacy data at all. The strongest efficacy figure on the list, a 47% post-enfortumab response rate at rank 5, is an unpublished dose-escalation abstract with no confidence interval.
- Three vetoes stand. Conservative and advocate each vetoed the high-dose ipilimumab boost, so rank 10 carries only the lower-ipilimumab schedule with protocolized lipase surveillance and written stopping rules that both vetoing personas named. The critic's objection travels with it: cutting the ipilimumab dose deletes the only arm that ever beat PD-1 monotherapy (26.9% versus 25.6% in CheckMate 032). Risktaker vetoed randomization onto the sacituzumab tirumotecan phase 3, where two of three control drugs are taxanes against established neuropathy, and that row stays at rank 11 as not recommended.
- Two ranked options rest on weak-positive or single-read biomarkers and are less certain than the rows driven by a validated HER2 tier. The TROP2 signal is weak on a research assay, and weak expression predicts TROP2-directed benefit less reliably than strong expression does. The TP53 R175H call behind rank 4 rests on one plasma read at 0.09% VAF.
- This ranking is scoped to the stated targetable features. If contemporary validated HER2 IHC reads low or negative, ranks 2, 6 and 8 are foreclosed. If the non-HER2 gates also close (no retained Nectin-4, no tissue-confirmed R175H, no FGFR3 alteration, negative validated stains on the research-lead antigens), the case has no within-scope recommendation left, and care for the indication becomes a treating-team conversation that this report does not cover.
- Two gaps the dossier could not close: creatinine and eGFR are absent from the record, which matters most for the alpha-radioligand at rank 3, and the 2026-03 and 2026-06 chest CT reads disagree about interstitial lung disease, which prices every deruxtecan-payload option and blocks one trial outright.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** retrieve the 2026-08 validated HER2 IHC tier and the full sequencing reports (rank 1). Turnaround is days, it spends no new tissue, and no persona dissented.

1. **Zanidatamab** (payload-free HER2 bispecific): the way to stay on a confirmed HER2 axis if the current HER2 drug stops for tolerability. Two endorsements, thin urothelial evidence, and the registered cohort excludes prior HER2 therapy. *Conditional on `her2_ihc` positive, foreclosed if the test is negative.*
2. **[225Ac]Ac-AKY-1189** (Nectin-4 alpha radioligand): first-in-human with no efficacy data, but it permits prior enfortumab vedotin and screens by imaging rather than tissue.
3. **CLSP-1025** (TP53 R175H x HLA-A\*02:01 T-cell engager): both eligibility gates already on file, no clinical results anywhere in the class.
4. **LY4052031** (Nectin-4 ADC, camptothecin payload): the trial written for this post-enfortumab history, with conservative and concensusite dissenting that an unpublished 47% cannot carry that much weight.
5. **Pertuzumab + trastuzumab**: the one HER2 route touching none of the four toxicity vetoes, doubly conditional on the pending tier and the unread ERBB2 copy number. *Conditional on `her2_ihc` positive, foreclosed if the test is negative.*
6. **Vepugratinib** (FGFR3): wholly conditional on the rank-1 retrieval, against roughly a 15-20% prior that a qualifying alteration exists at all.

Those six are global ranks 2 through 7. Ranks 8 through 15 continue in the same register, and ranks 11 and 13 through 15 are recorded as not recommended so the rejected paths stay visible.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing across lines, and payer or access questions. It also does not cover standard care for this disease, which has its own co-equal table and belongs with the treating team.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state by persona and 1-3 anchor citations; the per-trial extraction lives on the live case page. Read the rank-1 workup before the ranks: nothing below it can be weighted correctly until the HER2 tier and the sequencing reports land.
