# Executive summary

**Case:** `ulms-highgrade-pulm-mets-er-pr-neg-qqvt`
**Question:** What can target the histology, the entirely unmeasured molecular landscape, and the bilateral pulmonary disease pattern in newly diagnosed metastatic high-grade uterine leiomyosarcoma, systemic-therapy naive, with doxorubicin + trabectedin planned and imminent?
**Evidence base:** 29 trials, 25 clinical and 37 preclinical evidence rows, 5 board positions, 20 critiques; agreement scores 1.0 to -0.4; zero vetoes.

## What this report covers

The board's ranked, feature-scoped options, synthesized by the PI. One fact frames all of it: the 6.0 cm primary was resected in September 2024 and signed out benign, the error was caught on re-review in July 2026, and after roughly 23 months she is only now starting systemic therapy.

## Top-line findings

- The planned regimen is the best-evidenced option in her disease (LMS-04: mPFS 12.2 vs 6.2 months, mOS 33 vs 24 months) and the board's one unanimous therapeutic call. It is also off-label at this line, because the trabectedin label requires a prior anthracycline and she is naive; the authorization should be filed as off-label from day one.
- The guideline picture is adoption lag plus a reimbursement decision, not a finding against the trial: BSG 2025 keeps single-agent doxorubicin at I,A and records the combination as not NICE-supported, ESMO's operative guideline predates the trial, and NCCN carriage sits behind a login wall, so no category is quoted. The consequence is payer-side.
- The maintenance phase is the least-evidenced part of the regimen and the part she may spend longest on; nothing randomises continuation against stopping.
- Rank 1 is a workup that runs alongside cycle 1, not before it. Three items genuinely gate: LVEF before the first anthracycline dose, a clinician-documented ECOG, and a RECIST baseline CT before cycle 1. Germline TP53 should precede genotoxic chemotherapy per ERN GENTURIS, and it is a consent conversation with consequences for her relatives, not a scheduling item.
- All 15 surveyed panel biomarkers are unmeasured; zero were hardened. One DNA+RNA panel, one IHC block and one blood draw open or close nearly every targeted option, in parallel with induction. The ranking is scoped to her targetable features: negative results exhaust the within-scope ranks, and standard care for the indication is a separate care-team conversation this report does not cover.
- The 2024 operative report is a staging prerequisite, not just surveillance: morcellation status is unknown, abdominopelvic dissemination ran 44% vs 12.9% in the cited series, and the answer gates any lung-directed consolidation. Local control is not survival: the same series pairs 90% local control with 46% survival at 24 months, and it screened out the bilateral infiltrative pattern she has.
- ER/PR <1% forecloses endocrine therapy, but the stain's specimen provenance is unverified; a failed stain on the 2024 block reads identically to a true negative.
- No preferences were ever stated. Every weighting is an assumed intake default, including the ECOG. Four questions remain unasked: the 80% grade 3-4 neutropenia and 28% febrile neutropenia trade, trial and travel appetite from a home at 8,500 ft, ovarian function and the hormonal aftermath of chemotherapy at 39, and her tolerance for an off-label authorization fight.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** the gating workup alongside cycle 1 (LVEF, documented ECOG, RECIST baseline; germline TP53 consent and draw; 2024 operative report; one-pass NGS, IHC and HLA package).

1. **Doxorubicin + trabectedin, then trabectedin maintenance (LMS-04)**: first line; the largest replicated effect in the dossier, bought with 80% grade 3-4 neutropenia and an off-label filing.
2. **Olaparib + temozolomide**: queued for first progression. Conditional on `hrd` positive; foreclosed for practical purposes if testing returns proficient.
3. **B7-H3 ADC (MGC026)**: stain-gated phase 1 referral; no LMS response data for the class, and its predecessor was halted on safety.
4. **FAP alpha radioligand ([225Ac]RTX-2358)**: FAPi-PET-gated screen at progression; marrow-stroma dosimetry is the open safety question.
5. **REC-617 after RB1-null IHC**: lowest agreement (-0.4); the gate runs backwards in a near-universally RB1-null histology.
6. **Abemaciclib + gemcitabine**: NOT recommended. The concurrent schedule contradicts the sequential-priming evidence that motivated the pairing, and it would spend the chemo-naive slot.

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, sequencing beyond the branches named above, and payer or access strategy. Standard care for metastatic uLMS lives on its own surface and with the treating team.

## How to use this report

Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state (endorsement and dissent by persona) and 1-3 anchor citations; the full per-trial extraction is on the live case page. Read the caveats before the ranks: in this case they carry most of the decision weight.
