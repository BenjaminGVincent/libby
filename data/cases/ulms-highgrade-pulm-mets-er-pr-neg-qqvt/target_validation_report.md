## Target validation paths

Two test sets carry the weight of this case. An echocardiogram with LVEF and a clinician-documented ECOG decide whether the planned doxorubicin + trabectedin induction (the LMS-04 regimen, NCT02997358) can be given at all, and a tumor HRR/HRD panel decides whether olaparib + temozolomide (NCI protocol 10250, NCT03880019) exists as an option at progression. Neither result is on file. If the cardiac or pulmonary screen fails, the first-line fitness conversation changes and belongs to the treating team, not to Libby; if the HRR panel returns proficient, the olaparib + temozolomide branch closes for practical purposes. Nearly everything else below runs in parallel with induction and costs no treatment days.

### uLMS histology

Three items are essential before cycle 1. A transthoracic echocardiogram with LVEF and strain (same day to 1 week), because the anthracycline cannot be dosed without a reference value. A clinician-documented ECOG, because the 1 on file is an intake assumption and her symptoms fit either 1 or 2, a difference that decides full-dose induction and most trial screens. And a hepatic-renal panel with creatine kinase, which sets the trabectedin starting dose and its dose-modification rules. The CBC with reticulocytes and ferritin needs an altitude-adjusted reference interval stated in the chart: at roughly 8,500 ft her baseline haemoglobin runs high, and a sea-level-normed count will under-read chemotherapy anaemia. Expert sarcoma pathology review of both specimens, with mitotic index, coagulative necrosis and FNCLCC grade, is the essential item that gates no drug: a 6.0 cm mass handled as a benign fibroid was very unlikely to be sampled at the one-block-per-centimetre standard, major revisions occur in about a quarter of sarcoma second opinions, and the deeper levels should be cut before the molecular orders exhaust the block.

### Bilateral pulmonary pattern

PFTs with DLCO and oximetry recorded at residence altitude come before induction. Without them, an on-treatment desaturation cannot be split into progression, pneumonitis or altitude physiology, and the same numbers later price any lung-directed surgery or radiation. PD-L1 with CD3 and CD8 on the 2026 lung core answers whether the infiltrative, possibly inflammatory radiologic read reflects an immune infiltrate; it gates nothing on its own, and if the core runs scant, the MMR and B7-H3 stains take priority.

### Unmeasured molecular landscape

One RNA-inclusive tumor NGS panel on the 2024 resection block does most of the work here. The HRR gene set with an LOH score gates olaparib + temozolomide via NCT03880019 (2-3 weeks, archival FFPE). RNA fusion calling must be specified on the requisition: NTRK breakpoints sit in large introns, a DNA-only panel returns a false negative that reads as a closed question, and a positive gates larotrectinib, entrectinib or repotrectinib under the tumor-agnostic approval. TMB from a validated comprehensive panel and the four-stain MMR IHC close the pembrolizumab doors (TMB >=10 mut/Mb, or dMMR); both are rare in this histology and cheap to settle. B7-H3 IHC carries the best odds of a positive of any surface target (97% of soft-tissue sarcomas stained positive, 69% at high level) and gates screening for the B7-H3 ADC programs. The germline panel (BRCA1/2, PALB2, RAD51C/D, ATM, TP53 and the Lynch genes) goes out with the somatic order, after the consent conversation it actually is; ERN GENTURIS puts germline TP53 testing before genotoxic chemotherapy. HLA class I typing at allele-level resolution is one blood draw, with PRAME and MAGE-A4 IHC held as reflex stains until a restricting allele returns; that prices the TCR-T and ImmTAC class (IMA203, brenetafusp, the SURPASS programme) without spending tissue speculatively. TP53, RB1, ATRX and MED12 ride along free on the panel, and paired ATRX IHC on both blocks answers whether the 2024 primary represents the 2026 disease for the price of two stains. One assay cannot be ordered at all: the RAD51 foci test, the functional readout that separated 11.2 from 5.4 months of PFS in the olaparib + temozolomide trial, has no CLIA provider anywhere. Do not hold the somatic panel for it; ask any PARP-inhibitor trial site whether they run it at screening.

### ER/PR <1%

The <1% ER and PR result genuinely forecloses aromatase inhibition, since durable benefit with letrozole occurred only in tumours staining above 90%, but only if the stain was run on current disease with working controls. So the first step is a records one: which specimen was stained, which clone, and whether the internal control worked. A failed stain on the 2024 block reads identically to a true negative. If the result came off the 2024 primary, repeat ER and PR on the 2026 lung core with percentage and intensity reported; if it was already run on the lung core, skip the restain and leave the door closed.

### The 2024-2026 interval

Two records requests, one letter to the 2024 institution. The September 2024 operative report and gross description settle whether the specimen was morcellated; morcellation of an unrecognised leiomyosarcoma disperses tumour through the peritoneum, so a yes means pelvic and peritoneal disease must be actively excluded rather than assumed absent. And the pre-treatment CT with named RECIST target lesions has to happen before cycle 1, because the clean baseline window closes the day induction starts. Any interval imaging on disc bounds the growth rate of the lung disease, the closest available measure of tempo in a therapy-naive patient.

### Biomarkers measured, but not to decision resolution

This case has almost nothing in the category, and that is itself the finding. The biomarker survey handed eight questions across (HRR/HRD, NTRK fusion, MSI/MMR, TMB, HLA class I, B7-H3, PRAME and MAGE-A4), and every one arrived never measured or not assessable rather than measured at insufficient resolution; each has its closing assay in the sections above. The one result on file whose resolution is in question is the ER/PR <1% itself: the assay is decision-grade, the specimen provenance is not, and the records step above is what closes that gap.
