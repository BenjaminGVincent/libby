# Executive summary

**Case:** `aml-mds-related-rr-tp53-aberrant-hla-pending-x7q2-rerun2`
**Question:** What HLA-restricted immunotherapy, CD33/CD123-directed and second-transplant options are open to a woman in her 50s with relapsed/refractory MDS-related AML at roughly 85% marrow blasts, six years after a matched-sibling allograft and refractory to FLAG-IDA plus venetoclax.
**Evidence base:** 47 trials, 36 clinical-evidence rows, 33 preclinical rows, 5 board positions, 20 cross-critiques (10 dissents, 8 qualified, 2 endorsements, zero vetoes); agreement scores 1.0 to -0.4.

## What this report covers

The Experimental table: one rank-1 workup, ten ranked investigational options, seventeen surfaced but unranked. Approved standard care sits on a separate table.

## Top-line findings

- The referral asked for HLA-restricted immunotherapy and found a reversal. Two of the three flagship HA-1/HA-2 products exclude a prior allogeneic transplant, which she has had; NCT03326921 is open to her because it requires one.
- Rank 1 is a workup, and it runs alongside cycle 1 rather than before it. Sibling HA-1 genotyping gates a fresh donor collection, a lead time nothing else carries, and peripheral blasts moved 58% to 82% in a fortnight.
- Roughly 85% blasts sets the sequencing. The favourable second-transplant strata need under 27-35%, one trial caps blasts at 30% and another at 10%.
- TP53 stays unresolved in both directions, mutant in 2019 and negative in 2026 on a 2%-LOD panel with no copy-number calling. Organ function is undocumented, and the preferences file asks that the 0.75 efficacy weight be revisited once fitness is known.
- A negative HLA-A*02:01 forecloses the HLA-restricted rows rather than demoting them. The ranking is scoped to her stated features, so a negative result exhausts it and standard care becomes the treating team's conversation.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** HLA class I typing, HA-1/HA-2 genotyping of patient and sibling, CD33/CD123 density, TP53 NGS, organ function. Send it during induction.

1. **CLAG-GO** (NCT04050280): the cytoreduction everything waits on; randomised gemtuzumab data null in her karyotype.
2. **Second allo-HCT**: the only documented cure fraction, gated on blasts under 27%.
3. **HA-1 TCR-T** (NCT03326921): 4/9 CR on a feasibility endpoint. Conditional on `hla_a0201` positive, foreclosed if negative.
4. **Sibling DLI** after cytoreduction: off-guideline at this burden.
5. **CBX-250**: the second A*02:01 lever, no human data. Conditional on `hla_a0201` positive, foreclosed if negative.
6. **BMS-986497**: unusually open criteria, no clinical data.
7. **Pivekimab sunirine**: ORR 21% in her disease, held under a hepatic condition.
8. **Tagraxofusp**: the 72% figure is BPDCN, not AML.
9. **211At-BC8-B10**: admits her prior graft, wants a haploidentical relative.
10. **Rezatapopt: not recommended.** Its mechanism needed venetoclax, which she progressed through, and the Y220C entry assay sits at the detection limit of the panel that called her TP53-negative.
