# Executive summary

**Case:** `pdac-recurrent-cldn18-2-kras-g12r-tp53-y220c-p25m`
**Question:** In pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma that recurred roughly four months after adjuvant FOLFIRINOX, what can target CLDN18.2 (2+/3+ in 60% of tumor cells), KRAS G12R, TP53 Y220C, and KMT2C S2816fs, given that the recurrent mass has never been biopsied?
**Evidence base:** 54 trial rows, 32 clinical-evidence rows, 46 preclinical rows. Eight ranked entries (a shared workup, five therapeutics, two explicit not-recommendeds) plus 32 surfaced without a rank. Agreement scores run 1.0 down to -0.6, with no vetoes; daraxonrasib was rank 1 on all five persona lists.

## What this report covers

The feature-targeting investigational options: pan-RAS(ON) and pan-KRAS inhibition against G12R, the CLDN18.2 ADC, engager, cell-therapy, and radioligand classes, p53 Y220C reactivators, mutant-KRAS vaccines, and germline-gated PARP maintenance. Standard second-line chemotherapy targets none of the stated features and lives on the case's separate Standard-of-care page.

## Top-line findings

- Every molecular result in this case comes from the December 2025 resection specimen, with ten cycles of FOLFIRINOX between that tissue and the July 2026 mass now being treated, itself only a radiographic diagnosis. That is why a biopsy, not a drug, holds rank 1. It is the through-line of the whole case.
- One scheduling constraint can close the best door on timing alone: RASolute 303 requires consent within 6 weeks of the metastatic diagnosis, so site outreach has to be teed up before the pathology result lands. Starting chemotherapy first silently closes the same window.
- Daraxonrasib is the best-evidenced option and is reachable through an FDA-authorized expanded-access protocol (NCT07573215, drug free of charge). Confidence is moderate, not high: G12R sat inside RASolute 302's pooled RAS G12 estimand and was never a study population, and that trial enrolled previously treated metastatic patients, where this patient's only systemic exposure is perioperative and the recurrence is unstaged. Do not read 13.2 versus 6.6 months as this patient's numbers.
- CLDN18.2 at 60% (2+/3+) is a threshold call, not a yes/no: below the >=75% zolbetuximab cutoff, above the cutoffs for IBI343, ASP2138, and the radioligand class.
- Two options are recommended against. Off-label zolbetuximab: the only randomized CLDN18.2 readout in PDAC was flat (13.7 vs 13.6 months) and the tumor misses the companion-diagnostic cutoff. Single-agent PD-1 blockade: MSS with TMB 0.83 mut/Mb closes both tumor-agnostic routes.
- Germline testing has never been done, although NCCN has asked for it in every pancreatic adenocarcinoma patient since 2019. It is a blood draw, and it gates an entire drug class.
- The best mechanistic match in the dossier is unreachable. CLDN18.2 radioligand therapy clears its expression threshold threefold, but all three trials worldwide are single-center Chinese investigator-initiated studies with no route for foreign enrollees, and no Western program exists at any stage.
- Guideline claims are verified as of NCCN Pancreatic v2.2026 (22 April 2026); v3.2026 has posted and could not be read behind the paywall, and v2.2026 went to press before the RASolute 302 publication.
- The ranking is scoped to the stated targetable features. If the biopsy does not confirm recurrent PDAC carrying them, the within-scope ranks are exhausted, and standard care for the indication is a separate care-team conversation this report does not cover.
- Organ function is undocumented, the residual oxaliplatin neuropathy is ungraded, and the 0.7 efficacy/toxicity weight is an assumed default, not the patient's number.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** EUS-guided core biopsy of the recurrent mass, with restaging, paired CLDN18.2 IHC (recurrence and December 2025 block, one laboratory), and NGS/ctDNA re-profiling. One procedure unlocks four of the eight other essential workup items and produces the staging call the top drug's access turns on.

1. **Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236, pan-RAS(ON)).** Recommended. The only randomized survival evidence in PDAC containing this allele (RASolute 302: OS 13.2 vs 6.6 months, HR 0.40), read across a pooled-allele and line-of-therapy gap. Conditional on the biopsy confirming recurrent PDAC with these features; foreclosed if it does not.
2. **IBI343 (CLDN18.2 ADC, exatecan).** Recommended. The only prospective CLDN18.2 efficacy readout in this disease (confirmed ORR 22.7%); its 9.1-month median OS has not yet cleared the chemotherapy floor it would displace. Same biopsy condition.
3. **ASP2138 (CLDN18.2 x CD3 engager).** Considered with caveats. The widest-open CLDN18.2 trial door, with no PDAC efficacy readout; a T-cell-cold tumor is the mechanistic risk. Same biopsy condition.
4. **Olaparib maintenance.** Considered with caveats. Inert until the germline panel returns; ranked chiefly to force that blood draw. Pre-test probability roughly 5-7%.
5. **ASP5834 (IV pan-KRAS).** Considered with caveats. Nothing published; ranked because it is the one KRAS-directed route that survives a locally-recurrent staging call. Same biopsy condition.
6. **Zolbetuximab, off-label in PDAC.** Not recommended. A flat randomized readout plus a cutoff the tumor does not meet.
7. **Single-agent PD-1 blockade.** Not recommended. MSS, TMB 0.83 mut/Mb; carried so the negative result is visibly answered.
