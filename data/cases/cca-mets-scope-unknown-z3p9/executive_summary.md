# Executive summary

**Case:** `cca-mets-scope-unknown-z3p9`
**Question:** In metastatic cholangiocarcinoma supplied with no molecular profiling, which targeted options does the actionable biliary panel open, gated on comprehensive genomic profiling?
**Evidence base:** 30 trials across eight candidate axes, 26 clinical-evidence rows and 12 preclinical rows; board agreement runs from 1.0 (the profiling gate and ivosidenib) down to -0.6 (trastuzumab deruxtecan, held below zanidatamab). No formal vetoes.

## What this report covers

The board enumerated the actionable cholangiocarcinoma panel as candidate features, every one untested, and ranked the targeted drugs each feature would unlock. This report packages that ranking and the biomarker workup that has to come first.

## Top-line findings

- Nothing here is actionable until the tumor is genotyped. Comprehensive genomic profiling is the rank-1 action, and a single paired DNA plus RNA panel, run with HER2 IHC/ISH and MMR IHC, resolves seven of the eight candidate axes in one pass. Roughly 40% of metastatic cholangiocarcinoma carries an alteration the panel would surface.
- On a confirmed IDH1 R132, ivosidenib leads the therapeutics. It carries the only randomized phase-3 evidence in the set (ClarIDHy, PFS HR 0.37) and is the only NCCN category-1 anchor, but ORR is 2% and the intention-to-treat OS did not separate (HR 0.69, p=0.060), so the honest goal is disease control, not shrinkage.
- No drug was vetoed. The sharpest disagreement holds trastuzumab deruxtecan below zanidatamab in the HER2 lane over 10.5% adjudicated ILD with three treatment-related deaths and no pulmonary baseline on file; three personas dissented, so T-DXd sits at considered_with_caveats. Lirafugratinib carries a parallel caveat flag over abstract-only, pre-approval data.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. A negative panel exhausts the within-scope recommendations, and standard care for metastatic cholangiocarcinoma is then a separate conversation with the treating team that this report does not cover. Each feature forecloses independently.
- Tumor subsite was never specified, which shifts the pre-test probability of every axis.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Comprehensive genomic profiling, paired DNA plus RNA NGS with HER2 IHC/ISH and MMR IHC. Non-toxic; gates everything below.

1. **Ivosidenib**: only RCT-grade and only category-1 evidence, but the single-agent payoff is disease control, not response. *Conditional on `idh1_r132` positive; foreclosed if the hotspot is wild-type.*
2. **Futibatinib**: highest-yield axis, 42% ORR with cross-trial concordance and a resistance-coverage edge; 30% grade-3 hyperphosphatemia is the tradeoff. *Conditional on `fgfr2_fusion` positive; foreclosed if no fusion.*
3. **Pemigatinib**: same FGFR2 gate, gentler grade-3 profile, longer track record. *Conditional on `fgfr2_fusion` positive; foreclosed if no fusion.*
4. **Lirafugratinib (RLY-4008)**: highest ORR in the FGFR lane but abstract-only, pre-approval, one-in-three grade-3 hand-foot; three personas dissented (considered with caveats). Its own basket trial excludes cholangiocarcinoma, so access runs through the separate ReFocus cohort. *Conditional on `fgfr2_fusion` positive.*
5. **Zanidatamab**: guideline HER2 lead on dedicated biliary data, seated above T-DXd. *Conditional on `erbb2_amp` positive; foreclosed if HER2 IHC/ISH is negative.*
6. **Dabrafenib + trametinib**: 47% ORR on a true V600E; heavier serious-AE load, 1-5% prevalence. *Conditional on `braf_v600e` positive; foreclosed without a confirmed V600E.*
7. **Trastuzumab deruxtecan**: competitive IHC 3+ activity held below zanidatamab on the ILD signal; a HER2-low read is a weaker predictor and would be investigational (considered with caveats). *Conditional on `erbb2_amp` positive.*
8. **Pembrolizumab**: durable tail if MSI-H/dMMR or TMB-high confirms; biliary magnitude inferred from a cross-tumor basket at 1-3% prevalence. *Conditional on `msi_tmb` positive.*

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, line sequencing, payer and access logistics, and any standard care that does not target a panel feature all sit outside this ranking. This is decision support, not a treatment plan.

## How to use this report

Order the profiling first; the rest of the ranking only matters once a feature reads positive. Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state and one to three anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction lives on the live case page; this is the synthesis.
