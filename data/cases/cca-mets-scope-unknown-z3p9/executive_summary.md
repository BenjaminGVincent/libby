# Executive summary

**Case:** `cca-mets-scope-unknown-z3p9`
**Question:** In metastatic cholangiocarcinoma supplied with no molecular profiling, which targeted options does the actionable biliary panel open, gated on comprehensive genomic profiling?
**Evidence base:** 15 trials across eight candidate axes, 12 clinical-evidence rows and 7 preclinical rows; board agreement runs from 1.0 (the profiling gate and ivosidenib) down to -0.4 (the vetoed T-DXd row).

## What this report covers

The board enumerated the actionable cholangiocarcinoma panel as candidate features, every one untested, and ranked the targeted drugs each feature would unlock. This report packages that ranking plus the biomarker workup that has to come first.

## Top-line findings

- Nothing here is actionable until the tumor is genotyped. Comprehensive genomic profiling is the rank-1 action, and a single paired DNA plus RNA panel, with HER2 IHC/ISH and MMR IHC, resolves seven of the eight candidate axes in one pass. Roughly 40% of metastatic cholangiocarcinoma carries an alteration the panel would surface.
- On a confirmed IDH1 R132, ivosidenib is the lead therapeutic. It carries the only randomized phase-3 evidence in the set (ClarIDHy, PFS HR 0.37), but ORR is 2%, so the honest goal is delayed progression rather than shrinkage.
- The conservative vetoed trastuzumab deruxtecan over 10.5% adjudicated ILD with three treatment-related deaths and no pulmonary baseline to gate against. It sits at not_recommended, held behind documented HER2 IHC 3+ plus a pulmonary assessment and a written ILD-monitoring plan.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. A negative panel exhausts the within-scope recommendations, and standard care for metastatic cholangiocarcinoma is then a separate conversation with the treating team that this report does not cover. Each feature forecloses independently.
- Tumor subsite was never specified, which shifts the pre-test probability of every axis.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Comprehensive genomic profiling, paired DNA plus RNA NGS with HER2 IHC/ISH and MMR IHC. Non-toxic; gates everything below.

1. **Ivosidenib**: only RCT-grade evidence, but the single-agent payoff is disease control, not shrinkage. *Conditional on `idh1_r132` positive; foreclosed if the hotspot is wild-type.*
2. **Futibatinib**: highest-yield axis, 42% ORR with cross-trial concordance; 30% grade-3 hyperphosphatemia is the tradeoff. *Conditional on `fgfr2_fusion` positive; foreclosed if no fusion.*
3. **Pemigatinib**: same FGFR2 gate, gentler grade-3 profile, longer track record. *Conditional on `fgfr2_fusion` positive; foreclosed if no fusion.*
4. **Zanidatamab**: guideline HER2 lead on dedicated biliary data. *Conditional on `erbb2_amp` positive; foreclosed if HER2 IHC/ISH is negative.*
5. **Dabrafenib + trametinib**: 47% ORR on a true V600E; heavier serious-AE load. *Conditional on `braf_v600e` positive; foreclosed without a confirmed V600E.*
6. **Larotrectinib**: largest response signal if the rare fusion confirms, but cross-tumor pooled at sub-1% prevalence (considered with caveats). *Conditional on `ntrk_fusion` positive.*
7. **Pembrolizumab**: durable tail if MSI-H/dMMR or TMB-high confirms; biliary magnitude inferred from a cross-tumor basket. *Conditional on `msi_tmb` positive.*

## What this report does *not* cover

Dose adjustments, monitoring schedules, line sequencing, payer and access logistics, and any standard care that does not target a panel feature all sit outside this ranking. This is decision support, not a treatment plan.

## How to use this report

Order the profiling first; the rest of the ranking only matters once a feature reads positive. Each ranked option carries the board's agreement state and one to three anchor citations. The full per-trial extraction lives on the live case page; this is the synthesis.
