# Executive summary

**Case:** `gbm-mtap-cdkn2a-idhwt-subtotal-c4bq`
**Question:** In newly diagnosed IDH-wildtype glioblastoma (WHO grade 4, post-subtotal resection with intratumoral hemorrhage at presentation), what interventions act on MTAP loss and CDKN2A homozygous loss, gated on MGMT methylation, central MTAP IHC, EGFR / EGFRvIII status, and the comprehensive NGS panel that is currently pending?
**Evidence base:** 48 trials, 44 clinical-evidence rows, 42 preclinical rows, 11 target-validation rows; agreement scores span +1.0 (rank 1 workup, rank 2 Stupp + TTFields) to -1.0 (rank 13 front-line CAR-T, sustained conservative veto). All five personas converged without dissent on ranks 1 and 2.

## What this report covers

The PI synthesized 13 ranked rows: a rank-1 diagnostic gate, an OS-anchored backbone at rank 2, an MTAP-axis trial at rank 3, an MGMT-methylated conditional at rank 4, and recurrence pre-positions and biomarker-conditional plays through rank 12. Ranks 8 and 9 are MTAP-axis options added by a post-board re-screen and flagged not yet board-voted. Front-line CAR-T is surfaced at rank 13 as not recommended.

## Top-line findings

- Six parallel assays on one block of archival FFPE plus a peripheral blood draw resolve every load-bearing decision here: MGMT pyrosequencing, central MTAP IHC by the SP347-class clone, EGFR FISH + EGFRvIII RT-PCR, comprehensive NGS with RNA fusion, RB1 IHC, p16 IHC. No procedural toxicity; turnaround 5 to 28 days.
- Rank 2 is Stupp + TTFields. EORTC 26981 HR_OS 0.63 replicated at 5 years, EF-14 HR_OS 0.63 stacked on adjuvant TMZ, 20.9-month median. The regimen does not flip on the MGMT result; the candor of the TMZ-benefit conversation does.
- BGB-58067 (rank 3, NCT07485049 at Ivy / Phoenix) is the board-voted mechanism-matched MTAP-axis trial. Three of five personas dissented on rank order: no published phase 1 safety on the molecule, and the class has lost twice in CNS (TNG908 terminated November 2024, anvumetostat discontinued April 30, 2026). Biomarker fit is exact; the trial preserves the Stupp backbone.
- Two post-board MTAP-axis additions sit alongside BGB-58067, not above it, and carry no board vote (endorsed only by advocate and risktaker). Navlimetostat / BMS-986504 (rank 8) was materially upgraded: a recruiting recurrent-GBM CNS-penetration trial (NCT06883747) now shows human target engagement (77% SDMA reduction) and supra-threshold unbound tumor drug clearing the CNS bar that killed prior PRMT5 agents, plus cross-tumor NSCLC ORR 29%. TNG456 + abemaciclib (rank 9, new) is the only dual-axis row, hitting MTAP and CDKN2A at once on preclinical-plus-trial-in-progress evidence. Both are recurrence-gated with no GBM efficacy readout.
- The intratumoral-hemorrhage history forecloses front-line bevacizumab (4 of 5 personas) and sustains the conservative veto on front-line CAR-T at rank 13. The veto lifts only when E-SYNC Cohort 1 publishes a safety analysis including pre-bled patients with documented re-bleed surveillance.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. If MGMT returns unmethylated, rank 4 (CeTeG) drops off. If MTAP IHC returns intact despite the NGS loss call (the MTAPESTRY-101 four-of-six false-positive scenario), rank 3 drops off and rank 5 (GBM AGILE) inherits the trial slot. Front-line ICI in unselected GBM is closed by CheckMate-498 (HR_OS 1.31 worse than Stupp, p=0.0037); it re-opens only on a biomarker gate at recurrence.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Six parallel workup assays on archival FFPE plus a peripheral blood draw.

1. **Stupp + TTFields.** OS-anchored backbone; every other rank layers on or is measured against it.
2. **BGB-58067 on NCT07485049.** Board-voted, mechanism-matched at MTAP / CDKN2A; no molecule-specific efficacy yet.
3. **CeTeG / NOA-09.** *Conditional on MGMT methylated; foreclosed if unmethylated.* 16.7-mo OS gain, n=141, CI crossing 1.0.
4. **GBM AGILE (NCT03970447).** Multi-site randomized alternative when Phoenix is infeasible; no arm matches MTAP.
5. **SurVaxM on SURVIVE (NCT05163080).** Lowest-toxicity add-on; binary choice against TTFields (excludes Optune).
6. **Tumor-agnostic conditional plays.** Dabrafenib + trametinib, larotrectinib, pembrolizumab. Low prior; zero marginal cost.
7. **Navlimetostat / BMS-986504.** *Post-board, not board-voted.* Human GBM proof-of-mechanism (NCT06883747) + NSCLC activity; recurrence-gated, no GBM efficacy.
8. **TNG456 +/- abemaciclib (NCT06810544).** *Post-board, not board-voted.* Only dual-axis row; preclinical plus trial-in-progress, recurrence-gated.
9. **Abemaciclib (CDK4/6i).** Recurrence pre-position; gated on RB1 intact. Critic and concensusite did not pick it.
10. **CAN-3110 oncolytic HSV-1.** Recurrence pre-position; order HSV-1 IgG serology now.
11. **FUS-BBB opening (SONOBIRD NCT04528680).** Recurrence pre-position; requires re-resection candidacy.
