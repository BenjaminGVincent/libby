# Executive summary

**Case:** `gbm-mtap-cdkn2a-idhwt-subtotal-c4bq`
**Question:** In newly diagnosed IDH-wildtype glioblastoma (WHO grade 4, post-subtotal resection with intratumoral hemorrhage at presentation), what interventions act on MTAP loss and CDKN2A homozygous loss, gated on MGMT methylation, central MTAP IHC, EGFR / EGFRvIII status, and the comprehensive NGS panel that is currently pending?
**Evidence base:** 42 trials, 37 clinical-evidence rows, 42 preclinical rows, 11 target-validation rows; agreement scores span +1.0 (rank 1 workup, rank 2 Stupp + TTFields) to -1.0 (rank 12 front-line CAR-T, sustained conservative veto). All five personas converged without dissent on ranks 1 and 2.

## What this report covers

The PI synthesized 12 ranked rows: a rank-1 diagnostic gate, an OS-anchored backbone at rank 2, an MTAP-axis trial at rank 3, an MGMT-methylated conditional at rank 4, and recurrence pre-positions and biomarker-conditional plays through rank 11. Front-line CAR-T is surfaced at rank 12 as not recommended. The clinician page carries the rank-by-rank narrative; this PDF is the synthesis.

## Top-line findings

- Six parallel assays on one block of archival FFPE plus a peripheral blood draw resolve every load-bearing decision in this case. MGMT pyrosequencing, central MTAP IHC by the SP347-class clone, EGFR FISH + EGFRvIII RT-PCR, comprehensive NGS with RNA fusion, RB1 IHC, and p16 IHC. No procedural toxicity. Turnaround 5 to 28 days.
- Rank 2 is Stupp + TTFields. EORTC 26981 HR_OS 0.63 replicated at 5 years, EF-14 HR_OS 0.63 stacked on adjuvant TMZ, 20.9-month median is the threshold every other rank is measured against. The regimen does not flip on MGMT result; the candor of the conversation about TMZ benefit does.
- BGB-58067 (rank 3, NCT07485049 at Ivy / Phoenix) is the mechanism-matched MTAP-axis trial. Three of five personas dissented on rank order: no published peer-reviewed phase 1 safety on the molecule, and the MTA-cooperative PRMT5 class has lost twice in CNS (TNG908 terminated November 2024, anvumetostat program discontinued April 30, 2026). Biomarker fit is exact and the trial preserves the Stupp backbone in its arm design.
- The intratumoral-hemorrhage history forecloses front-line bevacizumab (4 of 5 personas) and sustains the conservative veto on front-line CAR-T at rank 12. The veto lifts only when E-SYNC Cohort 1 publishes a safety analysis that includes pre-bled patients with documented re-bleed surveillance.
- The ranking is targetable-feature-scoped. If MGMT returns unmethylated, rank 4 (CeTeG) drops off entirely. If MTAP IHC returns intact despite the NGS-level loss call (the MTAPESTRY-101 four-of-six false-positive scenario), rank 3 drops off and rank 5 (GBM AGILE) inherits the trial slot. Front-line ICI in unselected GBM is closed by CheckMate-498 (HR_OS 1.31 worse than Stupp, p=0.0037); it re-opens only on a biomarker gate at recurrence.

## Recommendation summary

**Shared first step:** Six parallel workup assays on archival FFPE plus a peripheral blood draw.

1. **Stupp + TTFields.** RT 60 Gy / 30 fx + concurrent and adjuvant TMZ + Optune Gio. OS-anchored backbone; every other rank layers on or is measured against it.
2. **BGB-58067 on NCT07485049 (Sanai phase 0/2).** Mechanism-matched at MTAP / CDKN2A with brain-penetrant chemistry; no published molecule-specific efficacy yet.
3. **CeTeG / NOA-09 (lomustine + TMZ + RT).** *Conditional on MGMT methylated; foreclosed if unmethylated.* 16.7-month median OS gain over Stupp in the methylated stratum, n=141, CI crossing 1.0.
4. **GBM AGILE adaptive platform (NCT03970447).** Multi-site randomized alternative when Phoenix is infeasible; no current arm matches MTAP.
5. **SurVaxM on SURVIVE (NCT05163080).** Lowest-toxicity add-on; forces a binary choice against TTFields because the protocol excludes concurrent Optune.
6. **Tumor-agnostic conditional plays.** Dabrafenib + trametinib (BRAF V600E), larotrectinib (NTRK), pembrolizumab (MSI-H or TMB-H ≥10). Low prior probability; zero marginal cost on top of the rank-1 NGS.
7. **Navlimetostat (BMS-986504) on MountainTAP-5 (NCT07492680).** Parallel-track MTAP-axis insurance against BGB-58067 single-site fragility; calendar-trigger July 17, 2026.
8. **Abemaciclib (CDK4/6i, CNS-penetrant).** Recurrence pre-position; gated on RB1 intact. Critic and concensusite did not pick it.
9. **CAN-3110 oncolytic HSV-1.** Recurrence pre-position; order HSV-1 IgG serology now (seropositive subset doubles the prior probability of benefit).
10. **FUS-mediated BBB opening (SonoCloud-9 / SONOBIRD NCT04528680).** Recurrence pre-position; requires re-resection candidacy.
