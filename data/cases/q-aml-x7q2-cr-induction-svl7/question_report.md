No, with moderate confidence: no therapy deliverable to her now has the best evidence for inducing a complete remission, for two reasons at once. On demonstrated true CR in every population that contains her, the confidence intervals all overlap, so nothing separates from anything else. And ELN 2022 writes absence of extramedullary disease into the CR definition itself, so every marrow-only figure in this dossier fails the question's own endpoint for a woman with a documented thigh myeloid sarcoma. That is also why this page carries no ranked table: the question was framed to allow one, and the evidence would not carry it.

Read the verdict precisely. It is not "we don't know". The matched populations are studied, in a 594-patient benchmark, a randomised control arm and a matched prospective cohort, and the evidence reaches the question and answers it negatively. Nor is it "nothing can be done". Every deliverable candidate keeps a real, undifferentiated chance of remission, and the board converged in practice: three of five members put CLAG-GO on NCT04050280 first, on eligibility, because it is the one open protocol written to admit her, not on a CR rate it has never published.

Two findings should travel with the choice. No guideline requires a complete remission before a second transplant, so going straight to sequential conditioning is a live alternative to buying a remission first; here the board questioned the goal, not just the options. And once demonstrated benefit stops discriminating, cost does: the one randomised trial to compare induction against none recorded 61% versus 21% grade 3 or worse non-haematological toxicity and a median 27 extra hospital days for a 3.4% day-56 CR difference, though it excluded prior allograft and did not show non-inferiority (p=0.036). Nobody has yet asked her the two questions that decide this: whether a 13 to 17% chance of the intended result is worth 7 to 21% sixty-day mortality, and whether she would accept conditioning without a CR first. One action stays open: the expanded-access call for lintuzumab-Ac225, the best-matched signal in the file (trial completed, follow-on suspended), has never been made, and one ranking changes on a yes. Whatever is chosen, thigh imaging must be built into response assessment or no CR claim about her can be checked.

This run assessed one endpoint, CR induction. Durability, survival and everything after remission belong to the source case, whose [ranked dossier](../aml-mds-related-rr-tp53-aberrant-hla-pending-x7q2-rerun2/recommendations.md) was not revisited and stands as published.
